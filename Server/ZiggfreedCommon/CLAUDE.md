# Server/ZiggfreedCommon/

- The reputations are native `ReputationGroup`s (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/`) with zc companions under `Reputations/`: Hallow's Eve's Old Jack's Favor (`Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`) and the Hallowed (`Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`, never renamed: favor is filed under it; the set, the pumpkin, the capstone and its title share the name, so a line naming the reputation says it is the reputation), Harvest Feast's favor with Martha (`Harvest_Feast_Host`), and the cross-season Festival Favor (`Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival`). Favor is paid on each quest's and contract's own rewards (a `Reputation` reward), never inside the shared pay lootables, so the quest card shows it.
- Every placement in `NpcPlacements/` names its season's `Season`, with `Lifecycle.KeepAlive`, and the library sweeps placements at every calendar start and end and at each rise and lapse of a Moon or Live event. A character on two seasons is two placements on one spot, each with its own conversation (the Gravekeeper's `Hallows_Eve` and `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Restless_Moon` pair), so neither conversation gates on a `_Live` switch (`HallowsEveRetrofitShapeTest` holds Hallow's Eve's files to that). A quest giver's role (Old Jack, Martha and her three helpers) is a `Template_Zig_QuestGiver` Variant naming only that template's five parameters.
- Everything season-only vanishes when the season or its kill switch is off: the `Season` leaf (its calendar event's id, `Hallows_Eve` or `Harvest_Feast`; the Harvest Moon bundle names `Harvest_Moon`) hides a file (a placement, the wallet, the storefront, the board, an offer, a contract, a quest, the trick-or-treat extension). A `Parent` base carries it to every child whatever `Requires` the child writes; never gate a season through a base's `Requires`, which a child's own replaces whole. `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_live` is for locks and rolls only. Achievements carry no gate: their `Occurrence` keeps each year's copy live only while that year runs.
- A season's keepsake is quest pay, never an achievement Claim: its last quest's own Claim rows pay it at the hand-in, its name stamped with `@run` so the event labels the run (a yearly one reads its year), and the keepsake achievement keeps its points, feat and ladder tag and pays nothing (R431, R432).
- A `Parent` skeleton for files under a `_<Season>/` folder sits outside it, named so its filename already spells its id (`Achievements/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Base.json`, `Achievements/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Achievement_Base.json`): the engine resolves `Parent` by filename and mmo-skills' content tests (`SeasonsOfOrbisPack`) by folded id, and only such a name satisfies both.
- Each season's quests, offers and contracts stand on Abstract bases outside its `_<Season>` folder (`Quests/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Quest_Base.json`, `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Quest_Base.json`, and the Offer and Contract bases in `ShopEntries/` and `Bounties/`) that carry shape and `Season`, never `Requires` or `Objectives`; an MMO skill-job base adds only the mod gate (`Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Skill_Contract_Base.json`). The four Lantern Bomb offers come from `ShopEntryGenerators/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Offers.json`, whose `IdPattern` spells the ids players' purchase counts are filed under. Old Jack's three `Jack_Daily_*` quests stay hand-written: their steps differ in which leaves they write, and an empty `Qualifier` changes what counts.
- mmo-skills' `HallowsEveRetrofitPinTest` holds every frozen id to its frozen meaning and never touches the frozen tree; a pin there that blocks a deliberate change is deleted, never taught it (R219, R389), so new ids and new MMO rows join with no allowance.
