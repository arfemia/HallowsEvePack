# Server/Item/

- A throw or crack chain is one Serial in the game's own order: the projectile (or the spend) first and the credit beside it, with the bomb spent last, or the payout inside the spend's Next. A Parallel forks the spend, and on the last item the engine's item-change check ends the throw still waiting on the client.
- Every `Projectile` step sets `Lifetime` (seconds; the game despawns the projectile then), above its projectile's fuse: a projectile's chains run on its thrower's interaction manager and a blast's Selector waits on the thrower's client, so the chain's own `RemoveEntity` can stall or die with the thrower, and without `Lifetime` the engine keeps the projectile 5 minutes.
- Every Lantern Bomb blast hits through one HitEntityRules rule with `"Matchers": []` (the key is required; empty matches every entity) and no plain HitEntity, so other players are hit too: the engine's PvP check spares them where the world's IsPvpEnabled is off, and the Forgotten Temple blocks all player damage. `IgnoreOwner: true` is what spares the thrower: it skips the chain's owner, which for a projectile is the player who threw it (the acting projectile is always skipped). The Shattering bomb's shard burst (`Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Shard_Burst`) uses the same rule (R361). A boss's bombs are the opposite (the pack's root router).
- A pointed item that must reach a character (the Christmas Present's give, `Christmas_Present_Give`) hits through HitEntityRules, never a plain HitEntity, which skips an invulnerable target. The last rule whose Matchers all hold wins, and the game has two matchers, `Player` and `Vulnerable`: a player is a friend, not a player and not Vulnerable is a character (every giver role built on CommonLib's Template_Zig_QuestGiver is Invulnerable; a role that is not lands in the creature rule), and not a player but Vulnerable is a creature, given its own do-nothing rule so a stray click never spends the item. A gift to a player gates its spend on `ZigGrantReward` `PayTarget`, which fails a chain that hit no player, so the payout comes before the spend there. A character's hand-in is `ZigCreditProgress` `QualifierFromTarget` with `Targets` (CommonLib 2.5.3), the NpcIds who take the item: it credits and finishes only for a character in that list and fails for any other, so the spend sits in its `Next` and another character never eats the item.
