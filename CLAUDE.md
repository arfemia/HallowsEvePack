# Seasons of Orbis

The yearly-events pack (plugin id `Ziggfreed:SeasonsOfOrbis`; repository and folder keep the `HallowsEvePack` / `hallows-eve-pack` name), whose first event is Hallow's Eve. The family-wide rules apply here; this file adds only what is specific to this pack.

- No MMO: the manifest hard-depends on ZiggfreedCommon and RPG Stations (the Carving Bench is an RPG Stations station). Author only zc and RPG Stations vocabulary and installed vanilla ids here; never an MMO id, kind or factor.
- The MMO layer ships in the MMO jar under a `Hallows_Eve` subtree, as additive contributions (`ContributesTo`, extensions); a same-id override only where a contribution cannot express it.
- Ids are prefixed `Hallows_Eve_`; quests and achievements sit under `_Hallows_Eve` folders.
- The event's creatures exist only through `ZiggfreedCommon/CalendarSpawns`: one native world-spawn rule per creature and environment (the game refuses a second), and Harvest Moon rewrites the same `Rule` ids at a higher `Priority`, never a rule of its own. Their roles are Variants of the vanilla templates carrying the vanilla creature's own `Modify`, never Variants of the creature itself: a Variant's `Modify` reaches only its immediate Reference's `Parameters`, and any other key refuses the whole role while the boot reads PASS, so grep the boot for `does not exist or is private`.
- The two reputations, Old Jack's Favor and the Hallowed, are native `ReputationGroup`s (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/`) with zc companions (`ZiggfreedCommon/Reputations/HallowsEvePack/`), and favor is paid on each quest's and contract's own rewards (a `Reputation` reward), never inside the shared pay lootables, so the quest card shows it.
- Old Jack's placement gates on `Hallows_Eve_Live` at the top of `Requires`, with `Lifecycle.KeepAlive`, and the library sweeps placements at every calendar start and end; his role names only the quest-giver template's five parameters.
- The pack carries its own nine-locale lang files.
- A pack file naming another mod's interaction Type stops a server without that mod at boot (the zip fails asset validation), which is why the pack requires RPG Stations rather than treating it as optional. The items carry no calendar gate; only the bench's Carve_Hallowed action locks on calendar_live.
- Everything event-only vanishes when the event or its kill switch is off: a top-level `Requires` factor `{"Factor": "ziggfreedcommon:feature", "Param": "Hallows_Eve_Live", "Min": 1}` hides a file (a placement, the wallet, the storefront, the board, an offer, a contract, a quest; a conversation line takes the same pair as a `Factor` condition). `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_live` is for locks and rolls only. Achievements carry no gate: their `Occurrence` keeps each year's copy live only while that year runs. The items stay plain native assets.
- A `Parent` skeleton for files under `_Hallows_Eve/` sits outside that folder, named so its filename already spells its id (`Achievements/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Base.json`): the engine resolves `Parent` by filename and the root content test by folded id, and only such a name satisfies both.
- Quests, contracts and offers at Old Jack stand on Abstract bases (`Quests/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Quest_Base.json`, `Bounties/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Contract_Base.json`, `ShopEntries/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Offer_Base.json`) that carry shared shape only, never `Requires` or `Objectives`: each quest and contract keeps its own `Hallows_Eve_Live` gate, and an offer hides with the stall. The four Lantern Bomb offers come from `ShopEntryGenerators/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Offers.json`, whose `IdPattern` spells the ids players' purchase counts are filed under. The three dailies stay hand-written: their steps differ in which leaves they write, and a generator row's empty `Qualifier` would change what counts. The root's `HallowsEveRetrofitPinTest` holds every id to its meaning before the bases existed (a frozen copy in root test resources): a deliberate change adds an allowance there with its own test, and never touches the frozen tree.
- A throw or crack chain is one Serial in the game's own order: the projectile (or the spend) first and the credit beside it, with the bomb spent last, or the payout inside the spend's Next. A Parallel forks the spend, and on the last item the engine's item-change check ends the throw still waiting on the client.
- Every Lantern Bomb blast spares players through one HitEntityRules rule, {Type Player, Invert true}, and no plain HitEntity: in a projectile's chain IgnoreOwner skips the projectile, never the thrower.
- The art under Common/ is generated: change tools/derive_art.py and rerun it, never hand-edit a file it writes. It reads byte copies of the vanilla files it needs from tools/seed/vanilla/, vendored locally and gitignored (refresh with --vendor <Assets.zip>), and python tools/check_refs.py checks every art path the pack's JSON names.
