# Seasons of Orbis

Yearly events for your Hytale server. Hallow's Eve runs from October 1 to November 12, Harvest Feast from November 20 to December 1, and every January 13 is Hytale's Anniversary.

Each season brings a host to the Forgotten Temple with quests, a daily board, a shop and achievements: Old Jack for Hallow's Eve, Martha the Cook for Harvest Feast. The pack needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.1 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer, on a Hytale Update 7 server. MMO Skill Tree and Alec's Tamework are optional. Everything plays in nine languages.

## Hallow's Eve, October 1 to November 12

- **Old Jack**, a pumpkin-headed host, sets up in the Forgotten Temple. His story runs through eight quests, out to the Horseman's graveyard and back, and ends with lighting the Hollow Lantern, a keepsake with the year on it.
- Old Jack's board posts a job for the night and one for the day, every day, beside his four dailies (one of them a win over the Headless Horseman) and two small errands that change every four hours. Ask him "What needs doing?" and one list shows them all. Take them, hand them in and collect your pay at Jack.
- **The Headless Horseman** rides his own graveyard. Buy a Grave Candle at Old Jack's shop for Hallow Sweets or Life Essence once he counts you a Regular, and the Gravekeeper beside him burns it to let you in, alone or with up to four friends, and the Horseman gets tougher with each of you. Four Jack Skeletons guard his fountain until you put them down. He fights from the saddle, then on foot, and his head flies off: he can't be hurt until you cut it down. As his health falls, his pumpkins hatch Pumpkin Fiends. Ask Old Jack about the Horseman for a hint. Die in his graveyard and you keep your items.
- Finish Old Jack's quest The Rider and the Gravekeeper takes you on the hard ride, for a Burning Grave Candle that Old Jack sells once you're Trusted with him, for Sweets or for Life Essence and Fire Essence, or once you're a Regular for more Sweets and some Life Essence. There he waits behind three waves of Jack Skeletons and Jack Archers, and as his health falls his pumpkins hatch five Pumpkin Fiends at a time, with two more Jack Archers. He has about twice the health and hits harder. He pays better for it.
- Every clear leaves a chest where he falls, with Hallow Sweets, Festival Tokens, Essence of Fire, Ectoplasm, a few random finds like Lantern Bombs or Fire Resistance Potions, and favor with Old Jack, the Hallowed and Festival Favor. A later clear the same day pays half.
- Your first win each day adds his daily Claim and his Boss Kill Claim to that chest: Hallow Sweets, Ectoplasm, Festival Tokens, a Spectral Lantern Bomb and favor with Old Jack, a Grave Candle back half the time, and sometimes a piece of his armour, a Burning Jack Helm or a Flaming Jack Shield. All his armour comes from the Boss Kill Claim, and the hard ride's is the only place to find his flaming armour and the Eternal Candle, which opens the normal graveyard without ever burning down. Once in a while the Boss Kill Claim holds the Horseman's Steed Trophy, a tiny figure of his black horse that you place as a block.
- After dark, away from torches, Jack Skeletons, Hollow Ghouls and Lost Souls come out, Pumpkin Fiends roam the autumn forests in small packs, and Geode Wraiths walk the mineshafts.
- Lantern Bombs, carved pumpkins packed with Boom Powder, in four tiers. They deal fire damage, and the stronger ones set creatures alight, slow them or throw flaming shards. Your own blast never hurts you, and it hurts other players only where PvP is on.
- The Hallowed set: the Jack Helm and Jack Shield, with the Horseman's armour. Any piece with a shield in your off hand gives more health and less fire damage, and you glow like a lantern. Wear all four armour pieces and your fire hits harder.
- The Flaming Hallowed set, the burning and flaming version of each piece, hits harder again. Both Jack Shields cut the fire damage you take, and Old Jack sells a Fire Resistance Potion that cuts it by about a third for three minutes, once he trusts you.
- A Costume Wand that turns a friend into a Jack Skeleton or a Lost Soul for a few minutes.
- Trick or treat. Knock on every character who has a conversation for you, once a day each, for sweets, apples, a pumpkin pie or a costume. On this pack alone, Old Jack and the Gravekeeper play along.
- Cursed Geodes turn up while you mine, and pumpkins you pick by hand sometimes come up Hallowed.
- Old Jack's shop takes Hallow Sweets for bombs, Grave Candles, the Jack set, the Costume Wand, Halloween decorations and the game's own Halloween Broomstick. Raise your standing and he sells five of Update 7's gadgets too, like the Spyglass and the Hookshot, and at the highest ranks he trades your Jack Helm and Jack Shield up to the Burning Jack Helm and Flaming Jack Shield.
- Two reputations, Old Jack's Favor and the Hallowed. His quests and jobs raise both, and putting down the night's creatures raises the Hallowed.
- The Carving Bench. Craft it at a Workbench and carve lanterns, the Jack Helm and Lantern Bombs on it all year.
- Harvest Moon, October 28 to November 1. About twice the creatures come out at night, and geodes and Hallowed Pumpkins come in pairs.
- Thirteen achievements each year, the last of them The Hallowed and its title, plus more that span the years and four for wearing the sets. The Hallowed asks for one win over the Horseman. Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane, for 10 and 50 wins in one Hallow's Eve, are a ladder of their own, with the title "Horseman's Bane" at the top. Bring him down with nothing but Lantern Bombs for By Lantern Light, or for Lamplighter and its title on the hard ride.

## Harvest Feast, November 20 to December 1

- **Martha the Cook** sets up a long table in the Forgotten Temple. A quest in your log, Meet Martha, sends you to her through any Forgotten Temple Gateway.
- Her four quests run from Wheat Harvest to The Feast. Turkey Hunt hands you the pumpkin pie recipe with a Dough and some Spices, and turns you into a turkey for three minutes. In Your First Pie you bake one at a Chef's Stove.
- The Feast comes back every year. Bring a pumpkin pie, some cooked meat and a basket of carrots, sit down, and take home a Feast Plate with the year on it and two Meat Pies.
- Three dailies: Daily Turkeys, Daily Wheat and Daily Roast. Shared Meal comes round every day too. Roast a vegetable on a Campfire and share it at her table.
- Extra Turkeys, as often as you like. Hunt ten more Wild Turkeys, take your pay, and go again.
- The Guest List, once a feast. Invite three people to the feast, then tell Martha who's coming. Tomas the Miller, Elsie the Weaver and Bram the Shepherd stand around the temple, so there's always somebody to ask, and each has a tip for you on dough, spices or turkeys.
- The Feast Board posts a Field job and a Kitchen job every day: turkeys, wheat, pumpkins or carrots out in the fields, pies, roast meat, roast vegetables or spices in the kitchen. Get to know Martha and a second of each opens up.
- Wild Turkeys roam the first zone's woods and plains by day, in small flocks, and every one you hunt earns Martha's favor. You can tame them too. Pumpkins you pick by hand sometimes come with Spices, which a pumpkin pie needs.
- Harvest Coins, the feast's own currency. Martha pays them for everything, and her shop takes them for the recipes for her own dishes, popcorn, corn seeds, autumn leaves, hay and a Scarecrow. They keep until next year. Its Festival Shelf has the winter decorations for Festival Tokens, from a Winter Stocking to a Snowman.
- Martha's own dishes, each better than any food in the game, cooked at a Chef's Stove once you buy the recipe: Turkey Pie for more max health, Harvest Salad for more max stamina, and Martha's Roast for both, with her favor coming faster while it lasts. The Salad's recipe opens at Neighbor, the Roast's at Family.
- The Harvest Set: the Harvest Gloves, Hat, Trousers and Tunic, four wool pieces from Martha's shop that open as her favor grows. Each adds health and stamina and earns her favor 5% faster. Wear two for more health, and all four for more health again and another 10% favor.
- Martha's Favor runs from Newcomer through Neighbor, Friend and Family to Head of the Table. Each rank pays a gift you collect on the achievements page, and Head of the Table adds a title. Past the top it keeps counting, and every 5,000 more favor packs you a Feast Basket of pies, coins and tokens.
- Five achievements each year. Feast Guest, Turkey Chaser, Full Baskets and Feast Plate, and The Full Table for earning the other four, with the title "of the Full Table". One more for wearing the whole Harvest Set.

## Every season

- Festival Favor, a reputation every season shares, and Festival Tokens, a wallet that keeps from one season to the next. Old Jack's and Martha's quests and board jobs pay both.
- The Festival Shelf at each host's shop sells by season for Festival Tokens. High Festival Favor opens the Goblin Plushie and the Carved Kweebec Figure, found nowhere else.
- Achievements for keepsakes from different seasons, up to Seasons of Orbis and the title "Friend of the Seasons".
- An Almanac page for each season with your keepsakes, your lifetime tallies and every item to find, with where it comes from. Hallow's Eve's shows what the Horseman's Boss Kill Claim pays and how often the whole server has brought him down.

## Hytale's Anniversary, January 13

From 2027, the day Hytale came out gets a banner and an Almanac page. Be on the server that day for that year's achievement and the title "the Ever-Present".

## How it works

1. Log in while a season runs. A banner marks it, and a quest in your log sends you to its host in the Forgotten Temple: Lanterns in the Temple to Old Jack, or Meet Martha to Martha the Cook.
2. Press F on the host to hand it in. Old Jack's and Martha's both give you an Objective Book, which opens your quests, achievements and the Almanac.
3. Ask "What needs doing?" for the host's quests and board jobs in one list, or open their shop. Once they know you, "How do we stand?" shows your reputation.
4. Head out for the season's work, Old Jack's creatures after dark or Martha's turkeys and crops by day, then spend what you earn at the host's shop.
5. When the season ends the host leaves, and everything you earned stays with you. An unfinished quest waits for next year.

## With MMO Skill Tree

Install MMO Skill Tree 1.7.2 or newer and both seasons train your skills. Old Jack's quests, jobs and the season's achievements pay XP in the skill the work trains: Defense for the fights, Harvesting for the pumpkins, Mining for the geodes and Artillery for the bombs. The Horseman pays Defense XP for each day's first win and for clearing his graveyard. During Harvest Feast, Martha's quests and jobs pay Harvesting, Defense, Crafting and Acrobatics XP, her rank gifts add an XP boost, her board posts a Skill job every day, the Harvest Set adds Harvesting XP and Harvesting luck, and Harvesting XP runs a quarter higher for everyone while the feast does. Without it, the rest of each season plays the same.

Add the [MMO Skill Bounty Pack](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree-bounty-board-pack-rich-customizable) and its Daily board posts a haunt contract each day of Hallow's Eve.

Add [MMO Mob Scaling](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-mob-scaling) and the Horseman grows with his graveyard's level, the same as his skeletons, so a stronger group meets a tougher Horseman.

## With Alec's Tamework

Install [Alec's Tamework](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/alecs-tamework) 5 and the Headless Horseman's Boss Kill Claim can drop the Horseman's Reins, which make his black barded horse yours to ride. They're very rare on the normal ride and 1 in 20 on the hard one. Right-click with the Reins to call your horse from your roster, then press F to ride. Only you can ride it. Old Jack buys a spare pair for Hallow Sweets.

Without Tamework the Reins never drop, and the Almanac leaves them out.

## For server owners

- Nothing to place. Each event starts and ends by date (UTC), and its host sets up on their own.
- Want a host somewhere else? Give their placement a `Where` naming another world in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/npc-placements.json`, and their shop, boards and quests go with them.
- To switch Hallow's Eve off, add `"Hallows_Eve": { "Enabled": false }` to `mods/ziggfreedcommon/calendar.json` and run `/zigcalendar reload`. Every event has its own switch there, and the same file can move an event's dates. Nothing anyone earned is lost.
- The Festival Shelf can also open all year as a shop of its own: switch it on in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/shops.json`.
- The Horseman's Graveyard and its hard ride are CommonLib dungeons, `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard` and `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Hard`: switch either off or change how many players share it in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json`. `/zigdungeon enter Hallows_Eve_Graveyard` takes you in alone while Hallow's Eve runs, without spending a candle.

## Install

1. Run your server on Hytale's Update 7, with Ziggfreed's CommonLib (2.5.1 or newer) and RPG Stations (1.1.1 or newer).
2. Drop the Seasons of Orbis zip into your server's `Mods/` folder beside them.
3. Start the server. While a season runs, its host is in the Forgotten Temple.
