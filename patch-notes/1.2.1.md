Seasons of Orbis 1.2.1 gives every clear of the Headless Horseman's graveyard a bigger reward, on both rides.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.4.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer.

## Changes

- Clearing the graveyard now pays 5 to 8 Essence of Fire, 3 to 4 Ectoplasm and three random finds on top of its 30 Hallow Sweets and 3 Festival Tokens. Each find is 2 Cursed Geodes, 3 Burning Lantern Bombs, 2 Spectral Lantern Bombs, 2 Fire Resistance Potions, 3 Grave Candles or, once in a while, a Shattering Lantern Bomb.
- Clearing the hard graveyard now pays 90 Hallow Sweets instead of 60, with its 6 Festival Tokens, 10 to 14 Essence of Fire, 6 to 8 Ectoplasm and four random finds from the same list, a Burning Grave Candle in place of the 3 Grave Candles.
- Later clears the same day still pay half of everything, finds included.
