Seasons of Orbis 1.2.3 is out now! The Headless Horseman rides out in a look all his own, and while you fight him his pumpkins hatch the new Pumpkin Fiends. After dark they roam the autumn forests too.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.1 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. Alec's Tamework 5 is optional and adds the Horseman's Reins.

## New

- Added the Pumpkin Fiend, a small pumpkin-headed creature that comes out in packs after dark in the autumn forests while Hallow's Eve runs. Squash one for a chance at a Hallowed Pumpkin.
- In the Headless Horseman's graveyard, his pumpkins hatch Pumpkin Fiends as his health falls, three at a time on the normal ride. On the hard ride, the waves that join him during the fight now bring five fiends in place of their three Jack Skeletons, beside the same two Jack Archers.
- Added two Pumpkin Fiend jobs for Old Jack: Weeding the Patch, a night job on his board, and Bad Seeds, a night errand. Fiends his pumpkins hatch in the Horseman's fight count toward both.
- Added the Horseman's Helm, a fourth piece of his armour, and the Flaming Horseman's Helm, both from his Boss Kill Claim. Each fills the head slot in the Hallowed set, and the flaming one counts toward the Flaming Hallowed set as well.
- Added the Horseman's Steed Trophy, a tiny figure of the Headless Horseman's black horse in its barding that you place as a block. It drops now and then from his Boss Kill Claim, and more often on the hard ride.
- Added the Horseman's Reins for servers running Alec's Tamework: the Headless Horseman's black horse as your own mount. They drop very rarely from his Boss Kill Claim, and 1 in 20 times on the hard ride. Right-click with them to open your roster and summon your horse, then press F to ride. Only you can ride it. Old Jack buys a spare pair for Hallow Sweets, and the Almanac lists the Reins only on servers running Tamework.

## Changes

- The Headless Horseman has a new look: he rides out on a black armoured horse with a rotted tail, wears his own black armour with a green fire burning in his neck, and on the hard ride his armour is trimmed in gold.
- The Horseman's Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves now look like the armour he wears, and their Flaming versions are trimmed in gold. Pieces you already have change too.
- The Horseman's Hoard is now called his Boss Kill Claim, paid for your first win over him each day, and all his armour comes from it. A Hoard still waiting on your Claims tab opens under the new name. His daily Claim still pays Hallow Sweets, Ectoplasm, Festival Tokens and Lantern Bombs.

We'll see you in the graveyard!
