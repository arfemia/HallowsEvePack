Seasons of Orbis 1.1.0 gives Hallow's Eve more to do and adds what every season shares. Old Jack's board gets four new jobs and a second board of small errands, his work pays Festival Favor and Festival Tokens, his stall carries the Festival Shelf and five of Update 7's gadgets, and the Almanac shows every item a season has to find. It also adds Hytale's Anniversary every January 13. Built for Hytale's Update 7, it requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.1]] beside it Hallow's Eve trains your skills.

### Hallow's Eve

- **Added four jobs to Old Jack's board.** At night, Mineshaft Wraiths has you put down Geode Wraiths in the mineshafts and Lantern Fuel wants Ectoplasm brought to his board. By day, Cold Pumpkins wants Hallowed Pumpkins and Bench Carving has you carve Jack Lanterns at a Carving Bench. They pay like his other board jobs.
- **Added Old Jack's Errands.** Ask Old Jack "Got any errands?" and he shows a second board with two small jobs, one for the night and one for the day, that change every four hours. An errand is half a board job, for about half the pay, and its pay waits at the errands board until you collect it.
- **Board pay you can see.** Every job on Old Jack's board now lists 10 Hallow Sweets in its rewards before you take it, and an errand lists 5. On top of that, collecting a job pays a bonus of anywhere from none to 12 sweets, where it was always 12.
- **New at Old Jack's stall: five of Update 7's gadgets.** Once you are Friendly with the Hallowed he sells the Spyglass for 400 Hallow Sweets and three Goblin Repair Kits for 150, at Honored the Scrap Glider for 800, and once you are Trusted with him the Hookshot for 800 and three Hook Anchors for 100.
- **Jack's Guest pays more.** The achievement Jack's Guest, for being here during Hallow's Eve, now gives you the title "Jack's Guest", plus 500 favor with Old Jack and 250 with the Hallowed, which wait on the achievements page for you to collect. If you earned this year's Jack's Guest before this update, the title arrives the next time you log in, and the favor waits on the achievements page for you to collect.
- **Cursed Geodes and trick-or-treating pay in full.** Cracking a Cursed Geode now hands over the Lantern Bombs, Ectoplasm or Essence of Fire it was meant to, beside its crystals, and a knock now hands out the extra sweets, apples, pumpkin pie and costumes it was meant to. Before, a geode gave only its crystals and the odd Shattering Lantern Bomb, and a knock only its first few sweets.
- **Lantern Bombs deal fire.** A Lantern Bomb's blast and its shards now deal the game's own Fire damage, so a creature's fire resistance softens them, and with MMO Mob Scaling a mob's Fire ward does too. With MMO Skill Tree they still train Artillery, and so does the burn the stronger bombs leave on a creature.
- **The Shattering Lantern Bomb's blast hurts.** Where a Shattering Lantern Bomb lands, its blast now damages the creatures around it like the other Lantern Bombs, and its four shards still fly out and burst.
- **Apples for trick-or-treaters.** The food a knock can hand out is apples now in place of candy canes, which belong to winter, and the Almanac shows the apple in its place.
- **The Hallowed's caches wait for you.** Past Exalted, every 5,000 more favor with the Hallowed earns a cache, which now waits on the Hallowed's Reputation page until you collect it.
- **The Hallowed set shows with your other effects.** While you wear the full Hallowed set, a Hallowed Set icon with the Hallowed Pumpkin shows with your other effects.
- **Costumes have names.** Hover a costume's icon and it now reads Jack Skeleton Costume or Lost Soul Costume.

### Every season

- **Added an items grid to the Almanac.** Each season's Almanac page shows every item you can find that season under its tallies, 33 of them for Hallow's Eve. The rarest show a question mark until you have held one, and hovering an item tells you where it comes from.
- **Festival Favor.** A reputation every season shares, listed above each season's own on the menu's Reputation tab. Every quest Old Jack gives after Lanterns in the Temple pays it, and so does every Night and Day job on his board, and the quest's rewards say how much. It uses the plain ranks up to Exalted and keeps going past it, through Exalted II, Exalted III and Exalted IV, and past Exalted IV every 5,000 more favor earns you 25 Festival Tokens, which wait on its Reputation page until you collect them. It stays with you from season to season and year to year.
- **Festival Tokens.** The wallet every season shares. Collecting a finished quest or board job from Old Jack pays them on top of his sweets, and Lanterns in the Temple pays a couple to start you off. You can't drop or lose them, they keep from one season to the next, and your wallet shows them all year.
- **The Festival Shelf.** Old Jack's stall carries the Festival Shelf after his own shelves, priced in Festival Tokens, and its header shows your tokens beside your Hallow Sweets. What it sells follows the calendar: during Hallow's Eve it has a Halloween Pumpkin Basket for 25, and anything out of season isn't on the shelf at all. A server owner can also open the shelf all year as a stall of its own, where the winter decorations sell from November 20 to January 6 and the Pumpkin Basket during Hallow's Eve.
- **What Festival Favor opens.** Exalted II opens the Goblin Plushie on the Festival Shelf for 60 tokens, and Exalted III the Carved Kweebec Figure for 80. No recipe or drop in the game hands either one out. Until you reach the rank, you see them on the shelf, locked. Reach Exalted IV for the achievement Guest of Honor and its title, "Guest of Honor".
- **Board pay you missed waits in your Objective Book.** A board job you finished but did not collect before Hallow's Eve ends shows Collect in your Objective Book.
- **Favor shows where you earn it.** When collecting a job, a quest or an achievement, buying at a stall or picking a line in a conversation raises your favor, the notice that pops up lists it with the rest of what you got, such as "+500 Old Jack's Favor". Your favor no longer pops up as a bar in the middle of the screen once you close the menu.
- **A locked ware names the rank it needs.** Hovering a ware you can't buy yet, or pressing Buy, now names the rank and the reputation it asks for, such as "Requires Friendly with the Hallowed", where it read "Requires a rank with a reputation".

### Keepsakes across the seasons

- **The Seasons of Orbis achievements.** Three achievements count the keepsakes you earn in different seasons, such as the Hollow Lantern, with any year's keepsake counting, including a Hollow Lantern you have already earned. Season to Season wants keepsakes from two seasons and Round the Year from four. Seasons of Orbis wants one from every season your server runs, never fewer than four, so it grows as seasons are added. Season to Season pays 25 Festival Tokens, Round the Year 50 and Seasons of Orbis 100, each with Festival Favor, waiting on the achievements page for you to collect, and Seasons of Orbis also pays a Harvest Trophy and gives you the title "Friend of the Seasons".

### Hytale's Anniversary

- **Hytale's Anniversary.** Every January 13, starting in 2027, the day Hytale came out gets a banner and an Almanac page of its own. Be on the server that day for that year's anniversary achievement, the first being Hytale's First Anniversary, and the title "the Ever-Present". The day runs from midnight to midnight UTC, and each year's achievement stays with you.

### With other mods

- **MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]] or newer.** Hallow's Eve pays more skill XP. Old Jack's dailies and every job on his board, the Skill jobs included, pay 1,500 XP each, and his errands 750. Each step of his questline and Lanterns in the Temple pay 2,500, and lighting the Hollow Lantern pays 5,000 plus 2,500. The achievements Ghoul Breaker, Lantern Lobber and Soul Warden pay 2,500, and Geode Cracker pays 5,000. Lost Souls and Jack Skeletons give 130 kill XP, and Hollow Ghouls and Geode Wraiths give 250.
- **The Hallowed set with MMO Skill Tree.** Wearing the full Hallowed set gives one notice that carries both its bonuses, MMO Skill Tree's included, where it gave two.

Harvest Feast, the next season, comes later this year.

Everything in the pack ships in nine languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
