Seasons of Orbis gets its second season, Harvest Feast, when Martha the Cook sets up a long table in the Forgotten Temple from November 20 to December 1. It also adds what every season shares (Festival Favor, Festival Tokens to spend at a Festival Shelf in each host's stall, and a ladder of achievements for keepsakes from different seasons) and Hytale's Anniversary every January 13. Built for Hytale's Update 7, it requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.1]] beside it both seasons train your skills.

### Harvest Feast

- **Harvest Feast comes round every year.** It runs from November 20 to December 1, and Martha the Cook takes her spot in the Forgotten Temple, behind a tavern table with a bench across from her, when it starts and leaves when it ends. A banner marks its start and its end, and a server owner can switch it off or move its days.
- **Wild Turkeys.** While the feast runs, small flocks of Wild Turkeys roam the first zone's autumn woods, forests and plains by day, and the ones still out are gone after dark. They drop what the game's own turkeys drop, they come to you for corn, and you can tame them. None of them are Memories.
- **A pinch of spice.** While the feast runs, a pumpkin you harvest by hand sometimes comes with Spices, one less thing to find for a pumpkin pie.
- **Off means gone.** When the feast ends or a server owner switches it off, Martha leaves with her stall, her board, her quests, the three villagers and the invitation line, and no more Wild Turkeys come. A quest you have not finished waits for her to come back, and so does pay you never collected at her board. Nothing you earned goes with her.

### Martha's table

- **Supper in the Temple.** While the feast runs, every player finds this quest in their quest log, tracked, sending them through any Forgotten Temple Gateway to find Martha the Cook. Talk to her to hand it in and collect two Festival Tokens, and she offers her first quest. It comes once, and never to a player who has already finished Wheat for the Ovens.
- **A questline at the Forgotten Temple.** Martha has four quests: Wheat for the Ovens, The Turkey Hunt, The First Pie and The Long Table. The Turkey Hunt hands you the recipe for a pumpkin pie and turns you into a turkey for three minutes (take the costume off any time with `/zigcostume off`, and if you are already wearing one, she pays a Festival Token instead). The First Pie has you bake one at a Chef's Stove. The Long Table is the feast itself: bring a pumpkin pie, cooked wildmeat and carrots, sit down, and take home two Meat Pies. It comes back once every Harvest Feast.
- **Dailies and a shared meal.** Once you have finished Wheat for the Ovens, Martha has three dailies: Turkey Run, Bread for the Morning and Roast for the Table. From then on The Shared Table comes round every day too: roast a vegetable over a fire, bring it to her table and take home a loaf.
- **The Guest List.** Once you have finished Wheat for the Ovens, and once every Harvest Feast, Martha asks you to invite people to the feast. While you carry it, every character with a conversation for you, Martha and Old Jack aside, has a line for it, and each one can be asked once a day. Ask three, then go back and tell her who's coming. Tomas the Miller, Elsie the Weaver and Bram the Shepherd stand around the Forgotten Temple while the feast runs, so there is always somebody to ask.
- **The Feast Board.** Martha's board posts a Field job and a Kitchen job every day. The Field work brings down Wild Turkeys or picks wild wheat, and the Kitchen work wants pumpkin pies or cooked wildmeat brought to the table. A second Field job opens once you are a Neighbor with Martha, and a second Kitchen job once you are a Known Face; until then you see them on the board, locked. The pie job stays locked until you finish The Turkey Hunt, which teaches you the recipe. A finished job's pay waits at the board until you collect it, so collect it before the table is packed away.
- **The Feast Stall.** Harvest Feast pays in Festival Tokens, and Martha's stall takes them. She sells the Apple Pie Recipe for 8, the Meat Pie Recipe for 12 once you are a Neighbor and the Caesar Salad Recipe for 20 once you are Family, each one once. You can also buy four Popcorn for 2 tokens twice a day, four Corn Seed Bags for 3 once a day, and 16 Autumn Leaves for 2. Her stall carries the Festival Shelf too. You find the stall and the board only in the Forgotten Temple.
- **Achievements and a title.** Sitting down to The Long Table earns Feast Plate, a keepsake plate with the year in its name, waiting on the achievements page for you to collect. A Seat at the Table is for being here during the feast, Turkey Chaser is for bringing down 25 Wild Turkeys and Full Baskets is for picking 100 crops by hand. Earn all four in one season and the achievement The Full Table gives you the title "of the Full Table". Each year's achievements stay with you as feats.
- **An Almanac page.** Harvest Feast has its own Almanac page, and Martha opens it for you. Its banner is built from pies, popcorn, crops and a Harvest Trophy under a warm glow, and it keeps your Feast Plates and lifetime tallies of Wild Turkeys brought down, crops picked by hand, feasts sat down to and errands run for Martha.

### Favor at the Feast

- **Favor with Martha.** Favor at the Feast runs from Newcomer through Neighbor, Known Face and Family to Head of the Table, and stops there. Every quest Martha gives after Supper in the Temple, and every job on her board, pays favor with her, and the quest's rewards say how much. Once a day she points you at her board and pays a little favor for stopping by. Once she knows you, "How do we stand?" opens the Reputation page. Favor stays with you from one Harvest Feast to the next.
- **What her favor opens.** As a Neighbor you can buy the Meat Pie Recipe and take the board's second Field job. Known Face opens its second Kitchen job, and Family the Caesar Salad Recipe. Reach Head of the Table, her top rank, for the achievement Head of the Table and its title, "Head of the Table". It is yours to keep.

### Festival Favor and Festival Tokens

- **Festival Favor.** A reputation every season shares, listed above each season's own on the menu's Reputation tab. Every quest Martha or Old Jack gives you, except the one that first sends you to them, pays it, and so does every Field, Kitchen, Night and Day job on their boards. It uses the plain ranks up to Exalted and keeps going past it, through Exalted II, Exalted III and Exalted IV, and past Exalted IV every 5,000 more favor pays you 25 Festival Tokens. It stays with you from season to season and year to year.
- **Festival Tokens.** The wallet every season shares. Martha's quests and her Field and Kitchen jobs pay them, and so do Old Jack's quests and board jobs. You can't drop or lose them, they keep from one season to the next, and your wallet shows them all year.
- **The Festival Shelf.** Martha's stall and Old Jack's both carry the Festival Shelf after their own shelves, priced in Festival Tokens: a Winter Stocking for 20, a Halloween Pumpkin Basket for 25, a Winter Wreath, Winter Garland or Winter Lights for 30 each, a Small Red Christmas Packet for 40 and a Snowman for 50. Every season's host carries the same shelf, so tokens from Hallow's Eve spend just as well at Harvest Feast. A server owner can also open it all year as a stall of its own.
- **What Festival Favor opens.** Exalted II opens the Goblin Plushie on the Festival Shelf for 60 tokens, and Exalted III the Carved Kweebec Figure for 80. No recipe or drop in the game hands either one out. Until you reach the rank, you see them on the shelf, locked. Reach Exalted IV for the achievement Guest of Honor and its title, "Guest of Honor".

### Keepsakes across the seasons

- **The Seasons of Orbis achievements.** Three achievements count the keepsakes you earn in different seasons, the Hollow Lantern and the Feast Plate in this pack, with any year's keepsake counting, including a Hollow Lantern you have already earned. Season to Season wants keepsakes from two seasons and Round the Year from four. Seasons of Orbis wants one from every season your server runs, never fewer than four, so it grows as seasons are added. Hallow's Eve and Harvest Feast are the two seasons so far, so of the three, Season to Season is the one within reach. Season to Season pays 25 Festival Tokens, Round the Year 50 and Seasons of Orbis 100, each with Festival Favor, waiting on the achievements page for you to collect, and Seasons of Orbis also pays a Harvest Trophy and gives you the title "Friend of the Seasons".

### Hytale's Anniversary

- **Hytale's Anniversary.** Every January 13, starting in 2027, the day Hytale came out gets a banner and an Almanac page of its own. Be on the server that day for that year's anniversary achievement, the first being Hytale's First Anniversary, and the title "the Ever-Present". The day runs from midnight to midnight UTC, and each year's achievement stays with you.

### Hallow's Eve

- **Old Jack's work raises your Festival Favor.** Every quest Old Jack gives after Lanterns in the Temple, and every Night and Day job on his board, also raises your Festival Favor, and the quest's rewards say how much.
- **Festival Tokens from Old Jack.** Collecting a finished quest or board job from Old Jack pays Festival Tokens on top of his sweets, and Lanterns in the Temple pays a couple to start you off.
- **The Festival Shelf at Old Jack's stall.** His stall carries the Festival Shelf after his own shelves, priced in Festival Tokens, and its header shows your tokens beside your Hallow Sweets.

### With other mods

- **MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]] or newer.** Hallow's Eve pays more skill XP. Old Jack's dailies and every job on his board, the Skill jobs included, pay 1,500 XP each. Each step of his questline and Lanterns in the Temple pay 2,500, and lighting the Hollow Lantern pays 5,000 plus 2,500. The achievements Ghoul Breaker, Lantern Lobber and Soul Warden pay 2,500, and Geode Cracker pays 5,000. Lost Souls and Jack Skeletons give 130 kill XP, and Hollow Ghouls and Geode Wraiths give 250.
- **Harvest Feast with MMO Skill Tree.** Martha's quests and board jobs and the achievements Turkey Chaser and Full Baskets pay XP in the skill the work trains: Harvesting for the wheat and crops, Defense for the turkeys, Crafting for the cooking and baking, and Acrobatics for the walk to the temple and the invitations. Her dailies and the Field and Kitchen jobs pay 1,500 XP each. Supper in the Temple, her first three quests, The Guest List, Turkey Chaser and Full Baskets pay 2,500, and The Long Table pays 5,000 plus 2,500. An achievement's XP waits on the achievements page until you collect it. The Shared Table, A Seat at the Table and The Full Table each add an XP boost token. Her board posts a Skill job every day (Full Fields, Busy Benches, Hunter's Practice or Oven Wood), which pays favor with Martha and 1,500 XP in its skill, and Harvesting XP runs a quarter higher for everyone while the feast does. On a server without MMO Skill Tree those rewards don't show and nothing else changes.

Everything in the pack ships in nine languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
