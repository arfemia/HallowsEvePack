### Hallow's Eve

- **Old Jack's work raises your Festival Favor.** Every quest Old Jack gives and every job on his board also raises Festival Favor, the reputation every season pays into, and the quest's rewards say how much.
- **Festival Tokens from Old Jack.** Collecting a finished quest or board job from Old Jack pays Festival Tokens on top of his sweets, and Lanterns in the Temple pays a couple to start you off.
- **The Festival Shelf at Old Jack's stall.** His stall carries the Festival Shelf after his own shelves, priced in Festival Tokens, and its header shows your tokens beside your Hallow Sweets.

### With other mods

- **MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]] or newer.** Hallow's Eve pays more skill XP. Old Jack's dailies and every job on his board, the Skill jobs included, pay 1,500 XP each. Each step of his questline and Lanterns in the Temple pay 2,500, and lighting the Hollow Lantern pays 5,000 plus 2,500. The achievements Ghoul Breaker, Lantern Lobber and Soul Warden pay 2,500, and Geode Cracker pays 5,000. Lost Souls and Jack Skeletons give 130 kill XP, and Hollow Ghouls and Geode Wraiths give 250.
