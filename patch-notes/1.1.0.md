Old Jack has more for you to do this Hallow's Eve, and every season gets Festival Favor, the Festival Shelf and an Almanac grid of every item to find.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.1]] beside it Hallow's Eve trains your skills.

### New

- **Added four jobs to Old Jack's board.** At night, Mineshaft Wraiths has you put down Geode Wraiths in the mineshafts and Lantern Fuel wants Ectoplasm brought to his board. By day, Cold Pumpkins wants Hallowed Pumpkins and Bench Carving has you carve Jack Lanterns at a Carving Bench. They pay like his other board jobs.
- Added Old Jack's Errands. Ask Old Jack "Got any errands?" and he shows a second board with two small jobs, one for the night and one for the day, that change every four hours. An errand is half a board job, for about half the pay, and its pay waits at the errands board until you collect it.
- Added five of Update 7's gadgets to Old Jack's stall. Once you are Friendly with the Hallowed he sells the Spyglass for 400 Hallow Sweets and three Goblin Repair Kits for 150, at Honored the Scrap Glider for 800, and once you are Trusted with him the Hookshot for 800 and three Hook Anchors for 100.
- **Added an items grid to the Almanac.** Each season's page shows every item you can find that season under its tallies, 33 of them for Hallow's Eve. The rarest show a question mark until you have held one, and hovering an item tells you where it comes from.
- **Added Festival Favor**, a reputation every season shares, listed above each season's own on the menu's Reputation tab. Every quest Old Jack gives after Lanterns in the Temple pays it, and so does every Night and Day job on his board. The quest's rewards say how much.
- Festival Favor runs through the plain ranks to Exalted and keeps going, through Exalted II, Exalted III and Exalted IV. Past Exalted IV, every 5,000 more favor earns you 25 Festival Tokens, which wait on its Reputation page until you collect them. It stays with you from season to season and year to year.
- Added Festival Tokens, the wallet every season shares. Collecting a finished quest or board job from Old Jack pays them on top of his sweets, and Lanterns in the Temple pays a couple to start you off. You can't drop or lose them, they keep from one season to the next, and your wallet shows them all year.
- Added the Festival Shelf to Old Jack's stall, after his own shelves, priced in Festival Tokens, with your tokens shown beside your Hallow Sweets. It sells what's in season: during Hallow's Eve that's a Halloween Pumpkin Basket for 25.
- Exalted II opens the Goblin Plushie on the Festival Shelf for 60 tokens, and Exalted III the Carved Kweebec Figure for 80. No recipe or drop in the game hands out either one. Until you reach the rank, you see them on the shelf, locked.
- Reach Exalted IV for the achievement Guest of Honor and its title, "Guest of Honor".
- Added three achievements for keepsakes from different seasons, such as the Hollow Lantern. Any year's keepsake counts, including a Hollow Lantern you have already earned. Season to Season wants keepsakes from two seasons and Round the Year from four. Seasons of Orbis wants one from every season your server runs, never fewer than four, so it grows as seasons are added.
- Season to Season pays 25 Festival Tokens, Round the Year 50 and Seasons of Orbis 100, each with Festival Favor, waiting on the achievements page for you to collect. Seasons of Orbis also pays a Harvest Trophy and gives you the title "Friend of the Seasons".
- Added Hytale's Anniversary. Every January 13, starting in 2027, the day Hytale came out gets a banner and an Almanac page of its own. Be on the server that day for that year's anniversary achievement, the first being Hytale's First Anniversary, and the title "the Ever-Present".
- The anniversary runs from midnight to midnight UTC, and each year's achievement stays with you.
- Everything in the pack ships in nine languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
- Harvest Feast, the next season, comes later this year.

### Changes

- Jack's Guest, the achievement for being here during Hallow's Eve, now gives you the title "Jack's Guest", plus 500 favor with Old Jack and 250 with the Hallowed to collect on the achievements page. Earned this year's before this update? The title arrives the next time you log in, and the favor waits there for you.
- Every job on Old Jack's board now lists 10 Hallow Sweets in its rewards before you take it, and an errand lists 5. Collecting a job adds a bonus of anywhere from none to 12 sweets, where it was always 12.
- Lantern Bombs now deal the game's own Fire damage, blast and shards alike, so a creature's fire resistance softens them, and with MMO Mob Scaling a mob's Fire ward does too. With MMO Skill Tree they still train Artillery, and so does the burn the stronger bombs leave on a creature.
- A knock now hands out apples in place of candy canes, which belong to winter, and the Almanac shows the apple in its place.
- Past Exalted with the Hallowed, the cache you earn every 5,000 more favor now waits on the Hallowed's Reputation page until you collect it.
- Wearing the full Hallowed set now shows a Hallowed Set icon, with the Hallowed Pumpkin, beside your other effects.
- Favor you earn in a menu now shows in the notice that pops up with the rest of what you got, such as "+500 Old Jack's Favor", whether you collected a job, a quest or an achievement, bought at a stall or picked a line in a conversation. It no longer pops up as a bar in the middle of the screen once you close the menu.
- A board job you finished but did not collect before Hallow's Eve ends now shows Collect in your Objective Book.
- With MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]] or newer, Hallow's Eve pays more skill XP. Old Jack's dailies and every job on his board, the Skill jobs included, pay 1,500 XP each, and his errands 750. Each step of his questline and Lanterns in the Temple pay 2,500, and lighting the Hollow Lantern pays 5,000 plus 2,500.
- With it, the achievements Ghoul Breaker, Lantern Lobber and Soul Warden pay 2,500 XP and Geode Cracker 5,000. Lost Souls and Jack Skeletons give 130 kill XP, and Hollow Ghouls and Geode Wraiths 250.
- With MMO Skill Tree, the XP boost tokens from Jack's Guest and The Hallowed now wait on the achievements page with the rest of what you collect, and the notice when you collect lists them. If you earned this year's Jack's Guest before this update, you get its token a second time when you collect its favor.
- With MMO Skill Tree, wearing the full Hallowed set gives one notice that carries both its bonuses, where it gave two.

### Fixes

- Cracking a Cursed Geode hands over the Lantern Bombs, Ectoplasm or Essence of Fire it was meant to, beside its crystals. Before, a geode gave only its crystals and the odd Shattering Lantern Bomb.
- A knock hands out the extra sweets, apples, pumpkin pie and costumes it was meant to, where it gave only its first few sweets.
- Where a Shattering Lantern Bomb lands, its blast now damages the creatures around it like the other Lantern Bombs, and its four shards still fly out and burst.
- Hovering a costume's icon reads Jack Skeleton Costume or Lost Soul Costume.
- A locked ware names the rank and the reputation it needs, such as "Requires Friendly with the Hallowed", where it read "Requires a rank with a reputation".

### For server owners

- You can open the Festival Shelf all year as a stall of its own in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/shops.json`. Each ware keeps its own dates there: the winter decorations sell from November 20 to January 6, and the Pumpkin Basket during Hallow's Eve.
