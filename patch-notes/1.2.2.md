Seasons of Orbis 1.2.2 is out now! It adds five waves of Jack Skeletons and the new Jack Archers to the hard ride, between you and the Headless Horseman, and more of them rise while he fights.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer.

## New

- Added skeleton waves to the hard ride. In place of the four Jack Skeletons at the fountain, five waves of three Jack Skeletons and two Jack Archers rise around it one after another, and the Horseman rides out only once the fifth is down. Beat them once and they stay beaten, even if he wipes your party.
- During the hard fight, another wave joins him each time he loses a fifth of his health. A wipe brings those back for your next try.
- Added the Jack Archer, a pumpkin-headed skeleton with a bow that keeps its distance and shoots. You only meet it in the hard ride's waves. It drops what a Jack Skeleton drops, pays Hallowed favor, and counts wherever a Jack Skeleton does: The Hollow Lantern's skeleton step, the Harvest Moon Hunt board job and the Almanac's skeleton tally. With MMO Skill Tree, it pays kill XP too.

## Changes

- With MMO Mob Scaling 1.2.4 or newer installed, the Headless Horseman now grows with the level of his graveyard, the same as his Jack Skeletons, so a stronger group meets a tougher Horseman on both rides.
- Old Jack's shop, boards and quests now work wherever a server puts him, not only in the Forgotten Temple.
- The Fire Resistance Potion's tooltip now lists its effect and how long it lasts, like the game's own potions.

## Fixes

- Fixed the Headless Horseman's fight losing track of you at the graveyard's entrance end. His music, health and wipe checks now reach that far, and he turns back instead of chasing you into the entrance room.

## For server owners

- To move Old Jack, give his placement a `Where` naming another world in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/npc-placements.json`. His shop, boards and quests go with him.

We'll see you in the graveyard!
