Seasons of Orbis 1.2.4 is out now! Old Jack takes Life Essence for his graveyard candles again, so you can ride against the Headless Horseman without spending your Hallow Sweets.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.1 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer.

## New

- Added a Life Essence price for the Grave Candle at Old Jack's shop: 500 Life Essence and no Sweets, once he counts you a Regular. It sits right beside the Sweets price.
- Added a Life Essence price for the Burning Grave Candle: 1,500 Life Essence and 2 Fire Essence, no Sweets, once you're Trusted with him.

## Changes

- The Burning Grave Candle Old Jack sells to Regulars is now marked as the Regular's price, and says the same candle costs less right beside it once you're Trusted.

We'll see you in the graveyard!
