## Changed

- `NPC/Groups/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Group.json` carries `"Tags": { "ZigRank": [ "Boss" ] }`, so CommonLib's crowd-control rule (`BossRules`) counts every form of the Headless Horseman, both rides, as a boss: a talent's or a proc's stun, root, slow or push never lands on him, an ability's own does at the cast's own strength, and a taunt always does. His Head (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head_Group`) and his adds stay ordinary foes. A CommonLib without the rule ignores the tag.
- `NPC/Groups/Seasons_Of_Orbis_Voidtaken_Horseman_Group.json` carries the same tag, so the Voidtaken Horseman, the restless graveyard's boss wave, counts as a boss too.
- Harvest Feast's files from 1.2.5 take the 2.0 stat ids: Martha's favor gain stat is `Entity/Stats/TrackGain_Harvest_Feast_Host.json` (1.2.5's `ReputationGain_Harvest_Feast_Host`, which CommonLib still reads as it), named by the four Harvest Set pieces, the Great Turkey Mantle, the set's full tier and Martha's Roast's `Harvest_Feast_Food_Favor` effect, with its tooltip key `itemTooltip.stats.TrackGain_Harvest_Feast_Host` in all nine locales; the Harvest Set's MMO Skill Tree tiers (`GearSets/SeasonsOfOrbis/Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Harvest_Set.json`) give `TrackGain_Harvesting` and `GatherYield_Harvesting` in place of `MMO_BonusXp_HARVESTING` and `MMO_Luck_HARVESTING`, at the same amounts.
- The Harvest Set's four pieces carry `"ZigLevel": ["Defense:30"]` and the Great Turkey Mantle `"ZigLevel": ["Defense:32"]`, each with `"ZigLevelFeature": ["mmoskilltree:Gear_Levels"]`, like the pack's other armour: with MMO Skill Tree's gear levels on, wearing one asks for that Defense level, and a piece a player already had keeps working for them.
