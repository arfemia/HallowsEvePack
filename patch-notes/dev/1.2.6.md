## Changed

- `NPC/Groups/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Group.json` carries `"Tags": { "ZigRank": [ "Boss" ] }`, so CommonLib's crowd-control rule (`BossRules`) counts every form of the Headless Horseman, both rides, as a boss: a talent's or a proc's stun, root, slow or push never lands on him, an ability's own does at the cast's own strength, and a taunt always does. His Head (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head_Group`) and his adds stay ordinary foes. A CommonLib without the rule ignores the tag.
- `NPC/Groups/Seasons_Of_Orbis_Voidtaken_Horseman_Group.json` carries the same tag, so the Voidtaken Horseman, the restless graveyard's boss wave, counts as a boss too.
