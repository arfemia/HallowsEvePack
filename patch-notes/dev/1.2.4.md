Seasons of Orbis 1.2.4 is a data hotfix cut from 1.2.3: two Life Essence offers at Old Jack's shop and the Regular offer's own words. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.4; `ServerVersion`, `Dependencies` and `OptionalDependencies` unchanged.

## Added

- `ShopEntries/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Offer_Grave_Candle_Life_Essence.json`: the Grave Candle for `Life_Essence` 500, no Sweets, behind the Sweets offer's own lock (`Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Friendly`), `SortOrder` 6 beside the Sweets offer's 5. No mod gate: the pack ships the `Life_Essence` wallet itself (`Currencies/MMOSkillTree/Life_Essence.json`, item-backed).
- `ShopEntries/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Offer_Burning_Grave_Candle_Life_Essence.json`: the Burning Grave Candle for `Life_Essence` 1500 and two `Ingredient_Fire_Essence` (`Cost.Items`), no Sweets, behind `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Honored`, `SortOrder` 8, before the trade by id. Both ids are the ones 1.2.2 shipped.
- Lang, in nine locales: `hallowseve.progression.lang` `shop.hallows_eve_offer_burning_grave_candle_friendly.title` and `.desc`, the Regular Burning Grave Candle offer's own words (`Hallows_Eve_Offer_Burning_Grave_Candle_Friendly.json`'s `Text`, which named the item's keys).

## Changed

- `items.lang` `Hallows_Eve_Burning_Grave_Candle.description`, in nine locales: Old Jack sells it from Regular, for less once Trusted, and trades one for ten Grave Candles.
- The candle offers' `$Comment`s name the new offers beside them.
