Seasons of Orbis 1.3.0 gives the hard ride its skeleton waves and a new creature, the Jack Archer; lets MMO Mob Scaling scale the Headless Horseman; takes the Forgotten Temple lock off the season hosts' shops, boards and conversations; rewrites the Fire Resistance Potion's tooltip in vanilla's shape; and keeps the Horseman's fight on his graveyard. Manifest: `Version` 1.3.0; `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` unchanged; `Dependencies` `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.5.0` (for `Zc_Wave_Chain`, `Zc_Wave_Rise` and a binding row's `Scale.Companion`) and `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`, unchanged. One new lang key, `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton_Archer.name` ("Jack Archer", `npcs.lang`); `dungeon.hallows_eve_graveyard_hard.goal` (`hallowseve.world.lang`) and `items.Hallows_Eve_Fire_Resistance_Potion.description` (`items.lang`) are reworded; `Hallows_Eve_Gravekeeper.hint`, which nothing read, leaves `npcs.lang`; all in nine locales.

## Shapes

- **The hard ride's waves.** The hard Horseman's `GuardWave` (`Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter_Hard.json`) is ZiggfreedCommon 2.5.0's `Zc_Wave_Chain`, in place of the four fountain guards both rides had: 5 waves of 3 `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton` (the near row, on the plaza south of the fountain, 7 blocks out across 90 degrees) and 2 `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton_Archer` (the far row, the fountain's north corners, 19 out across 37), with no toast. The first rises 8 seconds after players arrive, each next one 4 seconds after the last of a wave falls, and a wave none of which is seen within 3 seconds counts as beaten, so a spot that fails never holds the fight. The chain's Done flag `Orbis_Boss_Waves_Done` is never cleared, so the waves stay beaten through a wipe on him. The Orbis boss template gains a `FightWaves` slot, run through every phase (default `$Null`): the hard script fills it with `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Fight_Waves`, one wave of the same 3 and 2 at 80, 60, 40 and 20 percent of his current maximum health, once each per try, its `Orbis_Boss_Fight_Wave_` flags cleared on reset so the next try raises them again. The normal ride keeps its four guards and no fight waves. The hard graveyard's arrival goal names the waves, without numbers.
- **The Jack Archer.** `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton_Archer` (`Server/NPC/Roles/Hallows_Eve/`) is a Variant of `Template_Intelligent` carrying `Skeleton_Burnt_Archer`'s own `Modify`, with the look (a carved pumpkin head), the drop list (`Hallows_Eve_Drop_Jack_Skeleton`) and the nameplate changed: a shortbow skeleton that keeps its distance and shoots. Its id keeps `Skeleton`, so the game's Skeleton group holds it and MMO Skill Tree's Skeleton patterns reach it. It never spawns in the wild; it joins `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Adds_Group` (so Mob Scaling's graveyard band scales it and a reset sweeps it) and `Hallows_Eve_Event_Mobs` (Hallowed favor), and gets a kill XP row in `Mmo_Hallows_Eve` (170, twice the burnt archer's). It is a second `Targets` entry, under `EXACT`, on The Hollow Lantern's and the Harvest Moon Hunt board's kill steps and the Almanac's skeleton tally, so one kill counts once; the lines naming "Jack Skeletons" stay as they are.
- **Mob Scaling scales the Horseman.** Both Horseman binding rows (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Encounters/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter.json` and `_Hard.json`) set `Scale.Companion` `Health` and `Damage` (ZiggfreedCommon 2.5.0). With MMO Mob Scaling 1.2.4 he is scaled once per fight at the engage to his adds' level, anchored at the graveyard band's floor, so a group at the bottom of the band meets him as authored, and the scale rides the throw to his foot form. Without Mob Scaling the switch does nothing, since ZiggfreedCommon scales nothing through it.
- **The hosts follow their placements.** The storefronts `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` and `Harvest_Feast_Host` and the boards `Hallows_Eve_Daily`, `Hallows_Eve_Errands` and `Harvest_Feast_Daily` carry no `Where`: their `Season` and their host keep them in place. The two host conversations drop their three `World` conditions each (the welcome, the first greeting, the first quest's offer). Each greeting memory (`old_jack_greeted`, `feast_host_greeted`) is kept once per player in every world and forgotten with its host's meet quest (`ResetWithQuest`), so a player greeted before this version hears the first greeting once more. A server that moves a host through its placement's `Where` gets its shop, boards and questline with it; the placements and their coordinates are untouched. With no `Where`, the three boards also list in any world's board list while their season runs, behind the everyday boards (`Order` 90), as the stall already may in any shop list.
- **The Fire Resistance Potion's tooltip.** `items.Hallows_Eve_Fire_Resistance_Potion.description` takes the game's consumable shape, in the markup Update 7's client renders: "Grants:", a bullet reading "Fire Resistance: 35% less fire damage" with the name and the number in `<color is="#ffffff">`, "Duration: 3:00" in the same white, then an italic flavor line. The item's `$Comment` says the description repeats both numbers.
- **The Horseman stays in his fight.** `Orbis_Boss_Encounter_Template` adds `LeashRange` (default 41), forwarded to `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Hold`, which `Notify`s the Boss slot with `Orbis_Boss_Leash` each tick he stands past it. Both Horseman scripts set `ArenaRange` 61, `CorpseRange` 70, `ScalePlayerRange` [0, 61], `LeashRange` 57 and their volley ranges 61, so the roster, music, wipe and health-step checks reach the graveyard's entrance end but not the entrance room (its nearest point is 61.5 from the fight's entity; the outermost graves by the north wall stay just outside). The Ride and the foot template answer the notify with a `.Return` move, the pattern of vanilla's `Template_Void_Anomaly`; health is untouched.
