Seasons of Orbis 1.3.0 adds Christmas, the pack's fourth season, cut from 1.2.5 with Harvest Feast's final round merged in. Manifest: `Version` 1.3.0, `Dependencies` `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon` `>=2.5.3` (CommonLib 2.5.3's `Seasons` placement leaf, `PayTarget`, `QualifierFromTarget` with `Targets`, the `Factors` mod gate and the `Max: 0` mirror), `Description` naming Christmas from December 2 to January 10; `ServerVersion`, the RPG Stations floor and `OptionalDependencies` unchanged. Every Christmas id is new, so no saved progress moves; of the existing files, Christmas touches the three feast villagers' placements, Harvest Feast's invitation extension, the six winter festival-shelf offers and the festival shop's comment, and the files the Changed section names move onto MMO Skill Tree 2.0's ids. Against 1.2.5's `en-US`: 506 new keys (four new files, `christmas.progression.lang`, `christmas.world.lang`, `christmas.yeti.lang` and `christmas.mmo.lang`, plus Christmas blocks in `items.lang`, `npcs.lang` and `client.lang`) and no changed values, carried to all nine locales; the only keys renamed are the three favor-gain tooltip keys in `client.lang`, from `itemTooltip.stats.ReputationGain_` to `itemTooltip.stats.TrackGain_` (Changed, below).

## Changed
