Seasons of Orbis 1.2.1 fills out the two graveyard clear tables. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.1; `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` and `Dependencies` (`Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.4.0`, `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`) unchanged. No new lang keys (two values reworded in all nine locales, below), and the MMO layer is unchanged.

## Shapes

- **The normal clear.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Lootables/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Clear.json` keeps its two `Currency` rolls (30 `Hallows_Eve_Sweets`, 3 `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival_Token`) and adds two item rolls with no `Chance`, so they always pay: `Ingredient_Fire_Essence` `Count` 5 `CountMax` 8 and `Hallows_Eve_Ectoplasm` `Count` 3 `CountMax` 4, each drawn evenly in its range. Beside `Rolls` it adds a table-level `Pool` with `Rolls` `{"Base": 3}` and six weighted `Entries`: 30 `Hallows_Eve_Cursed_Geode` x2, 30 `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Uncommon` x3, 20 `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Rare` x2, 15 `Hallows_Eve_Fire_Resistance_Potion` x2, 10 `Hallows_Eve_Grave_Candle` x3 and 5 `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Epic` x1.
- **The hard clear.** `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Clear_Hard.json` raises `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` from 60 to 90, keeps 6 festival tokens, adds `Ingredient_Fire_Essence` 10 to 14 and `Hallows_Eve_Ectoplasm` 6 to 8, and a `Pool` of `{"Base": 4}` picks over the same weights, its candle entry one `Hallows_Eve_Burning_Grave_Candle`.
- **Favor.** Both tables add three `Reputation` rolls with no `Chance`, a board job's pay on the normal clear and twice it on the hard one: `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` 50 (100), `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed` 25 (50) and `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival` 30 (60). A Claim pays a reward kind through its deferred console line, and the Reputation kind's line carries `{amount}`, so the clear's `Diminish` halves the favor like the currencies. `reputation.hallows_eve_old_jack.earn.horseman` and `reputation.hallows_eve_hallowed.earn.horseman` (`hallowseve.progression.lang`) now name the clear's chest beside the Hoard.
- **The Fire Resistance Potion.** `Entity/Effects/Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Fire_Resistance.json`'s Fire `DamageResistance` `Amount` goes from 0.5 to 0.35. `ShopEntries/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Offer_Fire_Resistance_Potion.json` raises its `Cost` from 15 to 50 `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` and adds `Requires` `ziggfreedcommon:reputation_rank` `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Honored` (Trusted), the same offer with or without the MMO; the clear's pool still pays the potion to everyone. `items.Hallows_Eve_Fire_Resistance_Potion.description` (`items.lang`) is reworded in all nine locales for the new strength, without the line about Old Jack's shop.
- **How a pick and a later clear pay.** Pool picks draw with replacement, so one entry can come up more than once, and the Claim merges a repeated item into one row. Both dungeons keep `Rewards.Diminish` `[1, 0.5]` `Daily`: a later clear the same day still makes every pick, then the Claim scales each quantity by 0.5 and pays a fraction whole through `StochasticCount` (a floor, then the fraction as the chance of one more), so a single Epic Lantern Bomb or Burning Grave Candle pays half the time on a later clear.
- **What a player sees.** The clear's Claim joins the Horseman's chest (`Rewards.InChest`) and lists what the roll landed, never the pool's weights, so the tables still author no `Text`. `Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Clear.json` and `_Clear_Hard.json` still add their DEFENSE `Mmo_Xp` roll through `ContributesTo` and add no pool entry, so the pick count stays the table's.
