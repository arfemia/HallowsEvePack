Held, not yet released. The first release of Seasons of Orbis, the yearly-events pack, with its first event: the `Hallows_Eve` calendar event and everything it gates, built on ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0's calendar, calendar spawns, bonus rows, commerce, Almanac, titles, dialogue extensions, costumes and reputation, with the Carving Bench on RPG Stations 1.1.0. The pack names no MMO Skill Tree id, kind or factor; the MMO layer ships in the MMO jar's `Hallows_Eve` subtree (MMO Skill Tree 1.7.0's developer notes).

- **Plugin id** `Ziggfreed:SeasonsOfOrbis` (manifest `Group` and `Name`); `build.ps1` writes `SeasonsOfOrbis-1.0.0.zip`.
- **Requires** `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.2.0` and `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.0` (manifest `Dependencies`). RPG Stations is a hard dependency: the bench's RootInteraction names RPG Stations' interaction Type `rpg_station_use`, and a pack file naming another mod's interaction Type fails asset validation on a server without that mod, which drops the whole zip at boot.
- Ids are prefixed `Hallows_Eve_`; quests and achievements sit under `_Hallows_Eve` folders, so their ids read `hallows_eve_<name>`.

## Calendar and world

- Calendar (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/CalendarEvents/HallowsEvePack/`): `Hallows_Eve` (`Window` `10-01` to `11-03`, `FirstYear` 2026, UTC) and `Harvest_Moon` (`10-29` to `10-31`), each with `Presentation` keys and a `Herald` whose start banner is major. Content that vanishes gates on `ziggfreedcommon:feature` `Hallows_Eve_Live`; rolls and locks read `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_live`. An owner switches either event off, or moves its days, in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/calendar.json`.
- Spawns (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/CalendarSpawns/HallowsEvePack/`): the rules `Hallows_Eve_Night` (Jack Skeleton, Hollow Ghoul), `Hallows_Eve_Souls` (Lost Soul) and `Hallows_Eve_Wraiths` (Geode Wraith, the zone 1 to 3 mineshafts), written while `Hallows_Eve` runs and stilled after; `Harvest_Moon_Night_Spawns` and `Harvest_Moon_Soul_Spawns` rewrite the first two at `Priority` 10 with doubled weights. Every rule runs `DayTimeRange` 19 to 5 with `Despawn` 5 to 19 and `LightRanges.Light` 0 to 8, the mineshaft rule included: a stilled rule stops new spawns only, so the dawn `Despawn` is what clears the event's creatures after it ends.
- Creatures (`Server/NPC/Roles/Hallows_Eve/`): `Hallows_Eve_Hollow_Ghoul` (a `Template_Predator` Variant carrying the vanilla Ghoul's `Modify`), `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton` (`Template_Intelligent`, the Skeleton Fighter's), `Hallows_Eve_Lost_Soul` (`Template_Flying_Aggressive`, the Ember Spirit's without its fire immunity) and `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Wraith` (`Template_Intelligent`, the Wraith's), all at vanilla strength and all `IsMemory` false. Their models are under `Server/Models/Hallows_Eve/NPC/`: the Hollow Ghoul on the vanilla Ghoul with the derived ash-grey texture, the Lost Soul and Geode Wraith on the art's derived rigs, and the Jack Skeleton on the vanilla Skeleton; the Jack Skeleton and Lost Soul models are also the two costumes' shapes. Their drop lists are `Hallows_Eve_Drop_*` under `Server/Drops/Hallows_Eve/`.
- Old Jack: the role `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` (a `Template_Zig_QuestGiver` Variant: `Appearance`, `NameTranslationKey`, `OffHand` the Jack Shield, `DefaultOffHandSlot` 0), the model `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` (`Parent` Outlander under the Jack Helm, the Hunter's chest, legs and cape, `Light` `#ec9`) and the placement `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack_Temple` (`Where` `ForgottenTemple` with a `WorldSpawn` anchor, a top-level `Requires` on `Hallows_Eve_Live`, `Lifecycle` `KeepAlive`, `Respawn` and `Fortify`, `Interact.Dialogue` `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`). The library sweeps placements at every calendar start and end.
- Finds: the bonus rows `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Find` (`BreakBlock` `Rock_*`, 1 percent), `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Find_Ore` (`BreakBlock` `Ore_*`, 6 percent) and `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Find` (`PickupItem` `Plant_Crop_Pumpkin_Item`, 20 percent), every roll gated on `calendar_live` `Hallows_Eve` and a second roll on Harvest Moon; the cues `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Found` and `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Found`; the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Crack` (what a cracked Cursed Geode pays, open to `ContributesTo`).
- Lang: `Server/Languages/en-US/npcs.lang` (the five nameplates) and `hallowseve.world.lang` (the calendar's names and banners, the two find notices; bare keys in the pack's `hallowseve` namespace).

## Progression

- Quests (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Quests/HallowsEvePack/_Hallows_Eve/`): `hallows_eve_old_jack_1` to `_5` and `hallows_eve_old_jack_lantern` (chain `hallows_eve_old_jack`; the lantern quest repeats on a 60-day `Repeat.Cooldown` from completion, so it comes back once a year), and the dailies `hallows_eve_jack_daily_{souls,pumpkins,geodes}` (`Repeat.Reset` Daily). Each gates on a top-level `ziggfreedcommon:feature` `Hallows_Eve_Live` condition and pays one lootable (`Hallows_Eve_Chain_Pay`, `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Pay` or `Hallows_Eve_Daily_Pay`) plus two `Reputation` rewards, favor with `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` and `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`, on its own `Rewards.Claim`, outside the shared pay tables, so the quest card shows both. A quest still open when the event ends is hidden with the rest and is handed in to Old Jack when he is back.
- Commerce: the counter wallet `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` (no `OnDeath`, no `Decay`, hidden off season with its balance kept), the storefront `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` (`Order` 90, shelves `bombs`, `lights`, `gear`, `decor`) with sixteen `Hallows_Eve_Offer_*` offers named by each item's own lang keys (the Harvest Moon bundle gated on `Harvest_Moon_Live`; the Jack Shield on `gear`, locked until Honored with Old Jack by `ziggfreedcommon:reputation_rank` `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Honored`, a locked offer still showing on the shelf; the Ecto Lantern priced in sweets plus `Cost.Items` Ectoplasm; six vanilla decorations on the `decor` shelf, `Deco_Halloween_Lights`, `Deco_Halloween_Pumpkin_Scary`, `Deco_Halloween_Pumpkin_Cute`, `Halloween_Basket_Straw`, `Deco_Scarecrow` and `Halloween_Wagon`, titled by `server.items.<Id>.name` but the two pumpkins, which the game names alike and which carry `shop.hallows_eve_offer_pumpkin_scary.title` and `_cute.title`), the board `Hallows_Eve_Daily` (Daily rotation, drawn once a day at midnight UTC for the whole server, `Order` 90) with five `Slots`, each drawing only its own `Difficulty` band: `Hallows_Eve_Night` and `Hallows_Eve_Day`, open to all; a second `Hallows_Eve_Night` and a second `Hallows_Eve_Day`, each `Optional` with a slot `Requires` of its own (`ziggfreedcommon:reputation_rank` `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Friendly`, then `/Honored`, `Min` 1), so everyone sees what they post and a player below the rank sees it locked; and the `Optional` `Hallows_Eve_Skill`, which only a companion's contracts carry (MMO Skill Tree's four) and which `/zigcommerce validate` notes as `UNFILLABLE_SLOT` (INFO) on a server without one. `Grades` names the Night and Day badges (`board.hallows_eve_daily.grade.night`, `.grade.day`). Night is the world's night: the Night band holds the creature contracts, which finish only while their spawns run (world time 19 to 5). Its five `Hallows_Eve_Board_*` contracts, `Souls`, `Ghouls` and `Harvest_Moon` in the Night band and `Bombs` and `Geodes` in the Day band, each pay `Hallows_Eve_Board_Pay` and two `Reputation` rewards (`Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`, `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`) through `Rewards.Auto` (`Hallows_Eve_Board_Harvest_Moon` at `Weight` 4, gated on `Harvest_Moon_Live`). The storefront and the board carry `Where` `GameplayConfig` `ForgottenTemple`, which zc-commerce enforces: anywhere else they leave every list and open closed, and a player whose world cannot be read at that moment sees neither. A contract taken and not finished when the event ends waits for the board's return.
- Achievements: the abstract `hallows_eve_hallowed_base` (`Occurrence {Event: Hallows_Eve}`, `Listing {Seasons, Hallows_Eve}`), the keepsake `hallows_eve_lantern_keepsake` (`Listing.Feat`, `COMPLETE_QUEST hallows_eve_old_jack_lantern`, the Hollow Lantern with the year in its stack name), five `hallowed` achievements on `USE_ITEM`, `KILL_ENTITY` and `CALENDAR_ATTENDED`, the capstone `hallows_eve_the_hallowed` (`MetaSelector {Seasons, Hallows_Eve, [hallowed]}`, the `Title` reward `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`), and the Seasons of Orbis achievement `seasons_of_orbis`, shipped `Enabled: false` with no children. Each yearly file mints `<id>_<year>`. Three more under `Listing {Seasons, Hallows_Eve}` are kept across the years: no `Parent` (so no yearly copy) and no `hallowed` tag (so the capstone's selector skips them). `hallows_eve_old_friend` counts `CALENDAR_ATTENDED Hallows_Eve` to 3 (its step worded by `objective.hallows_eve.attend`); `hallows_eve_lantern_keeper` counts `COMPLETE_QUEST hallows_eve_old_jack_lantern` to 3 and pays the `Title` `Hallows_Eve_Keeper_Of_Lanterns` through `Rewards.Auto`; `hallows_eve_first_lanterns` is the same step once, gated `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_year Hallows_Eve` `Min`/`Max` 2026, and its `$Comment` says how to retire it as a feat (`Enabled: false`, `Listing.Feat`, `LegacySince`). The two lantern achievements count through ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0's own `COMPLETE_QUEST` producer, so they move without MMO Skill Tree. `hallows_eve_jacks_confidant` (Jack's Confidant) is kept across the years the same way, with no `Parent` and no `hallowed` tag: one `REPUTATION_RANK` criterion, `reach-confidant` (Target `Revered`, Qualifier `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`, worded by `objective.hallows_eve.jack_confidant`), paying the `Title` `Hallows_Eve_Jacks_Confidant` through `Rewards.Auto`. The library re-credits every held rank at each login and after each favor or equip change, so the Jack Helm's gear favor can earn it and a yearly copy would pay again for nothing; on a server with MMO Skill Tree it needs that mod's 1.7.0 vocabulary row, or the MMO's fold drops the criterion. Until a second event gives the Seasons of Orbis achievement children, the library logs one INFO `NO_CRITERIA` line for it per boot; MMO Skill Tree's fold and achievement audit skip it quietly.
- Dialogue: the conversation `hallows_eve_old_jack` (the questline and dailies as quest rows, the board, the stall, two lore lines, a Harvest Moon line on `calendar_live`, the Almanac line on the `Almanac` feature, "How do we stand?" (`opt.standing`, shown once `ziggfreedcommon:reputation` `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` reads at least 1, opening `{"Type": "Reputation", "Reputation": "Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack"}`), and the daily board line as four `Start` beats, `board_fresh_revered`, `board_fresh_honored`, `board_fresh_friendly` and `board_fresh`, highest rank first on `ziggfreedcommon:reputation_rank`, sharing one `Once {Period: Daily}` through the `OnceId` `board_fresh` and paying favor with Old Jack through their `Actions` (a `Grant` of one `Reputation` reward) whichever way the line is left, Escape aside; a rank climbed later that day does not bring it back, and the two answers are the fragment `jack_greeting`) and the extension `Hallows_Eve_Trick_Or_Treat` (one line in every ZiggfreedCommon conversation, `Once {Period: Daily}` keyed by its `OnceId`, a `Grant` of the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Trick_Or_Treat` and a `Reputation` reward, favor with the Hallowed). That table's one roll always pays sweets and carries the `Cue` `Hallows_Eve_Treat`, which plays the feedback moment `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/FeedbackMoments/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Treat.json` (a toast and a sound), and one pool draw weighs sweets and two vanilla foods against two `Costume` tricks. Each trick names the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Treats` (one roll, always sweets, never a costume) as its `FallbackLootable`, so a trick that cannot go on pays a treat instead. A vanilla merchant or an NPC with no ZiggfreedCommon conversation offers no line.
- The titles `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`, `Hallows_Eve_Keeper_Of_Lanterns` and `Hallows_Eve_Jacks_Confidant`, the Almanac page `Hallows_Eve` (keepsake `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Keepsake`; tallies `Bombs_Thrown`, `Ghouls_Felled`, `Skeletons_Knocked_Down` (`KILL_ENTITY Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton`), `Souls_Freed`, `Geodes_Cracked`, `Wraiths_Put_Down` (`KILL_ENTITY Hallows_Eve_Geode_Wraith`)), and `Server/Languages/en-US/hallowseve.progression.lang` (bare keys in the pack's `hallowseve` namespace).

## Reputation

- Two reputations, each a native `ReputationGroup` with a ZiggfreedCommon companion, on the library's seven-rank ladder: `Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack.json` (`NPCGroups` the native `NPCGroup` `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`, which holds his role, so his attitude follows each player's earned favor) and `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed.json` (`NPCGroups` an empty list: a reputation with nobody behind it, and the key stays, since a group with no `NPCGroups` key breaks every NPC that spawns), both `InitialReputationValue` 0. The NPC group `Hallows_Eve_Event_Mobs` (`Server/NPC/Groups/`) holds the four night creatures for the Hallowed's kill table; no reputation group names it, so it changes no creature's attitude.
- Companions (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Reputations/HallowsEvePack/`): `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` (Old Jack's Favor; `Order` 10, `Icon` the Jack Helm, `Gear.Stat` `Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`, `Cap` 21000, Exalted's floor, so earning stops at Jack's Own; `Ranks` names Neutral to Exalted Stranger, Regular, Trusted, Confidant and Jack's Own) and `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed` (The Hallowed; `Order` 20, `Icon` the Hollow Lantern, `Gear.Stat` `Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`, `Kills` 2 per kill in `Hallows_Eve_Event_Mobs`, no `Cap`, and `Beyond {Every 5000}` paying the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Cache`: a first roll that always pays sweets and two Rare bombs with the `Cue` `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Cache`, a toast and a sound, and a 25 percent second roll for an Epic bomb).
- Earning, each on its content's own rewards: a chain quest 250 with Old Jack and 100 with the Hallowed, the lantern quest 500 and 100, a daily 75 and 10, a board contract 50 and 25 (MMO Skill Tree's four skill contracts pay the same), the daily board line 10 with Old Jack, a trick-or-treat knock 5 with the Hallowed, and a night-creature kill 2 with the Hallowed. Amounts are the `Amount` on each `Reputation` reward.
- Gear favor: the stats `Server/Entity/Stats/Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack.json` and `Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Hallowed.json` (base `Max` 0), which an item raises `Additive` on `Target: Max`. The Jack Helm adds 100 with Old Jack (`Armor.StatModifiers`); the Jack Shield adds 100 with the Hallowed (`Utility.StatModifiers`, which the game applies in the utility slot and ZiggfreedCommon's equip bridge to a held shield, so it counts in either hand and two shields stack); the Hallowed set's tier adds 100 with the Hallowed. Their tooltip lines are new `client.lang` files, `itemTooltip.stats.<Stat>` ("Old Jack's Favor: {value}", "Hallowed Favor: {value}").
- The Factors overlay `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Factors/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack_Favor.json` names the two favor gates through `ParamNames.Keys` (`Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Friendly` to `factor.hallows_eve_old_jack.friendly`, `/Honored` to `.honored`), so the board's locked slots and the shield's offer read "Requires Regular with Old Jack" and "Requires Trusted with Old Jack"; a gate on another rank needs its own entry, or it reads the library's generic line.
- Lang: the two reputations' names and descriptions, Old Jack's rank names, the two favor gate names, the cache notice, the greeting's three rank wordings, "How do we stand?" and Jack's Confidant's achievement and title, in `hallowseve.progression.lang`.

## Items

- Lantern Bombs `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_{Common,Uncommon,Rare,Epic}`: a `Parent` chain on the game's `Weapon_Bomb` shape (`MaxStack` 10 on the root tier). Each throw is one `Serial` in the game's order: the throw animation, a one-second cooldown, the `Projectile`, a `ZigCreditProgress` (`USE_ITEM`, Qualifier `Throw`, Target the tier's id), then `ModifyInventory` `AdjustHeldItemQuantity -1`, so the last bomb of a stack finishes its own throw. Each tier flies on its own config (`Parent` `Projectile_Config_Bomb_Base`, both roots replaced, `UseModelScale`) and bursts in a `Selector` `AOECircle` whose one hit rule matches only non-players, dealing the cause `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Burst` (`Inherits: Fire`, no `DeathMessageKey`: the key does not exist on 0.6.8). Uncommon adds the game's `Burn`, Rare `Burn` and `Slow`, Epic both plus four `Fireball` shards (`Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Shard_Projectile` and its `_Right`, `_Back`, `_Left` siblings).
- The Hallowed set `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed` (`GearSets/HallowsEvePack/`): one tier `{Armor 1, Utility 1}` over `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Helm` and `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Shield`, adding `Health`, favor with the Hallowed (`Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`) and the dedicated effect `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Set` (15% `Fire` resistance, the ModelVFX `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Glow`, sparks). The helm hides `EarAccessory`, `Ear`, `Haircut`, `HeadAccessory`, `FacialHair` and `FaceAccessory`; the shield is a `Template_Weapon_Shield` child.
- `Hallows_Eve_Cursed_Geode`: Secondary runs `ModifyInventory -1`, and only its `Next` pays the table `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Crack` (`ZigGrantReward`) and credits `USE_ITEM` `Crack`. No item carries a calendar gate.
- `Hallows_Eve_Costume_Wand`: Primary and Secondary share the cooldown `Hallows_Eve_Costume_Wand` and cast a `Raycast` whose player rule runs `ZigCostume` (`Hallows_Eve_Costume_Jack` or `Hallows_Eve_Costume_Lost_Soul`, three-minute model changes, never a debuff) and credits `USE_ITEM` `Costume` only for a costume that lands.
- The lights `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Lantern`, `Hallows_Eve_Ecto_Lantern` and `Hallows_Eve_Hollow_Lantern` (the keepsake), the ingredient `Hallows_Eve_Ectoplasm` and the placeable `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Pumpkin`.
- The Carving Bench: the block `Hallows_Eve_Carving_Bench` (crafted at a Workbench from pumpkins and wood, all year; F runs the RootInteraction `Hallows_Eve_Carving_Bench_Use`) and the RPG Stations station `hallows_eve_carving_bench`, actions `Carve_Hallowed` (selected by a held Hallowed Pumpkin, locked on `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_live` `Hallows_Eve`; the Jack Shield) and `Carve` (all year; seven rows: both lanterns, the Jack Helm, Common bombs from pumpkin and boom powder, and each higher tier from three of the tier below), recipes as `Recipe.Conversions` worked from the bag, sneak+F to pick a row. Its words are in `items.lang` and `rpgstations.lang`.
- Lang: `items.lang` (every item's name and description) and `hallowseve.items.lang` (the set's notice).

## Art

- The pack's art is generated by `tools/derive_art.py` from one seed model (`tools/seed/JackoLantern01.*`) and twelve vanilla files vendored under `tools/seed/vanilla/`, byte copies from the game's `Assets.zip`, kept locally and gitignored (refresh them with `--vendor <Assets.zip>`). `python tools/derive_art.py` rewrites every file under `Common/`, `--check` compares without writing, and `python -m unittest tools/test_derive_art.py` checks node counts, integer box sizes, face rects, that a derived rig keeps every vanilla node, and that a rerun is byte-identical.
- New art: the Carving Bench (the vanilla Workbench with a lit jack-o'-lantern, a knife and pumpkins, every Workbench node kept so the Workbench's own animations play on it); the Ecto Lantern; the Hallowed Pumpkin on its own uncarved seed model, since Update 7 redraws the vanilla pumpkin; the Cursed Geode; the Costume Wand and Ectoplasm textures over the vanilla wand and essence models; the Lost Soul (the Spirit_Ember rig, hornless) and the Geode Wraith (the Wraith rig with amethyst), each with its own texture; the Hollow Ghoul's texture over the vanilla Ghoul (ash grey, pumpkin-orange eyes); a 128x128 model icon for each of the three; and a 64x64 icon for every new item.
- `python tools/check_refs.py` checks that every `.png`, `.blockymodel` and `.blockyanim` path the pack's JSON names exists in the pack or the game's `Assets.zip`; `--orphans` lists art nothing names, which in 1.0.0 is four files (two textures and two icons) kept for a later update.
