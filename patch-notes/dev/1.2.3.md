Seasons of Orbis 1.2.3 adds the Pumpkin Fiend, a Hallow's Eve night creature that the Headless Horseman's pumpkins also hatch during his fight, with two contracts for it; gives Old Jack a fourth daily, a normal-ride win over the Horseman; re-dresses the Horseman and his chase set in the artist's hand-made armour and adds a fourth piece, the Horseman's Helm; renames his Hoard for players as his Boss Kill Claim and moves all his armour to it; adds the Horseman's Steed Trophy on every server and, with Alec's Tamework, the Horseman's Reins and his steed; and gates the Reins' Almanac slot on Tamework. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.3; `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` unchanged; `Dependencies` `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.5.1` (from `>=2.5.0`, for an Almanac collection slot's own `Requires`) and `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`, unchanged; `OptionalDependencies` gains `Alechilles:Alec's Tamework! >=5.0.0 <6.0.0` beside `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree`. Lang, in nine locales: 26 new keys (`npcs.lang` `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend.name`, `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Steed.name` and `.family`; `items.lang` `.name` and `.description` of `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Helm`, `Hallows_Eve_Flaming_Horseman_Helm` and `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Steed_Trophy`, and `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Reins.name`, `.description` and `.command_calm`; `hallowseve.world.lang` `notify.hallows_eve_horseman_pumpkins` and `reward.hallows_eve_horsemans_steed`; `hallowseve.progression.lang` `objective.hallows_eve.squash_fiends`, the two fiend contracts' `.title` and `.flavor`, `almanac.hallows_eve.source.steed_trophy` and `.reins`, `shop.hallows_eve_offer_sell_horsemans_reins.title` and `.desc`, `quest.hallows_eve_jack_daily_horseman.title` and `.flavor`, and `dialogue.hallows_eve_old_jack.daily_offer.opt.horseman`); 26 reworded for the Boss Kill name, the armour's new look and the fiends as droppers (`claim.hallows_eve_horseman_chase` and `_hard`; `almanac.hallows_eve.hoard.line` and the sources `grave_candle`, `bomb_rare`, `hoard`, `hoard_hard`, `hoard_normal`, `eternal_candle`, `burning_helm_trade`, `flaming_shield_trade` and `pumpkin`; `reputation.hallows_eve_old_jack.earn.horseman` and `reputation.hallows_eve_hallowed.earn.horseman`; the two armour achievements' `.desc`; `dialogue.hallows_eve_old_jack.horseman_beaten.text`; `gearset.hallows_eve_flaming_hallowed.tier_armor`; the six chase pieces', the Grave Candle's and the Hallowed Pumpkin's `.description`); `almanac.hallows_eve.source.horseman_daily` removed.

## Shapes

- **The Pumpkin Fiend.** `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend` (`Server/NPC/Roles/Hallows_Eve/`) is a `Template_Intelligent` Variant carrying `Skeleton_Fighter`'s `Modify` (health 30, a 6-damage hit), attacking through `Root_Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend_Attack` (the Razorleaf's three sapling spear moves) with `Weapon_Spear_Leaf`, on the model asset `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend` (`Parent` `Kweebec_Sapling`; the model's front-right leg chain is named `R-Thigh`, `R-Calf`, `R-Foot`, so it walks with the sapling's right-leg animation). It drops `Hallows_Eve_Drop_Pumpkin_Fiend` (a Hallowed Pumpkin one kill in ten, no Sweets), joins `Hallows_Eve_Event_Mobs` (Hallowed favor) and gets a kill XP row in `Mmo_Hallows_Eve` (110). It spawns only through the calendar rule `Hallows_Eve_Fiends` (`CalendarSpawns/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Fiend_Spawns.json`: `Env_Zone1_Autumn`, nights, `Light` 0 to 8, weight 6, flocks of 2 to 3), which `Harvest_Moon_Fiend_Spawns.json` rewrites at a higher `Priority`.
- **His pumpkins hatch fiends.** `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend_Hatchling` is a `Template_Intelligent` role of its own carrying the wild fiend's look and settings (sight range 15, as the Jack Skeleton adds), never a Variant of it, at `MaxHealth` 60, twice the wild fiend's. The hard ride raises `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend_Hatchling_HM` instead, the same role at `MaxHealth` 130 (the Horseman's own hard ratio, 2376 / 1100) with its own `FlockArray`. The two are the only fiends in `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Adds_Group`, so Mob Scaling's graveyard band scales them and a reset sweeps them; they count wherever the wild fiend does (`Hallows_Eve_Event_Mobs`, both fiend contracts' `Targets`), and `Mmo_Hallows_Eve` pays 160 and 240. The normal ride gains `FightWaves` (`Macro_Orbis_Boss_Fight_Waves`): two near and one far Hatchling 7 blocks out on the plaza south of the fountain, at 75, 35 and 10 percent of his maximum health. The hard ride's `FightWaves` keep their far row of two `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton_Archer` and trade the near row's three `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton` for five Hatchlings; its `GuardWave` chain is unchanged. Each hatching on either ride fires the FeedbackMoment `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Pumpkins` (`notify.hallows_eve_horseman_pumpkins`).
- **Two Pumpkin Fiend contracts.** `Bounties/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Board_Fiends.json` ("Weeding the Patch", a night posting on `Hallows_Eve_Daily` through `Hallows_Eve_Contract_Base`, 10 kills) and `Hallows_Eve_Errand_Fiends.json` ("Bad Seeds", `Hallows_Eve_Errands` in the `Hallows_Eve_Night` band, 5 kills), each a `KILL_ENTITY` step on `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend` with `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend_Hatchling` and `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Fiend_Hatchling_HM` as `Targets` under `EXACT`, the plain pumpkin as their icon; their pay is Rattling Bones' and Loose Bones', Defense `Mmo_Xp` 1500 and 750 behind the MMO's mod gate.
- **Old Jack's fourth daily.** `Quests/HallowsEvePack/_Hallows_Eve/Jack_Daily_Horseman.json` (`hallows_eve_jack_daily_horseman`, "Back in the Saddle"): a calendar daily (`Repeat.Reset` `Daily`, `CooldownFrom` `Complete`) on `Hallows_Eve_Quest_Base` that `Requires` The Rider. Its one `ENCOUNTER_DEFEATED` step on `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter` is `EXACT`, so the hard ride's script does not count, worded by `objective.hallows_eve.bring_down_horseman`, with the base's report-back step to Old Jack. It pays `Hallows_Eve_Daily_Pay`, Old Jack's Favor 150, the Hallowed 10, Festival Favor 40 and Defense `Mmo_Xp` 1500 behind the MMO's mod gate. Old Jack offers it as a fourth `daily_offer` answer, with `horseman` as its Active screen, and each of the four answers carries a `Requires` condition naming its daily's own prerequisite.
- **The Burning Grave Candle at Regular.** `ShopEntries/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve_Offer_Burning_Grave_Candle_Friendly.json` sells it at `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack/Friendly` for `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` 150 and `Life_Essence` 500 in one `Cost`, at SortOrder 7 beside the Trusted Sweets offer. The candle now has three offers: this one, and at Trusted 75 Sweets and the trade of ten Grave Candles; `Hallows_Eve_Offer_Burning_Grave_Candle_Life_Essence` (1000 Life Essence at Trusted) is removed.
- **The Life Essence wallet ships here.** `Currencies/MMOSkillTree/Life_Essence.json` is the same item-backed wallet the Bounty pack and MMO Skill Tree ship, at the same path and under the same id (`Backing` `Ingredient_Life_Essence`, every field identical, so whichever copy loads last reads the same), so the Regular Burning Grave Candle offer prices in Life Essence on any server. Nothing saved moves: the balance is the item a player carries.
- **The Grave Candle's price.** `Hallows_Eve_Offer_Grave_Candle` costs `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` 18 (from 25), and `Hallows_Eve_Offer_Grave_Candle_Life_Essence` (250 Life Essence, behind the bounty pack's gate) is removed, so the candle has one offer.
- **The Horseman's own look.** Both Horseman models are re-dressed in the hand-made armour (`Common/Items/Hallows_Eve/Horseman_Armor/`): the mounted one is `Parent` `Horse` with the black texture, the barding, the rotted tail, `RandomAttachmentSets {}` and the rider's armour derived from the same files; the neck particle is `Fire_Green` (`Color` `#d1ff7f`) on the collar's `Fire` node, with a `#8c4` light. The hard ride's `_HM` roles name the gold models `_HM` and `_Mounted_HM`. The six chase items take the new models with generated icons, the Flaming pieces gold. Both Breastplates wear the artist's player export of the chests (`Common/Items/Hallows_Eve/Horseman_Armor/Breastplates/`, the plain chest and the collar chest with their `Chest2` group about 13 px lower, so the collar sits as a mantle and a player's head is clear); the Horseman keeps `Horseman_Armor/Headless_Horseman_Collar_Chest.blockymodel`; the unused plain draft is dropped; `tools/derive_art.py` reads both for the two icons and builds worn icon rigs only for the armour an icon draws.
- **The Horseman's Helm.** New `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Helm` (Rare, level 20) and `Hallows_Eve_Flaming_Horseman_Helm` (Epic, level 30), hiding hair and face like the Jack Helm, join both gear sets (`Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`, and the flaming one `Hallows_Eve_Flaming_Hallowed` too), the Almanac and the Boss Kill tables.
- **The Boss Kill Claim.** The Hoard keeps every id (`Rewards/Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase` and `_Hard`, the Lootables `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase` and `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase_Hard`), so a Claim saved before this version opens under the new words: `claim.hallows_eve_horseman_chase` reads "Headless Horseman: Boss Kill" and `_hard` adds "(Hard)". The three plain pieces that paid from `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily` and `_Daily_Hard` (Breastplate, Gauntlets, Greaves) move to `_Chase` and `_Chase_Hard` beside the new plain helm, at 15 and 25. The Burning Jack Helm and the Flaming Jack Shield roll at 5 in `_Chase` and 15 in `_Chase_Hard`; the Horseman's four flaming pieces pay only from `_Chase_Hard`, at 15 (`_Gauntlets` and `_Greaves` leave `_Chase`, where they rolled at 5), and their Almanac slots read `almanac.hallows_eve.source.hoard_hard`. The Boss Kill is not share-scaled, so each group member rolls the full chance. `almanac.hallows_eve.source.horseman_daily` goes, in all nine locales.
- **The Steed Trophy.** `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Steed_Trophy`, a model block (the horse, barding and tail on one atlas at 1/8 scale, on a plinth; Epic, stone, breaks by hand, stacks to 25), rolls at 2 in `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase` (`HideInPreview`) and 6 in `_Chase_Hard`, on every server.
- **With Alec's Tamework: the Reins and his steed.** Optional Tamework data, inert without it: the roster `SeasonsOfOrbis:Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Reins` (`Server/Tamework/BondedCompanions/Rosters/`), a command-item config and an interaction config with the owner-checked Mount rule (`PlayerIsOwner`), and two Patchwork patches (`Server/Patchwork/Patches/Hallows_Eve/`) gated `When` `ModInstalled`. The zc Lootables `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Reins_Chase` and `_Hard`, gated on `hytale:mod_installed` for Tamework, contribute the Reins to the Boss Kill at `Chance` 0.04 and 5 with a console `Command` reward, `tw bonded grant {uuid} SeasonsOfOrbis:Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Reins Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Steed`, run on Collect; a second drop pays only the Reins, since the grant refuses a steed already bonded. The offer `Hallows_Eve_Offer_Sell_Horsemans_Reins` (same gate) buys a spare Reins back for 100 Sweets. The role `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Steed` is a Variant of `Hallows_Eve_Template_Horsemans_Steed`, a byte copy of 0.7.2's `Template_Livestock`, since a Variant cannot carry the owner patch; it never spawns, brings no herd and drops nothing. The item `Hallows_Eve_Horsemans_Reins` sits on the vanilla strap model in black (Legendary, kept on death).
- **The Reins' Almanac slot.** The Reins slot in `Almanac/HallowsEvePack/Hallows_Eve.json` writes its own `Requires` on `Alechilles:Alec's Tamework!` (`Min` 1), so a server without Tamework leaves it off the page; the Steed Trophy's slot shows everywhere. It needs CommonLib 2.5.1: on 2.5.0 the key logs as unused and the slot shows.
- **Generated art.** `tools/derive_art.py` gains `graft()` (the rider's armour), the chase set's eight icons, the trophy block, the Reins' texture and the steed's portrait, and vendors `Characters/Player.blockymodel` and three more vanilla files: rerun `--vendor <Assets.zip>` before running the tool again.
