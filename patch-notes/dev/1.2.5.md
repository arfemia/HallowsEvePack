Seasons of Orbis 1.2.5 is a data release cut from 1.2.4 for Harvest Feast's first live run (November 20 to December 6, 2026): a conversation fix, found auditing the feast forced on with and without MMO Skill Tree on Hytale 0.7.4, the feast's own step lines and an Objective Book in Martha's welcome, and a content pass on the feast: its own currency, an unlimited turkey grind, favor past the top rank, a gift at each rank, a gear set, three dishes of Martha's own in place of the game's recipes, four board jobs, villager tips and plainer words throughout, a golden plate for the yearly keepsake, the feast's achievements reworked as one set, the feast's villagers on the player body with a real face, and Martha's table moved off the temple's arrival path. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.5 and the `Description`'s Harvest Feast dates; `ServerVersion`, `Dependencies` and `OptionalDependencies` unchanged. No quest, step, objective key or criterion key changes, so saved progress stands; every rename is a lang value. The three removed recipe offers' saved purchase counts go unused. Against 1.2.4's `en-US`: 259 new keys, 79 changed values and 20 keys removed (`dialogue.harvest_feast_host.active_generic.text`, the six `shop.harvest_feast_offer_recipe_{apple_pie,meat_pie,caesar_salad}` keys, and the filler screens cut from the conversations: `dialogue.harvest_feast_host.{who_are_you,about_feast}.text` and `.opt.{who,feast}`, and `dialogue.harvest_feast_{miller,weaver,shepherd}` `mill`/`cloth`/`sheep` `.text`, `.opt.{mill,cloth,sheep}` and `.opt.back`), carried to all nine locales.
