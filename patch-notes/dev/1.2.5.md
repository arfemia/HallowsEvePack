A hotfix cut from 1.2.4 whose content loads on Hytale Update 8 (0.8.0-pre.5 and later 0.8 builds): `ServerVersion` is `>=0.8.0-pre.0 <0.9.0`. The manifest's dependency floors stay (`>=2.5.1`, `>=1.1.1`), since the pack names no newer API; on Update 8 only CommonLib 2.5.3 and RPG Stations 1.1.4 load.

- **The Spyglass.** The Hallow's Eve Spyglass offer and its Almanac collection slot name `Tool_Spyglass_Scrap`, the id Update 8 gives the game's spyglass. It still names itself with `server.items.Tool_Spyglass.name`, so the offer's title is unchanged.
- **Knockback.** The Lantern Bomb and Orbis boss bursts' knockbacks move from Point, deprecated for removal, to Radial with the same force. The Pumpkin Fiend's and the two Pumpkin Fiend Hatchlings' melee knockbacks move from Directional, deprecated for removal, to Angled with force 0 and their lift kept, the same feel as on Update 7.
- **The Horseman's Steed.** Its template is copied again from Update 8's `Template_Livestock` (its Mount action seats the rider on the model's mount node), and its Variant drops `MountAnchorX`, `MountAnchorY` and `MountAnchorZ`, which the template no longer declares.
