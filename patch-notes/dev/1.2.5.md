Seasons of Orbis 1.2.5 is a data hotfix cut from 1.2.4 for Harvest Feast's first live run (November 20 to December 1, 2026): a conversation fix, found auditing the feast forced on with and without MMO Skill Tree on Hytale 0.7.4, plus the feast's own step lines and an Objective Book in Martha's welcome. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.5 and the `Description`'s Harvest Feast dates; `ServerVersion`, `Dependencies` and `OptionalDependencies` unchanged. Three new lang keys, in all nine locales; none changed or removed.

## Fixed

- `Dialogues/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Host.json`: the `Then` line `feast_done` reads `harvest_feast_the_feast` in `States` `COMPLETED` or `ON_COOLDOWN`, where it read `State` `COMPLETED` alone. A `Repeat.PerRun` quest reads `ON_COOLDOWN` once this run has paid it (it comes back next year), so Martha's after-feast line never showed and she fell back to her menu line. Old Jack's `lantern_lit` reads both states the same way.
- Five feast steps name their own `TextKey`, as every one of Old Jack's steps does: `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Host_2.json` and `Daily_Turkeys.json` (`bring_down_turkeys`), `The_Feast.json` (`bring_carrots`), and `Bounties/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Board_Turkeys.json` and `Harvest_Feast_Board_Pies.json`. CommonLib's default kind lines name the target by its singular name, so the tracker read "Defeat 5 Wild Turkey", "Hand in 2 Pumpkin Pie" and "Hand in 10 Carrot"; it now reads "Bring down 5 Wild Turkeys", "Bring 2 Pumpkin Pies" and "Bring 10 Carrots". New keys in `harvestfeast.progression.lang`, all nine locales: `objective.harvest_feast.bring_down_turkeys`, `objective.harvest_feast.bring_pies` and `objective.harvest_feast.bring_carrots`, `{0}` the step's amount. The other feast steps keep the default lines, which read right (wheat and cooked wildmeat are counted in the plural already, and the pie and roast vegetable steps ask for one).
- `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Martha_Meet.json`: Martha's welcome pays CommonLib's Objective Book, the same `Item` row as Old Jack's welcome (`Ziggfreed_Objective_Book`, `Count` 1), so a player whose first season is Harvest Feast has the book that opens their quests and achievements. It pays on Collect with the rest of the welcome. A player who already holds a book gets a second, as at Old Jack's.

## Changed

- The README and the store page describe Harvest Feast now that it runs: Martha's quests, dailies, board, shop, villagers, Wild Turkeys, favor and achievements, its MMO Skill Tree XP, and the Festival Shelf at each host's shop. Both say Martha's welcome gives an Objective Book, as Old Jack's does.
