Seasons of Orbis 1.2.5 is a data release cut from 1.2.4 for Harvest Feast's first live run (November 20 to December 1, 2026): a conversation fix, found auditing the feast forced on with and without MMO Skill Tree on Hytale 0.7.4, the feast's own step lines and an Objective Book in Martha's welcome, and a content pass on the feast: its own currency, an unlimited turkey grind, favor past the top rank, a gift at each rank, a gear set, three dishes of Martha's own in place of the game's recipes, four board jobs, villager tips and plainer words throughout, the feast's villagers on the player body with a real face, and Martha's table moved off the temple's arrival path. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.5 and the `Description`'s Harvest Feast dates; `ServerVersion`, `Dependencies` and `OptionalDependencies` unchanged. No quest, step, objective key or criterion key changes, so saved progress stands; every rename is a lang value. The three removed recipe offers' saved purchase counts go unused. Against 1.2.4's `en-US`: 102 new keys, 53 changed values and 7 keys removed (`dialogue.harvest_feast_host.active_generic.text` and the six `shop.harvest_feast_offer_recipe_{apple_pie,meat_pie,caesar_salad}` keys), carried to all nine locales.

Direct `Currency` rows: `Martha_Meet` 10, each `Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Daily_*` skill job 10, `Extra_Turkeys` 6. - `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Extra_Turkeys.json` (`harvest_feast_extra_turkeys`): `KILL_ENTITY` Harvest_Feast_Turkey 10, `Repeat: {}` (repeatable with no cooldown, reset or limit), `Requires` harvest_feast_host_2, `MainLine` false. ## Changed

- Martha's shop no longer sells the game's recipes: `Harvest_Feast_Offer_Recipe_{Apple_Pie,Meat_Pie,Caesar_Salad}.json` and their six `shop.*` keys are gone, replaced by the three dish recipes above under new offer ids, so a 1.2.4 purchase count never blocks the new pages. The Almanac's collection swaps the three game recipe pages for the three dishes and their pages (the Roast and its page `Hidden`).
- The feast's four villagers' models (`Models/Harvest_Feast/NPC/Harvest_Feast_{Host,Miller,Weaver,Shepherd}.json`) are `Parent` Player on `Characters/Player.blockymodel` with a Skin gradient body, a detached face, mouth and ears on the same tone (02, 08, 03, 10), `Eyes_Gradient` eyes, eyebrows and a haircut, the shape of the temple's own `Temple_Mithril_Guard`, where they were `Parent` Outlander with the Outlander's grey skin and eyes. Under a hat the haircut is the creator's generic fallback (`HaircutFallbacks`); Elsie's Bun sits on GenericShort (`RequiresGenericHaircut`). Their clothes are unchanged.
- The game's own grown turkey counts beside the feast's Wild Turkey (R1158): `Host_2.json`, `Daily_Turkeys.json`, `Extra_Turkeys.json`, `Harvest_Feast_Board_Turkeys.json`, `Turkey_Chaser.json`'s criterion and the Almanac's `Turkeys_Brought_Down` tally add `"Targets": [ "Turkey" ]` beside `Target` Harvest_Feast_Turkey under the one `MatchMode` EXACT (`Turkey_Chick` and the `Tamed_*` roles stay out). Martha's reputation adds a `Kills` row: the new group `NPC/Groups/Harvest_Feast_Game_Turkeys.json` (`IncludeRoles` Turkey), `Amount` 2 against the Wild Turkey's 4, `Season` Harvest_Feast. `objective.harvest_feast.bring_down_turkeys`, `achievement.harvest_feast_turkey_chaser.desc`, `almanac.harvest_feast.turkeys_brought_down` and `reputation.harvest_feast_host.earn.turkeys` name turkeys in general.
- Martha's `feast_ready` and `feast_done` lines say her story is done till next year's feast and point to the daily work, the turkeys and her shop.
- Martha's role (`NPC/Roles/Passive/Harvest_Feast_Host.json`) holds the game's `Furniture_Lumberjack_Lantern` in her off hand (`OffHand`, `DefaultOffHandSlot` 0).
- `Host_2.json` (Turkey Hunt) pays one `Ingredient_Dough` and one `Ingredient_Spices` beside the pumpkin pie recipe.
- Martha's own offers are priced in Harvest Coins: Turkey Pie Recipe 40, Harvest Salad Recipe 60, Martha's Roast Recipe 100, Popcorn 6, Corn Seeds 8, Autumn Leaves 6. The festival shelf stays on tokens.
- Plain Harvest Coin rows on the achievements that paid only MMO rows: `Guest.json` 10, `Turkey_Chaser.json` 25, `Full_Baskets.json` 25, `Full_Table.json` 100.
- Martha's conversation: new screens `turkeys_brief` (the Active screen of Turkey Hunt, the turkey daily and Extra Turkeys), `roast_brief` (the roast daily's) and `extra_turkeys_ready`; the wheat daily's Active screen is `wheat_brief`. `active_generic` is gone. Quest and job flavors say what to do and where.

## Fixed

- `NpcPlacements/Harvest_Feast_Host_Temple.json`: `Anchor.WorldSpawn.Offset` is X -6, Z -8, where it was X -4, Z -2. The temple's arrival spot faces negative X down a three-wide Rock_Chalk_Brick path (Z offset -1.5 to +1.5), and the props at +2 and +3 Z from Martha put the table on its centre line and the bench on its south lane; read from the vanilla `Instances/Forgotten_Temple` chunks. Martha, her table and bench now stand on the open marble on the hall's north side, behind the statue and bench row at Z offset -3, facing the path.
- `Dialogues/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Host.json`: the `Then` line `feast_done` reads `harvest_feast_the_feast` in `States` `COMPLETED` or `ON_COOLDOWN`, where it read `State` `COMPLETED` alone. A `Repeat.PerRun` quest reads `ON_COOLDOWN` once this run has paid it (it comes back next year), so Martha's after-feast line never showed and she fell back to her menu line. Old Jack's `lantern_lit` reads both states the same way.
- Seven feast steps name their own `TextKey`, as every one of Old Jack's steps does: `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Host_2.json` and `Daily_Turkeys.json` (`bring_down_turkeys`), all three of `The_Feast.json`'s (`bring_pie`, `bring_roast`, `bring_carrots`), and `Bounties/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Board_Turkeys.json` and `Harvest_Feast_Board_Pies.json` (`bring_pies`). CommonLib's default kind lines name the target by its singular name, so the tracker read "Defeat 5 Wild Turkey", "Hand in 2 Pumpkin Pie" and "Hand in 10 Carrot"; it now reads "Hunt 5 Wild Turkeys", "Bring 2 Pumpkin Pies" and "Bring 10 Carrots". The Feast shows its three steps together, so its pie and roast steps take lines in the same voice: "Bring a Pumpkin Pie" and "Bring 4 Cooked Wildmeat". Keys in `harvestfeast.progression.lang`: `objective.harvest_feast.bring_down_turkeys`, `objective.harvest_feast.bring_pies`, `objective.harvest_feast.bring_carrots` and `objective.harvest_feast.bring_roast`, `{0}` the step's amount, and `objective.harvest_feast.bring_pie`, which takes no argument (the step asks for one pie), like Old Jack's `objective.hallows_eve.bring_pumpkin`. The four new board jobs' steps name keys of their own the same way.
- `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Martha_Meet.json`: Martha's welcome pays CommonLib's Objective Book, the same `Item` row as Old Jack's welcome (`Ziggfreed_Objective_Book`, `Count` 1), so a player whose first season is Harvest Feast has the book that opens their quests and achievements. It pays on Collect with the rest of the welcome. A player who already holds one gets a second, as at Old Jack's.
