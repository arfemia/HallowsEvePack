Seasons of Orbis 1.2.5 is a data release cut from 1.2.4 for Harvest Feast's first live run (November 20 to December 1, 2026): a conversation fix, found auditing the feast forced on with and without MMO Skill Tree on Hytale 0.7.4, the feast's own step lines and an Objective Book in Martha's welcome, and a content pass on the feast: its own currency, an unlimited turkey grind, favor past the top rank, a gift at each rank, a gear set, four board jobs, villager tips and plainer words throughout. Manifest: `Version` 1.2.5 and the `Description`'s Harvest Feast dates; `ServerVersion`, `Dependencies` and `OptionalDependencies` unchanged. No id, objective key or criterion key changes, so saved progress stands; every rename is a lang value. In `en-US`: 74 new keys, 54 changed values and 1 key removed (`dialogue.harvest_feast_host.active_generic.text`), carried to all nine locales.

## Added

- `Currencies/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Coins.json`: Harvest Coins, a counter wallet shaped like Hallow Sweets (`Season` Harvest_Feast, `Cap` 0, `Icon` Plant_Crop_Corn_Item, `Color` #d9a441). Each pay table's first, always-paying roll now pays it beside the festival token: `Harvest_Feast_Chain_Pay` 15, `Daily_Pay` 8, `Board_Pay` 10, `Feast_Pay` 40, `Invitations_Pay` 25, `Shared_Meal_Pay` 4. Direct `Currency` rows: `Martha_Meet` 10, each `Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Daily_*` skill job 10, `Extra_Turkeys` 6. Keys `currency.harvest_feast_coins.name`, `.amount` and `.description` in `harvestfeast.progression.lang`.
- `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Extra_Turkeys.json` (`harvest_feast_extra_turkeys`): `KILL_ENTITY` Harvest_Feast_Turkey 10, `Repeat: {}` (repeatable with no cooldown, reset or limit), `Requires` harvest_feast_host_2, `MainLine` false. Pays 6 Harvest Coins, 40 favor and, behind the row gate, Defense `Mmo_Xp` 750. Martha's conversation row names only `Active` (`turkeys_brief`) and `Ready` (`extra_turkeys_ready`), so the start ladder never opens on it as an offer; it is offered from her quest list.
- Martha's reputation (`Reputations/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Host.json`): `Cap` removed; `Kills` 4 favor per kill of a role in the new NPC group `NPC/Groups/Harvest_Feast_Turkeys.json` (`IncludeRoles` Harvest_Feast_Turkey); `Beyond` `Every` 5000 paying the new `Lootables/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Feast_Basket.json` (2 Festival Tokens, 40 Harvest Coins, 2 Pumpkin Pies, 2 Meat Pies; a 25% roll of 4 Bundles of Hay), cued by `FeedbackMoments/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Feast_Basket.json`; `Earn` with five lines (`reputation.harvest_feast_host.earn.*`).
- Rank gifts, three new achievements with no `Parent`, kept for good: `_Harvest_Feast/Marthas_Neighbor.json` (Friendly: 30 Harvest Coins, 8 `Plant_Hay_Bundle`), `Marthas_Friend.json` (Honored: 60 Harvest Coins, a `Deco_Scarecrow`, 16 `Plant_Leaves_Autumn`) and `Marthas_Family.json` (Revered: 120 Harvest Coins, 4 `Food_Pie_Pumpkin`, 4 `Food_Pie_Meat`), each with an ALL `Mmo_Boost_Token` behind the row gate (1.25 for 30 and 60 minutes, 1.5 for 60). `Head_Of_The_Table.json` keeps its `Auto` title and gains a `Claim` of 250 Harvest Coins and an ALL 1.5 token for 120 minutes.
- The Harvest Set. Items `Item/Items/Harvest_Feast/Harvest_Feast_Harvest_{Hat,Tunic,Gloves,Trousers}.json`: full bodies with no `Parent` (so no inherited recipe), on the game's wool model, texture, icon and armor numbers, `Quality` Uncommon, `ItemLevel` 30, `ISS_Armor_Cloth`; each `Armor.StatModifiers` adds `Stamina` (Hat 2, Tunic 3, Gloves 2, Trousers 3) and the new stat `Entity/Stats/ReputationGain_Harvest_Feast_Host.json` +5 (`Target` Max). Set `GearSets/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Harvest_Set.json`: `Armor` 2 adds Health 5, `Armor` 4 adds Health 10 and the favor stat +10. Its MMO layer `Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Harvest_Set.json` (`Joins`, top-level mod gate) joins the same two tiers with `MMO_BonusXp_HARVESTING` +10, then +15 with `MMO_Luck_HARVESTING` +10. Achievement `_Harvest_Feast/Harvest_Set.json` (`GEAR_SET_TIER`, `Amount` 4), 25 points, no `Parent`, pays nothing. Tooltip key `itemTooltip.stats.ReputationGain_Harvest_Feast_Host` in `client.lang`; item names and descriptions in `items.lang`.
- Martha's shop (`Shops/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Host.json`): `Currencies` Harvest_Feast_Coins and a `clothes` shelf between `kitchen` and `decor`. New offers `Harvest_Feast_Offer_Harvest_{Gloves,Hat,Trousers,Tunic}.json` at 120, 160, 200 and 240 coins, locked on Friendly, Honored, Revered and Exalted; `Harvest_Feast_Offer_Hay_Bundle.json` (4 for 10) and `Harvest_Feast_Offer_Scarecrow.json` (30).
- Four board jobs: `Bounties/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Board_Pumpkins.json` (Field, `PICKUP_ITEM` 10 pumpkins) and `_Carrots.json` (Field, 15 carrots), each with Harvesting `Mmo_Xp` 1500; `_Vegetables.json` (Kitchen, `TURN_IN` 5 `Food_Vegetable_Cooked`) and `_Spices.json` (Kitchen, 3 `Ingredient_Spices`), each with Crafting `Mmo_Xp` 1500. Each band has four contracts for its two slots.
- One tip screen and option per villager: `Harvest_Feast_Miller.json` `dough`, `Harvest_Feast_Weaver.json` `spice`, `Harvest_Feast_Shepherd.json` `turkeys`, each keeping its `goodbye` group.
- The Almanac page's collection adds the four set pieces (`source.stall_favor`), `Plant_Hay_Bundle` and `Deco_Scarecrow` (new `almanac.harvest_feast.source.stall_gift`); the hero is unchanged.

## Changed

- `Host_2.json` (Turkey Hunt) pays one `Ingredient_Dough` and one `Ingredient_Spices` beside the pumpkin pie recipe.
- Martha's own offers are priced in Harvest Coins: Apple Pie Recipe 40, Meat Pie Recipe 60, Caesar Salad Recipe 100, Popcorn 6, Corn Seeds 8, Autumn Leaves 6. The festival shelf stays on tokens.
- Plain Harvest Coin rows on the achievements that paid only MMO rows: `Guest.json` 10, `Turkey_Chaser.json` 25, `Full_Baskets.json` 25, `Full_Table.json` 100.
- Martha's conversation: new screens `turkeys_brief` (the Active screen of Turkey Hunt, the turkey daily and Extra Turkeys), `roast_brief` (the roast daily's) and `extra_turkeys_ready`; the wheat daily's Active screen is `wheat_brief`. `active_generic` is gone. Her lines say where each thing is (the Emerald Wilds' plains, forests and autumn woods, a Campfire, a Chef's Stove) and point to the villagers; the plain board line names the currency through `<msg key="harvestfeast.progression.currency.harvest_feast_coins.name"/>`.
- Plain names, lang values only: the reputation `Martha's Favor`, rank `Honored` `Friend`; quests `Meet Martha`, `Wheat Harvest`, `Turkey Hunt`, `Your First Pie`, `The Feast`, `Shared Meal`, `Daily Turkeys`, `Daily Wheat`, `Daily Roast`; board jobs `Turkey Job`, `Wheat Job`, `Pie Job`, `Cooking Job`; skill jobs `Harvesting Job`, `Crafting Job`, `Archery Job`, `Woodcutting Job`; `Feast Guest`; the turkey step `Hunt {0} Wild Turkeys`. Quest and job flavors say what to do and where.

## Fixed

- `Dialogues/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Host.json`: the `Then` line `feast_done` reads `harvest_feast_the_feast` in `States` `COMPLETED` or `ON_COOLDOWN`, where it read `State` `COMPLETED` alone. A `Repeat.PerRun` quest reads `ON_COOLDOWN` once this run has paid it (it comes back next year), so Martha's after-feast line never showed and she fell back to her menu line. Old Jack's `lantern_lit` reads both states the same way.
- Seven feast steps name their own `TextKey`, as every one of Old Jack's steps does: `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Host_2.json` and `Daily_Turkeys.json` (`bring_down_turkeys`), all three of `The_Feast.json`'s (`bring_pie`, `bring_roast`, `bring_carrots`), and `Bounties/SeasonsOfOrbis/Harvest_Feast_Board_Turkeys.json` and `Harvest_Feast_Board_Pies.json` (`bring_pies`). CommonLib's default kind lines name the target by its singular name, so the tracker read "Defeat 5 Wild Turkey", "Hand in 2 Pumpkin Pie" and "Hand in 10 Carrot"; it now reads "Hunt 5 Wild Turkeys", "Bring 2 Pumpkin Pies" and "Bring 10 Carrots". The Feast shows its three steps together, so its pie and roast steps take lines in the same voice: "Bring a Pumpkin Pie" and "Bring 4 Cooked Wildmeat". Keys in `harvestfeast.progression.lang`: `objective.harvest_feast.bring_down_turkeys`, `objective.harvest_feast.bring_pies`, `objective.harvest_feast.bring_carrots` and `objective.harvest_feast.bring_roast`, `{0}` the step's amount, and `objective.harvest_feast.bring_pie`, which takes no argument (the step asks for one pie), like Old Jack's `objective.hallows_eve.bring_pumpkin`. The four new board jobs' steps name keys of their own the same way.
- `Quests/SeasonsOfOrbis/_Harvest_Feast/Martha_Meet.json`: Martha's welcome pays CommonLib's Objective Book, the same `Item` row as Old Jack's welcome (`Ziggfreed_Objective_Book`, `Count` 1), so a player whose first season is Harvest Feast has the book that opens their quests and achievements. It pays on Collect with the rest of the welcome. A player who already holds one gets a second, as at Old Jack's.
