Seasons of Orbis 1.1.0 adds a second calendar event, `Harvest_Feast`, the layer every season shares (Festival Favor, the festival token, the festival shelf and the Seasons of Orbis ladder) and the one-day `Hytale_Anniversary`, on ZiggfreedCommon 2.3.0 and RPG Stations 1.1.1 for Update 7 (manifest `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0`, `Dependencies` `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.3.0` and `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`). Every id Hallow's Eve shipped is unchanged. New words are in `seasonsoforbis.lang` (the shared layer and the anniversary) and `harvestfeast.progression.lang`, `harvestfeast.world.lang` and `harvestfeast.mmo.lang`, bare keys. The shapes below are the ones a pack author can copy.

## Shapes

- **The `Season` leaf on Hallow's Eve's bases.** `Hallows_Eve_Quest_Base`, `Hallows_Eve_Contract_Base` and `Hallows_Eve_Offer_Base` carry `"Season": "Hallows_Eve"` beside their shape, and the storefront, the board, the `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` wallet, Old Jack's placement and the trick-or-treat extension name it in place of their `Requires`; the typed `ziggfreedcommon:feature` `Hallows_Eve_Live` gates on the quests, contracts and the intro are gone. `Season` is a leaf of its own, so a child keeps it whatever `Requires` it writes, where a base's `Requires` is replaced whole by a child's. The Harvest Moon bundle names `"Season": "Harvest_Moon"`, and `Hallows_Eve_Board_Harvest_Moon` keeps its own `Harvest_Moon_Live` gate. A conversation has no `Season` leaf, so a line that opens a board or a stall still reads `ziggfreedcommon:feature` `<Event>_Live` with `Min` 1, and `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_live` stays for locks and rolls. What shows in each calendar state is unchanged.
- **Each MMO reward row gated on its own.** Every `Mmo_Xp` and `Mmo_Boost_Token` row in a quest's, contract's or achievement's `Rewards` carries its own `"Requires": { "Factors": [ { "Factor": "hytale:mod_installed", "Param": "Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree", "Min": 1 } ] }`, so a server without MMO Skill Tree leaves that row out, unpaid and unshown, while the file's other rows pay. A contract keeps a plain row beside its gated one, since a contract whose every row is gated pays nothing there (`EMPTY_REWARDS`). The four `Mmo_Hallows_Eve_{Chain,Daily,Board,Lantern}_Pay` contributions are gone, as they are from the MMO jar. Hallow's Eve's XP rows move to the bounty board's scale: the dailies, the five board contracts and the four skill dailies 1,500; the questline steps and `hallows_eve_old_jack_meet` 2,500; `hallows_eve_old_jack_lantern` DEFENSE 5,000 plus HARVESTING 2,500; `ghoul_breaker`, `lantern_lobber` and `soul_warden` 2,500 and `geode_cracker` 5,000.
- **Hallow's Eve's MMO layer ships in the pack.** The manifest names `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` (`*`) under `OptionalDependencies`, which only orders the load, so the pack's copy of each shared id loads after the jar's. The layer keeps the jar's ids in `HallowsEvePack` folders. The five files in the MMO's own stores and RPG Stations (`Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Harvest_Moon` under `MMOSkillTree/BoostRules`, `Mmo_Hallows_Eve` under `MobKillXp`, the XP map `Hallows_Eve`, and the extensions `Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Carving` and `Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Carving_Hallowed` on the `mmoskilltree:skill_xp` channel) carry no gate. The six ZiggfreedCommon-store files (the four skill dailies, the gear set `Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Hallowed` and the lootable contribution `Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Geode_Crack`) carry the top-level `{"Factor": "hytale:mod_installed", "Param": "Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree", "Min": 1}`, which ZiggfreedCommon drops at load without the MMO; only the skill dailies keep a `Hallows_Eve_Live` gate beside it. Their words are in the pack's own `hallowseve.mmo.lang`, never a `mmoskilltree.lang`. `Mmo_Hallows_Eve`'s `Payload` names each creature by its whole role id, so the longest match beats the MMO's shorter Ghoul, Skeleton and Wraith patterns: `Hallows_Eve_Lost_Soul` and `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton` 130, `Hallows_Eve_Hollow_Ghoul` and `Hallows_Eve_Geode_Wraith` 250.
- **The festival stall through `Includes`.** `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival_Stall` ships `Enabled: false` with no `Where` and no `Season`, so it sits in no storefront list, and `Currencies` `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival_Token`, one `festival` category and `Order` 95. Each host's storefront (`Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack`, `Harvest_Feast_Host`) writes `"Includes": [ "Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival_Stall" ]` and nothing else for it: the included stall brings its wallet, place and category name, and its shelf follows the host's own `CategoryOrder`. Its nine offers name it in their `Shop` leaf, so a purchase limit is one limit across every host. Two are locked by `ziggfreedcommon:reputation_rank`: `Deco_Goblin_Plush` (60) on `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival/Festival_Tier_1` and `Furniture_Kweebec_Statue` (80) on `Festival_Tier_2`. An owner opens the stall all year with `enabled` true in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/shops.json` and an Almanac link whose `Destination` has `Type` `Shop`; its words ship as `almanac.seasons_of_orbis.link.festival_stall`.
- **Festival Favor's ranks above Exalted, with `From`.** `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival` is a native `ReputationGroup` (`NPCGroups` an empty list, `InitialReputationValue` 0) with a companion in `Reputations/SeasonsOfOrbis/` (`Order` 5, `Icon` `Deco_Trophy_Harvest`, no `Cap`). Its `Ranks` add `Festival_Tier_1`, `Festival_Tier_2` and `Festival_Tier_3` with `From` 30000, 40000 and 50000, each above Exalted's floor so the shared ranks keep theirs, named Exalted II, III and IV in `seasonsoforbis.lang`. `Beyond {Every 5000}` pays a `Currency` reward of 25 festival tokens past the top tier's floor. Favor is a `Reputation` reward on each file's own `Rewards.Claim`: a daily 40, a questline step 150 and a season's finale (the lantern, The Long Table) 400 at both hosts; a Field, Kitchen, Night or Day contract 30; Martha's shared meal 25 and her invitation tour 200; the skill contracts none. `seasons_of_orbis_guest_of_honor` (one `REPUTATION_RANK` criterion, Target `Festival_Tier_3`, Qualifier `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival`, filed under `Seasons` > `Festival_Favor`, no `Occurrence`) pays the `Title` `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Guest_Of_Honor` through `Rewards.Auto`.
- **The festival token wallet.** `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Festival_Token` is a counter wallet with no `OnDeath`, `Decay`, `Season` or `Requires`, so it keeps from season to season and shows all year. A pay table pays it in a roll that always lands, through the `Currency` reward kind, since a counter wallet has no item to hand over: Hallow's Eve's chain table 2, lantern 5, daily and board 1; Harvest Feast's chain 2, feast 5, invitation tour 4, daily, shared meal and board 1. Each season's way in (`hallows_eve_old_jack_meet`, `harvest_feast_martha_meet`) pays 2 on its own `Rewards.Claim`.
- **The ladder's `MetaSelector`, with `AnyYear` and `Needs`.** `seasons_of_orbis_rung_1`, `seasons_of_orbis_rung_2` and `seasons_of_orbis` (the id 1.0.0 shipped disabled, now switched on) sit under `Seasons` with no subcategory, drawn as one ladder by `Chains` `seasons_of_orbis` tiers 1 to 3. Each selects `Category` `Seasons` and `Tags` `["season_keepsake"]` with `AnyYear` true, so any year's copy of a season's keepsake stands for it and copies count once per season. The rungs write `Needs` 2 and 4; the top rung writes no `Needs`, so it asks for every season it finds, with `AtLeast` 4 as the floor. A season's yearly keepsake carries `season_keepsake` and nothing else does (`hallows_eve_lantern_keepsake`, `harvest_feast_keepsake`); a season switched off in `calendar.json` leaves the count. The rungs pay festival tokens (25, 50, 100) and Festival Favor (1500, 3000, 6000) through `Rewards.Claim`, and the top rung also a `Deco_Trophy_Harvest` and, in `Rewards.Auto`, the `Title` `Seasons_Of_Orbis_Every_Season`. Each description's number rides `TextArgs`, kept equal to `Needs` or `AtLeast`.
- **The anniversary's one-day `Window`.** `Hytale_Anniversary` writes `"Window": { "Start": "01-13", "End": "01-13" }`, `FirstYear` 2027 and no `Clock`, so it runs one UTC day a year, with a major start `Herald` and no end banner. Its achievement `hytale_anniversary` (`Occurrence {Event: Hytale_Anniversary}`, one `CALENDAR_ATTENDED` criterion, `Listing` `Seasons` > `Hytale_Anniversary`) mints `hytale_anniversary_<year>`, each copy paying the `Title` `Hytale_Anniversary_Ever_Present` through `Rewards.Auto`; a year with an `achievement.hytale_anniversary_<year>.title` line (2027 to 2036 ship) takes that name. It carries no `season_keepsake` tag and its Almanac page `Hytale_Anniversary` writes no `Keepsake`, `Stats` or `Links`, so the ladder never counts it.
- **The per-run repeat on the Lantern.** `hallows_eve_old_jack_lantern` writes `"Repeat": { "PerRun": { "Event": "Hallows_Eve", "Times": 1 } }` in place of 1.0.0's 60-day `Repeat.Cooldown`: one completion per run of the event, so a forced or moved run cannot pay it twice, and a lantern left unfinished at the end waits for the next run. Harvest Feast's `harvest_feast_the_feast` and `harvest_feast_invitations` use the same shape on `Harvest_Feast`.

## Harvest Feast

- Calendar `Harvest_Feast` (`Window` `11-20` to `12-01`, `FirstYear` 2026, UTC, `Presentation` icon `Food_Pie_Pumpkin`, a major start `Herald` and an end banner). Content that vanishes names `"Season": "Harvest_Feast"`, on itself or its base.
- Characters: the host `Harvest_Feast_Host` (Martha the Cook) and three villagers, `Harvest_Feast_Miller`, `Harvest_Feast_Weaver` and `Harvest_Feast_Shepherd`, each a role under `NPC/Roles/Passive/` with a placement in `NpcPlacements/` (`Where` `GameplayConfig` `ForgottenTemple`, `Lifecycle` `KeepAlive`, `Respawn` and `Fortify`, `Interact.Dialogue` its own conversation). Martha and Bram stand on `Anchor.WorldSpawn` offsets; Tomas and Elsie on `Anchor.Structure` offsets from the `Temple_Kweebec_Merchant_Static` marker with `Limits.OncePerWorld`. Each villager has an id and a conversation of its own, since the invitation line is spent per conversation. Martha's placement carries `Props`, still items drawn at her spot while she stands: a `Furniture_Tavern_Table` and a `Furniture_Tavern_Bench`, each with an `Offset` in world axes from her spot (`Y` 0.5, since a block prop is drawn centred on its point) and a `Yaw` in degrees.
- Reputation `Harvest_Feast_Host` (Favor at the Feast; native group plus companion, `Order` 30, `Cap` 21000, `Ranks` renaming Neutral to Exalted as Newcomer, Neighbor, Known Face, Family and Head of the Table). `Friendly` locks the meat pie recipe and the board's second Field slot, `Honored` the second Kitchen slot, `Revered` the Caesar salad recipe, and `harvest_feast_head_of_the_table` (`REPUTATION_RANK` `Exalted`, no `Parent`, so no yearly copy) pays its title. Martha's daily board line comes in four wordings by rank, sharing one `OnceId` with `Once {Period: Daily}`, each paying 10 favor through its `Actions`.
- Quests on `Harvest_Feast_Quest_Base`: the intro `harvest_feast_martha_meet` (`Flow.AutoAccept`, a blank-target `TURN_IN` at the giver, `Requires` `Not` `Quests` `harvest_feast_host_1`); the chain `harvest_feast_host_1` to `_3` and `harvest_feast_the_feast`; the dailies `harvest_feast_daily_{turkeys,wheat,roast}` and `harvest_feast_shared_meal` (`Repeat.Reset` Daily); and `harvest_feast_invitations`, whose one `TALK_TO_NPC` step counts the `Qualifier` `Harvest_Feast_Invitation` to 3 with no `Target`. `harvest_feast_host_2` also pays the `Item` `Recipe_Food_Pie_Pumpkin` and the `Costume` `Harvest_Feast_Costume_Turkey` (180 seconds) with a `FallbackLootable`.
- The extension `Harvest_Feast_Invitation` adds one line to every other ZiggfreedCommon conversation while the tour is `ACTIVE`: `Once {Period: Daily, PerConversation: true}`, so it is spent per character and comes back at midnight UTC; `MarkTalked` with that `Qualifier` and no `Target`, then `Close`; `Dialogues.Exclude` keeps it out of both hosts' conversations.
- The board `Harvest_Feast_Daily` (Daily rotation, `Where` `ForgottenTemple`, `Currencies` the festival token) has five slots: `Harvest_Feast_Field` and `Harvest_Feast_Kitchen`; a second of each, `Optional` with a slot `Requires` on `Harvest_Feast_Host/Friendly` and `/Honored`; and the `Optional` `Harvest_Feast_Skill`. Four contracts on `Harvest_Feast_Contract_Base` fill the two bands, `Harvest_Feast_Board_Pies` locked by `Requires` `Quests` `harvest_feast_host_2`. MMO Skill Tree's four skill contracts stand on `Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Skill_Contract_Base`, whose top-level `hytale:mod_installed` `Requires` drops the base and every child at load without the MMO; a child writes no `Requires` of its own, since it would replace the gate whole.
- The storefront `Harvest_Feast_Host` (`Order` 90, shelves `kitchen` and `decor`, `Where` `ForgottenTemple`, `Includes` the festival stall) with six offers on `Harvest_Feast_Offer_Base`, all priced in festival tokens: three recipe pages (`Limits.Total` 1, two locked by rank), `Food_Popcorn` (`Limits.Daily` 2), `Plant_Seeds_Corn` (`Limits.Daily` 1) and `Plant_Leaves_Autumn`.
- World: the spawn rule `Harvest_Feast_Turkeys` (`Harvest_Feast_Turkey`, a `Template_Animal_Neutral` Variant carrying the vanilla Turkey's `Modify`, `IsMemory` false; `Env_Zone1_Autumn`, `_Forests` and `_Plains`, `DayTimeRange` 6 to 18, `Despawn` 19 to 5) and the bonus row `Harvest_Feast_Spice_Find` (`PickupItem` `Plant_Crop_Pumpkin_Item`, 15 percent, `Ingredient_Spices`, the cue `Harvest_Feast_Spice_Found`). It names the same item as `Hallows_Eve_Pumpkin_Find`, and two rows on one item resolve to the id that sorts last, so a server running both seasons at once gets the spice and no Hallowed Pumpkins until the feast ends.
- Achievements on `Harvest_Feast_Achievement_Base` (`Occurrence {Event: Harvest_Feast}`, `Listing` `Seasons` > `Harvest_Feast`): the keepsake `harvest_feast_keepsake` (`Listing.Feat`, tags `full_table` and `season_keepsake`, a `Deco_Plate` stamped with `StackNameKey` `keepsake.harvest_feast_plate.name` and the year, through `Rewards.Claim`), `harvest_feast_guest`, `harvest_feast_turkey_chaser` and `harvest_feast_full_baskets`, and the capstone `harvest_feast_full_table` (`MetaSelector` on the `full_table` tag, the `Title` `Harvest_Feast_Full_Table`). The Almanac page `Harvest_Feast` names the keepsake and four tallies, its `Hero.Composition` a `Gradient` sky with a `Glow` and nine vanilla items.
- MMO Skill Tree's layer: gated `Mmo_Xp` rows in the skill the work trains (dailies and board contracts 1,500, the intro, questline steps, the tour and two achievements 2,500, `harvest_feast_the_feast` CRAFTING 5,000 plus HARVESTING 2,500), `Mmo_Boost_Token` rows on the shared meal (`ALL`, x1.1, 60 minutes, `Claim`), `harvest_feast_guest` (x1.25, 30 minutes, `Auto`) and `harvest_feast_full_table` (x1.5, 60 minutes, `Auto`), the four skill contracts (`GAIN_XP` in ARCHERY, CRAFTING, HARVESTING or WOODCUTTING, paying favor with Martha and 1,500 XP in that skill), and the boost rule `Mmo_Harvest_Feast_Harvesting` (`Server/MMOSkillTree/BoostRules/SeasonsOfOrbis/`, HARVESTING x1.25 while `ziggfreedcommon:calendar_live` `Harvest_Feast` reads 1), which needs no gate since only the MMO reads that store.
