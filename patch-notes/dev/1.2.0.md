Seasons of Orbis 1.2.0 adds the Headless Horseman, Hallow's Eve's boss, fought in a ZiggfreedCommon dungeon of his own on Hytale's encounter scripts, on a normal ride and a hard one, with the boss parts a later boss reuses, Grave Candles to open his gate, his Gravekeeper's conversation, his place in the Almanac and two Jack sets built around his armour; hangs Old Jack's two boards (and Martha's) on their hosts' quest pages; tells Old Jack's story as one tale, runs it through the Horseman and makes each season's keepsake its last quest's own pay; moves Hallow's Eve's gear to a percent favor gain; and lets the Lantern Bombs hit other players where PvP is on. Manifest: `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` unchanged; `Dependencies` `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.4.0` (for `ZigClaim`, dungeons with their `EntryCost` and `EntryYaw`, a board's `Hosts`, `ReputationGain`, `DamageResist_Fire`, `Participation.DamageToAdds`, `Progression.Feats` and `ENCOUNTER_FEAT`, `GEAR_SET_TIER`, a boss's spoils chest and a quest step's item `Drop`, `Listing.MainLine`, a Lootable's `Text` and `HideInPreview`, an Almanac picture's `Npc` and a quest reward's `@run`) and `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`, unchanged. New words are in `hallowseve.world.lang`, `hallowseve.progression.lang`, `hallowseve.items.lang`, `npcs.lang`, `items.lang` and `client.lang`, bare keys, in all nine locales. The shapes below are the ones a pack author can copy.

## Shapes

- **A shared boss encounter template.** `Server/EncounterManager/SeasonsOfOrbis/Orbis_Boss_Encounter_Template.json` is an Abstract template in the shape of the game's phased boss example: a behaviour and a transition slot per phase, filled by a Variant through `_ForwardedModifiers`. It raises the boss from his spawn marker when players come near (or beside the script when no marker is in reach), applies one health step per player to five and refills him after a one-second settle, then sends `zc:engaged`. Each phase keeps him bound by his NPC group through a change of form, and the fight ends only when he stays gone a second (`Macro_Orbis_Boss_Defeat`: the bar down, `zc:defeated`). Complete grants the Variant's two reward ids (`GrantReward`) before `zc:reset`; the game's arena reset handles a wipe or an empty arena, and Resetting sweeps the Variant's `SweepGroups` and owes `zc:reset` (flag `Orbis_Boss_Reset_Owed`), which Intro sends once that reset has removed the boss, so the library never binds the old boss again. `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Hold` locks any NPC of the Variant's `BossGroups` in reach into the Boss slot each tick, so a boss who changes role mid-fight stays the fight's; `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Volley` is the telegraphed volley (a warning ring under each player near the fight, then a bomb dropped onto it, the game's Gobliterator falling-burst pattern), tuned by `Interval`, `BurstDuration` and `DropDelay`; `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Transition_HealthAndFlag` is the game's `Encounter_Macro_Transition_TargetInHealthRange` with a `Flag` it also waits on, so a phase's own beat plays before the next phase starts. `HealthPacks` (default false) with `HealthPackTag`, `HealthPackRange`, `HealthPackInteraction`, `HealthPackInterval` and `HealthPackSpawnCount` drops the game's health packs the way its Feastmaster's fight does: the game's `Macro_Health_Pack_Spawn` runs `Root_Health_Pack_Spawn` on a random point tagged `HealthPackTag` within `HealthPackRange` of the script, every `HealthPackInterval` seconds, while the flag `Orbis_Boss_HealthPack_Active` is set (set as the fight begins when `HealthPacks` is true, cleared at the win and at a reset). These parts carry no `Season`.
- **Boss bombs never share the players' files.** `Orbis_Boss_Bomb_Burst` and `Orbis_Boss_Bomb_Burst_Heavy` are AOE selectors whose one hit rule matches `{"Type": "Player"}` only, with `IgnoreOwner` true and vanilla `Burn`, so a boss and his adds are never hurt and no hit pays the MMO's Artillery row. `Orbis_Boss_Fire_Patch_Spawn` places the trigger-volume effect `Server/TriggerVolumes/Effects/SeasonsOfOrbis/Orbis_Boss_Fire_Patch.json`, a players-only fire patch for 10 seconds that refreshes vanilla `Burn` on whoever stands in it each second and deals no damage of its own, so all its harm is Fire, which fire resistance cuts (a trigger volume's `DamageEntity` tick is always Environment, `DamageEntityEffect.resolveCause`). A boss's own projectile configs give the look and point at the shared burst.
- **The Horseman's three roles, from vanilla parts.** `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Mounted` is a Variant of `Template_Flying_Aggressive` on vanilla's skeletal horse (its airborne sets FlyIdle, Fly, FlyFast and FlyBackward remapped to the horse's Walk, Run and Walk_Backward, since vanilla's Fly sets hold the legs still; a model's `AnimationSets` merge per key with its `Parent`'s) with a headless burnt rider and his lit lantern hung from the horse's `Saddle-Attachment` beside vanilla's saddle (the neck flame, vanilla's `Campfire_New_Cartoon`, sits low and small, `PositionOffset` Y 0.45 and `Scale` 0.55, since its smoke and sparks rise well above it). Its `MotionFlyMaxHeightAboveGround` is 11, above every point his flight seeks. Its `CombatAdditionalBehaviorComponent` is `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Ride` (`Server/NPC/Roles/Hallows_Eve/Components/`), a component with its own local states that picks where he flies (the first instruction to set a body motion in a tick wins, so its moves override the template's) and leaves the template's chase to fly him while he fights low: he dives at a point 2 blocks above his target (`AdjustPosition` on `Target`, `DesiredAltitudeWeight` 0, so he comes down on one line instead of skimming the graves) and fights low for 6 to 9 seconds in the template's 1 to 3 block combat band, then breaks off, the first due of: his first fountain lap (8 seconds into the ride, always), a bomb pass (22 seconds in, then every 20 to 28), a second or third lap (each 15 to 25 seconds after the last, an even chance against a swing), else a swing for 4 to 6 seconds at full speed between random points 8 or 9 blocks up on an oval round the graveyard's fountain, about 12 blocks out along the road and 6.5 to 9 across it, at least 4 blocks inside its barrier walls (`Seek` with `DesiredAltitudeWeight` 0 toward his `Leash` point moved by `AdjustPosition`); a lap is 5 to 10 seconds round eight points 7 from the fountain's centre and 8 up, 2 over its brazier. A swing, lap or pass ends in `.Break_End`: a swoop when one is due (10 seconds in, then every 14 to 20) and his target is within 20 blocks, else a dive. The swoop is the engine's `Charge` body motion on his `Fly` controller in the Gobliterator's dash shape (`LockedOnDurationRange` 0.5, `LockedOnToleranceAngle` 10, `WindingUpDurationRange` 1, uninterruptible, `WindingUpRelativeTurnSpeed` 0 so the line holds, `ChargeAbsoluteSpeed` 20, `ChargeDistanceRange` 30, `PlayerCollisionInteraction` vanilla's `Root_NPC_Skeleton_Burnt_Praetorian_Charge_Hit`, no `NPCCollisionInteraction`); on `WindingUp` he plays the horse's `Alerted` (its neigh), the horse's scream `SFX_Horse_Death` and `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Swoop_Glow` (vanilla's `Saw_Charge_VFX` highlight, 1.5 seconds), and `Feastmaster_Slam_Warning` (Scale 1.7) lights at his target's ground point; `Obstructed`, `Finished`, `EntityHit` or `Knockback` sends him to fight low, and no lock within 3 seconds sends him to dive. The bomb pass stores his target's spot 8 up and the two ends 12 blocks north and south (`StorePosition`; a coin picks the start), flies to the start, and as the pass begins lights six `Stalactite_Ground_Indicator` rings 4 blocks apart along the road through that spot (`AdjustPosition` action, `ProjectToGround`, `SpawnParticles`) with `Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Line_Drop` spawned on each in the same frame (`SpawnInteraction`): `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Line_Projectile`, the volley bomb with the lighter `Orbis_Boss_Bomb_Burst`, lands about 2 seconds later as he flies over. The `Fly` controller stops dead at the first block it touches and never slides along it (`MotionControllerFly.doMove`), so a touch in any state but the swoop (`Eval` `blocked`, its obstructed flag, while no unstick runs; never a `Nav` state, which a `Wander` leaves as it was) runs vanilla's own stuck move for 1 second (`UnstickTime`) ahead of every other move, whose timers keep running: the `Wander` of `Template_Flying_Aggressive`'s `.Chase` (`DesiredAltitudeWeight` 1, `RelativeSpeed` 1, `MaxHeadingChange` 180, `TestsPerTick` 8), which probes the directions round him and flies the clearest. Every timer is armed with `TimerStart` and `TimerRestart` together, since `TimerStart` only ever starts a timer once (`ActionTimer.executeStartAction`). The normal ride's `RangedAttack` is empty, which disables the template's ranged attack (`Enabled` `!isEmpty(RangedAttack)`), so he throws nothing; `_Mounted_HM` keeps `Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Bomb` at anyone within 18 blocks with `RangedAttackAimingRange` 3 (the template's `Aim` head motion tracks the player the whole time), then a 2 to 3 second pause. Every 4 to 6 seconds (`TargetSwitchTimer`) he turns on the nearest player unless his target is within 6 blocks (`TargetRange`). `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman` (on foot) is a Variant of the pack's `Hallows_Eve_Template_Horseman_Foot` (`Server/NPC/Roles/Hallows_Eve/Templates/`), a copy of vanilla's `Template_Boss_Goblin_Gobliterator` kept to its combat-evaluator core (Idle, Alerted, `.Default`, `.Charge`, `.ChargeEnd`, `.Stunned`, the chase), so his charge is the Gobliterator's: 0.5 seconds locked on at 10 degrees, a 1 second uninterruptible wind-up with `Goblin_Feastmaster_StampedePrepare`, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Dash_Glow` (vanilla's `Saw_Charge_VFX` highlight and `Gobliterator_Saw_Charge_Warning` ground marker, without the saw sparks) and his model's `DashPrepare` (the skeleton's `Alerted` with `SFX_Skeleton_Elite_Downstrike_Charging`, the cry vanilla's elite skeleton gives winding up its downstrike), a launch on `SFX_Staff_Flame_Fireball_Launch`, 20 blocks a second for 20 to 25 blocks under `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Dash_Loop` (`SFX_Staff_Flame_Flamethrower`, refreshed each charging tick), and any block stops him with a 1 second stun (`.Stunned`, `Goblin_Feastmaster_Stunned`). The copy drops what is the Gobliterator's alone: its saw launch and loop sounds, its hit sound, its beacons, its block-breaking pass-through set (no block is ever broken), its NPC collision hit (his Jack Skeletons are never hit), and its club-slam, out-of-control, aggressive and slow-tracking states; `DeathParticles` is `Effect_Death_Flames` and `BreathesInWater` true. His model `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman` adds `DashPrepare`, `Dash`, `DashImpact`, `DashMiss` and `DashCrash` sets on the skeleton's own animations (a set named there replaces only that key of its `Parent`'s). His combat evaluator `CAE_Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman` holds longsword swings, the charge (5 to 15 blocks, every 8 seconds) and `Slam` (`Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Slam`, within 4 blocks, every 15 seconds): the Feastmaster's slam with his sword, `Feastmaster_Slam_Warning` (Scale 3.4) about him for 1 second rooted, an overhead chop (`Skeleton_Sword` `SwingDown`), then `AOECircle` 5 hitting `Player` only for 36 Physical and a throw; `PickTarget` carries `OffhandSlot` -1, since he holds no off-hand item. No shield, no summon, no back-off (the template has no strafe state), and nothing thrown. The Skeleton rig with its skull hidden, the Burnt Knight's armour and a lantern in his left hand. `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head` is a Variant of `Template_Flying_Aggressive` carrying the Incandescent skull's own `Modify`, wearing the Jack Helm, with an empty `RangedAttack`, so it throws nothing. All three take half damage from fire (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Fire_Ward`, 50% Fire `DamageResistance`, through `_CombatConfig` on the fliers and the combat evaluators `CAE_Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman` and `_HM` on foot, so a Lantern Bomb and its burn hurt them at half), are never shoved (`KnockbackScale` 0 on the bodies), drop nothing (`DropList` Empty; the pay is Claims) and share one name key; the two bodies share `MaxHealth` 1100 (the hard ride's `_HM` pair 2376), since health carries across the swap, and the Head has a fixed 300 (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head_HM` 450). Their groups are `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Group`, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head_Group` and `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Adds_Group`, and his marker `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Marker`. `tools/derive_art.py` writes the five derived models and the on-foot icon from two newly vendored vanilla files (refresh with `--vendor <Assets.zip>` before regenerating); its byte-for-byte output-list assertion is deleted, its determinism and staleness checks kept.
- **His fight, a Variant.** `Server/EncounterManager/Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter.json` (suffixed, since a script named after a role replaces it) fills the template: phase 1, the Ride, a volley every 6 seconds (`Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Volley`, range 45; its bomb drawn three times a Lantern Bomb's size through its own model, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Volley_Bomb`, whose `HitBox` is a third of the thrown bomb's so it strikes where the smaller one did); at half his health (`HealthRange` `[0, 0.5]`, 550 of 1100 solo) the Gobliterator's transition (`Macro_Goblin_Gobliterator_Transition_OnHealth`, `GuardTimeout` 1.5 seconds, so a chain running on him delays the swap only a moment) swaps him to his foot role, invulnerable while it lands, with two Jack Skeletons; phase 2, `Macro_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head`, 6 seconds after the swap (`FirstTossSeconds`, set on the re-armed toss timer with `TimerModify`) and then 40 seconds after each flight ends turns his body invulnerable, puts his shield on him and raises the Head: kill it and he bleeds again, ignore it 25 seconds and it returns and heals him 5% (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Mend`), and either way the flight sets `Horseman_Head_Flown`. The shield, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Invulnerable` (the macro's `ShieldEffect`), is vanilla's `Dungeon_Feastmaster_Invulnerable` sized to him: its `EntityBottomTint` and its `Dungeon_Feastmaster_State_Invulnerable` bubble and `Dungeon_Feastmaster_State_Protected` ring at `Scale` 0.6, only a look (the script's `SetTargetNPCInvulnerable` does the guarding), with a 30 second `Duration` as a backstop. The hard script `_Hard` forwards a volley every 4 seconds, the same `GuardTimeout` and the same head gate, the throw at `[0, 0.5455]` and the Wrath at `[0, 0.3]` of the `_HM` pair's 2376 (1296 and 713 solo, so the saddle holds 1080), and `HealthPacks` false. Each turn is told through a feedback moment (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Unhorsed`, `_Head_Off`, `_Head_Down`, `_Head_Returned`, `_Wrath`, words `notify.hallows_eve_horseman_*`).

- **His binding row names him, shares his credit and keeps his records.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Encounters/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter.json`: `NameKey` `npcs.Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman.name`, `Subject.TargetSlot` `Boss`. `Participation` writes `DamageToAdds` 2.5 (ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's weight for damage dealt to a fight's adds, here his Head and his Jack Skeletons), `DamageTaken` 0.5 and `Presence` 2.0 over the server rule's `DamageDealt` 1.0 and `MinShare` 0.05, so in a three-minute fight for three where two players split his body and one cuts his one Head down and kills the skeletons, the cutter reads about 0.95 of the top share, a member who fights nothing about 0.25 and one who arrives for the last minute and fights nothing about 0.08 (one player landing all the body damage pulls the others to about 0.53, 0.13 and 0.04, under `MinShare`; a second Head puts the cutter on top). `Leaderboard.Bucket` `hallows_eve_headless_horseman` with `ByPartySize` true and `Group` `Seasonal`, so Records' Bosses tab ranks his wins by party size under the Seasonal group. `Feedback` names his own `Engaged`, `Defeated` and `Wiped` moments, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_{Engaged,Defeated,Wiped}` under `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/FeedbackMoments/`: a Major broadcast to his participants in the same world within 96 blocks, at most once every 5 seconds; a Reward toast carrying the player's share and the library's discovery sting; an Info toast after a wipe whose second line teaches the head. No `Scale` (his scaling is native, one step per player) and no `Loot` (the script pays his Claims).
- **His Claims, in a chest where he falls.** `Server/Rewards/Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily.json` is a `ZigClaim` on the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily` (25 Hallow Sweets, 3 Ectoplasm and 2 festival tokens always; 30% two Spectral and 10% one Shattering Lantern Bomb; 15% each for his plain Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves), `ShareScaled` true and no `Diminish`, so the first win each day pays. `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily_Hard.json` is the same on `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily_Hard` (40 Hallow Sweets, 5 Ectoplasm and 4 festival tokens always; 50% two Spectral and 20% one Shattering Lantern Bomb; 25% each for the plain pieces). `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase.json` ("The Horseman's Hoard", `TitleKey` `claim.hallows_eve_horseman_chase`) is a `ZigClaim` on `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase`, `ShareScaled` false, `Diminish` `[1, 0]` Daily: a roll with no `Chance` pays a Spectral Lantern Bomb every time, so a roll is never empty; `_Chase_Hard.json` (`claim.hallows_eve_horseman_chase_hard`, "The Horseman's Hoard (Hard)") is its hard twin; the rest of each ride's Hoard is under **The Hoards' candles, favor and XP** below. Both binding rows keep ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's default `Spoils`, so a win's Claims wait in a chest where he fell, each player seeing only their own share and what they leave waiting under Claims; both graveyards set `Rewards.InChest` true and `Text.ChestKey` `dungeon.hallows_eve_graveyard.chest`, so the clear's Claim joins the Horseman's chest and no results screen opens.
- **His armour, plain and flaming.** `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Breastplate`, `_Gauntlets` and `_Greaves` under `Server/Item/Items/Hallows_Eve/` are vanilla's ancient steel pieces written whole (no `Parent`, so no crafting recipe is inherited), Rare at item level 20, toned to the Jack line, each with a little health and `DamageBonus_Fire` 3 (ZiggfreedCommon's per-type damage stat, whole percent points) in `Armor.StatModifiers`. `Hallows_Eve_Flaming_Horseman_Breastplate`, `_Gauntlets` and `_Greaves` reuse those models, textures and icons with a burning light: Epic, item level 30, Fire `DamageResistance` (10%, 5% and 8%) and `DamageBonus_Fire` 8 each. Armour, never a weapon: a weapon's stats count only while it is held, and a player throwing a Lantern Bomb holds the bomb.
- **The graveyard, a dungeon.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Dungeons/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard.json`: `Area.Prefab` `Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard.prefab.json` (an enclosed night graveyard, 64 by 110 by 36), `EntryOffset` Y 1, `Share` five to an area and four areas to a copy, `Complete.Bosses` his script, `Rewards` the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Clear` (30 Hallow Sweets and 3 festival tokens) with `Diminish` `[1, 0.5]` Daily, `Copy.EmptyCloseSeconds` 60, `"Season": "Hallows_Eve"` and `Text` keys `dungeon.hallows_eve_graveyard.{name,description,goal}` (the goal a player reads on arrival). The prefab is generated: `tools/arena_markers.py` writes it from the source prefab and its spec in `tools/arenas/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard.json` (the fight's encounter entity on the road south of the central plaza, his spawn marker on the plaza's north side, a vanilla `Leave_Instance` pad against the arrival room's back wall, and six `Orbis_Boss_Health_Pack` points 4 cells over open ground around the plaza; no UUIDs, so every paste stages a fresh fight); `--check` compares, and the output is never hand-edited. A spec's `Points` list writes each point as the game's own prefab point (a `Transform` and a `Point` component with `Name` and `Tags`, the shape its builder tools save), which a paste registers as a live point for an encounter's `Points` sensor.
- **A hidden chest, stocked on every paste.** The source prefab's one container, a `Furniture_Dungeon_Chest_Epic` in a sealed treasure room inside the graveyard's west rock (cell -25, 1, 46 from the anchor, reached down a cobwebbed shaft), names the drop list `Hallows_Eve_Drop_Graveyard_Chest` (`Server/Drops/Hallows_Eve/`: one or two `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Common` always and a `Hallows_Eve_Grave_Candle` at `Weight` 10) in both graveyards, through the game's own prefab chest fill: the `Droplist` key on the block's `ItemContainerBlock` component, as vanilla's `Goblin_Thief_Chest` prefab writes it. A spec's `Containers` list (`At`, `Droplist`) writes it and refuses a cell that holds no container. Each paste places a fresh chest, and the game's stash rolls the list into it as it lands and then clears the list, so every party finds it full; it is one native container, so the first player to open it takes what it holds; what a party leaves in it carries into the fresh chest at the next paste (the game moves a replaced container's items into its replacement) and the roll adds to it.
- **Arriving in the graveyard faces into it.** Both graveyard dungeon files set `Area.EntryYaw` 180 (ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0), so a party arrives facing south, toward the fight, with the entrance room's back wall and its `Leave_Instance` pad behind them.
- **An intro camera sweeps the graveyard on arrival.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/CameraSequences/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Intro.json` (ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's camera sequence) is a 5.6 s pan in blocks from the area's paste spot: a cut high over the gate, a sweep south over the graves toward the fight, a glide back down outside the entrance door, then a cut to the player's eyes.
