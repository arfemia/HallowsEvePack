Seasons of Orbis 1.2.0 adds the Headless Horseman, Hallow's Eve's boss, fought in a ZiggfreedCommon dungeon of his own on Hytale's encounter scripts, with the boss parts a later boss reuses; hangs Old Jack's two boards (and Martha's) on their hosts' quest pages; moves Hallow's Eve's gear to a percent favor gain; and lets the Lantern Bombs hit other players where PvP is on. Manifest: `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` unchanged; `Dependencies` `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.4.0` (for `ZigClaim`, dungeons, a board's `Hosts`, `ReputationGain` and `Participation.DamageToAdds`) and `Ziggfreed:RpgStations >=1.1.1`, unchanged. New words are in `hallowseve.world.lang`, `hallowseve.progression.lang`, `npcs.lang` and `items.lang`, bare keys; the other eight locales of the Horseman's fight, graveyard, Gravekeeper and armour lines come with the release's language pass, and his ladder's and title's nine lines are in all nine already. The shapes below are the ones a pack author can copy.

## Shapes

- **A shared boss encounter template.** `Server/EncounterManager/SeasonsOfOrbis/Orbis_Boss_Encounter_Template.json` is an Abstract template in the shape of the game's phased boss example: a behaviour and a transition slot per phase, filled by a Variant through `_ForwardedModifiers`. It raises the boss from his spawn marker when players come near (or beside the script when no marker is in reach), applies one health step per player to five and refills him after a one-second settle, then sends `zc:engaged`. Each phase keeps him bound by his NPC group through a change of form, and the fight ends only when he stays gone a second (`Macro_Orbis_Boss_Defeat`: the bar down, `zc:defeated`). Complete grants the Variant's two reward ids (`GrantReward`) before `zc:reset`; the game's arena reset handles a wipe or an empty arena, and Resetting sends `zc:reset` once and sweeps the Variant's `SweepGroups`. `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Hold` locks any NPC of the Variant's `BossGroups` in reach into the Boss slot each tick, so a boss who changes role mid-fight stays the fight's; `Macro_Orbis_Boss_Volley` is the telegraphed volley (a warning ring under each player near the fight, then a bomb dropped onto it, the game's Gobliterator falling-burst pattern), tuned by `Interval`, `BurstDuration` and `DropDelay`. These parts carry no `Season`.
- **Boss bombs never share the players' files.** `Orbis_Boss_Bomb_Burst` and `Orbis_Boss_Bomb_Burst_Heavy` are AOE selectors whose one hit rule matches `{"Type": "Player"}` only, with `IgnoreOwner` true and vanilla `Burn`, so a boss and his adds are never hurt and no hit pays the MMO's Artillery row. `Orbis_Boss_Fire_Patch_Spawn` places the trigger-volume effect `Server/TriggerVolumes/Effects/SeasonsOfOrbis/Orbis_Boss_Fire_Patch.json`, a players-only fire patch for 10 seconds. A boss's own projectile configs give the look and point at the shared burst.
- **The Horseman's three roles, from vanilla parts.** `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Mounted` is a Variant of `Template_Flying_Aggressive` on vanilla's skeletal horse (its Fly animations by `Parent`) with a headless burnt rider and his lit lantern hung from the horse's `Saddle-Attachment` beside vanilla's saddle; he circles 1 to 3 blocks over his target and lobs `Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Bomb` at anyone within 18 blocks every 3 to 4 seconds. `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman` (on foot) is a Variant of `Template_Intelligent` carrying the Burnt Praetorian's own `Modify`, with his own combat evaluator `CAE_Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman` (longsword swings, a charge and a lantern bomb; no shield, no summon), the Skeleton rig with its skull hidden, the Burnt Knight's armour and a lantern in his left hand. `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head` is a Variant of `Template_Flying_Aggressive` carrying the Incandescent skull's own `Modify`, wearing the Jack Helm, with a smaller bomb (`Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head_Bomb`) in place of the skull's spores. All three never burn, are never shoved (`KnockbackScale` 0 on the bodies), drop nothing (`DropList` Empty; the pay is Claims) and share one name key; the two bodies share `MaxHealth` 1200, since health carries across the swap, and the Head has a fixed 300. Their groups are `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Group`, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head_Group` and `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Adds_Group`, and his marker `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Marker`. `tools/derive_art.py` writes the five derived models and the on-foot icon from two newly vendored vanilla files (refresh with `--vendor <Assets.zip>` before regenerating); its byte-for-byte output-list assertion is deleted, its determinism and staleness checks kept.
- **His fight, a Variant.** `Server/EncounterManager/Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter.json` (suffixed, since a script named after a role replaces it) fills the template: phase 1, the Ride, a volley every 12 seconds (`Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Volley`, range 45); at 66% the Gobliterator's transition (`Macro_Goblin_Gobliterator_Transition_OnHealth`) swaps him to his foot role, invulnerable while it lands, with two Jack Skeletons; phase 2, `Macro_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Head`, every 40 seconds turns his body invulnerable and raises the Head: kill it and he bleeds again, ignore it 25 seconds and it returns and heals him 5% (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Mend`); at 33% (`Encounter_Macro_Transition_TargetInHealthRange`) phase 3, `Macro_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Wrath`: the effect `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Wrath` (his own attacks +30% through `OutgoingDamage`, 1.2 times his speed, a red tint), two more Jack Skeletons and burning volleys every 8 seconds (`Root_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Volley_Fire`). `Macro_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Reset`, in the reset slot, clears every `Horseman_` flag and the Head slot so the next try starts from phase 1. Health steps: `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Scale_Health_P2` to `_P5`, +600 maximum health each, then `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Scale_TopUp`. Music is vanilla's `Track_Z1D_Goblin_Boss`. Each turn is told through a feedback moment (`Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Unhorsed`, `_Head_Off`, `_Head_Down`, `_Head_Returned`, `_Wrath`, words `notify.hallows_eve_horseman_*`).
- **His binding row names him, shares his credit and keeps his records.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Encounters/Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter.json`: `NameKey` `npcs.Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman.name`, `Subject.TargetSlot` `Boss`. `Participation` writes `DamageToAdds` 2.5 (ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's weight for damage dealt to a fight's adds, here his Head and his Jack Skeletons), `DamageTaken` 0.5 and `Presence` 2.0 over the server rule's `DamageDealt` 1.0 and `MinShare` 0.05, so in a three-minute fight for three a player who only cuts the Head down and kills skeletons reads about 0.95 of the top share, a member who fights nothing about 0.25 and one who arrives for the last minute and fights nothing about 0.08. `Leaderboard.Bucket` `hallows_eve_headless_horseman` with `ByPartySize` true, so Records' Boss Fights ranks his wins by party size. `Feedback` names his own `Engaged`, `Defeated` and `Wiped` moments, `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_{Engaged,Defeated,Wiped}` under `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/FeedbackMoments/`: a Major broadcast to his participants in the same world within 96 blocks, at most once every 5 seconds; a Reward toast carrying the player's share and the library's discovery sting; an Info toast after a wipe whose second line teaches the head. No `Scale` (his scaling is native, one step per player) and no `Loot` (the script pays his Claims).
- **His two Claims.** `Server/Rewards/Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily.json` is a `ZigClaim` on the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily` (25 Hallow Sweets, 3 Ectoplasm and 2 festival tokens always; 30% two Spectral and 10% one Shattering Lantern Bomb), `ShareScaled` true and no `Diminish`, so the first win each day pays. `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase.json` ("The Horseman's Hoard", `TitleKey` `claim.hallows_eve_horseman_chase`) is a `ZigClaim` on `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Chase`, `ShareScaled` false, `Diminish` `[1, 0]` Daily: a roll with no `Chance` pays a Spectral Lantern Bomb every time, so a roll is never empty, and three 5% rolls pay his armour.
- **His armour.** `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Breastplate`, `_Gauntlets` and `_Greaves` under `Server/Item/Items/Hallows_Eve/` are vanilla's ancient steel pieces written whole (no `Parent`, so no crafting recipe is inherited), Epic, twice the durability, each with `DamageBonus_Fire` 5 (ZiggfreedCommon's per-type damage stat, whole percent points) in `Armor.StatModifiers`. Armour, never a weapon: a weapon's stats count only while it is held, and a player throwing a Lantern Bomb holds the bomb.
- **The graveyard, a dungeon.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Dungeons/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard.json`: `Area.Prefab` `Hallows_Eve/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard.prefab.json` (an enclosed night graveyard, 64 by 110 by 36), `EntryOffset` Y 1, `Share` five to an area and four areas to a copy, `Complete.Bosses` his script, `Rewards` the lootable `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Clear` (10 Hallow Sweets and a festival token) with `Diminish` `[1, 0.5]` Daily, `Copy.EmptyCloseSeconds` 60, `"Season": "Hallows_Eve"` and `Text` keys `dungeon.hallows_eve_graveyard.{name,description}`. The prefab is generated: `tools/arena_markers.py` writes it from the source prefab and its spec in `tools/arenas/Hallows_Eve_Graveyard.json` (the fight's encounter entity on the road south of the central plaza, his spawn marker on the plaza's north side, a vanilla `Leave_Instance` pad against the arrival room's back wall; no UUIDs, so every paste stages a fresh fight); `--check` compares, and the output is never hand-edited.
- **The gate.** `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/NpcPlacements/Hallows_Eve_Gravekeeper_Temple.json` stands the role `Hallows_Eve_Gravekeeper` (a full passive role body on the game's lantern wraith, invulnerable and still, its press-F `Hint` read literally by the engine, no `BusyStates` so entry is per player) on Old Jack's line (`Anchor.WorldSpawn` offset X -7.5, Z 4.5) in `GameplayConfig` `ForgottenTemple`, with `"Season": "Hallows_Eve"`, `Lifecycle` `KeepAlive`, `Respawn` and `Fortify`, and `"Interact": {"Open": {"Type": "Dungeon", "Dungeon": "Hallows_Eve_Graveyard"}}`. Entering is no fight; the temple stays peaceful. Old Jack's menu gains `dialogue.hallows_eve_old_jack.opt.horseman`, whose answer names the Gravekeeper and teaches the head.
- **His ladder.** `Horseman_Down`, `Horseman_Hunter` and `Horseman_Bane` under `Achievements/HallowsEvePack/_Hallows_Eve/` stand on `Hallows_Eve_Hallowed_Base` (a yearly copy each), chained as `kills_hallows_eve_horseman` tiers 1 to 3, each one `ENCOUNTER_DEFEATED` criterion on `Hallows_Eve_Headless_Horseman_Encounter` (`EXACT`, `Amount` 1, 10 and 50). Their `Rewards.Claim` pay Hallowed favor (250, 500, 1,000) and a gated `Mmo_Xp` DEFENSE row (1,000, 3,000, 8,000); `Horseman_Bane` pays the `Title` `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Bane` in `Rewards.Auto`.
- **His MMO layer.** `ZiggfreedCommon/Lootables/HallowsEvePack/Mmo_Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily.json` names `Hallows_Eve_Horseman_Daily` in `ContributesTo` and adds a roll of 1,500 DEFENSE `Mmo_Xp`, behind the top-level `hytale:mod_installed` `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` `Min 1` gate, so without the MMO the table pays exactly what its own file says.
- **Boards hosted on a quest page.** `Hallows_Eve_Daily` and `Hallows_Eve_Errands` name `Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` in `Hosts`, and `Harvest_Feast_Daily` names `Harvest_Feast_Host`, so their current contracts list on their hosts' quest pages (ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0). `Hallows_Eve_Errands`' `Text.PillKey` is `board.hallows_eve_errands.pill` ("Errand"). Old Jack's menu loses `opt.board` and `opt.errands`, and Martha's greeting answer and footer lose their board lines, her greeting answering with her quests line; those four keys leave all nine locales. Jack's once-a-day greeting, which pays a little favor and leads to his menu's "What needs doing?", stays.
- **Gear speeds favor by a percent.** The stats `ReputationGain_Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` and `ReputationGain_Hallows_Eve_Hallowed` (`Server/Entity/Stats/`, ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's `ReputationGain_<Id>` shape, whole percent points) replace the flat `Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Old_Jack` and `Reputation_Hallows_Eve_Hallowed`, and both companions drop their `Gear` leaf. The Jack Helm writes 10 and the Burning Jack Helm 20 on Old Jack's; the Jack Shield 10 and the Flaming Jack Shield 20 on the Hallowed's; the Hallowed set's tier 10 more. `itemTooltip.stats.ReputationGain_Hallows_Eve_{Old_Jack,Hallowed}` read "<Favor> Gain: {value}%"; the earn lines and the set's full-tier line say the gear earns favor faster. `Jacks_Confidant` counts earned favor alone, so the helm only speeds the climb.
- **Lantern Bombs hit other players where PvP is on.** The four `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Bomb_Burst_*` interactions and `Hallows_Eve_Lantern_Shard_Burst` each hit through one `HitEntityRules` rule with `"Matchers": []` (every entity in range) where it matched `{"Type": "Player", "Invert": true}`. `IgnoreOwner` still spares the thrower; the game's own PvP check and the Forgotten Temple's protection decide whether another player is hurt.
- **Old Jack's board pay is named.** Every Hallow's Eve board contract, errand, daily and the contract base that pays `Hallows_Eve_Board_Pay` names it through the `NameKey` `reward.lootable.hallows_eve_board_pay` ("Old Jack's Extras"), so a reward notice heads its rows with that name instead of "Loot Bundle".
- **ru-RU.** Martha's first quest reads "Ужин в храме", the temple lower-case as the game writes it.
