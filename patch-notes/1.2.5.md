Harvest Feast is on now, until December 6! Martha the Cook has more for you: the Turkey Graveyard and its Great Turkey, a supper round after The Feast and a trophy to take home, a currency of her own, three dishes of her own to cook, a set of clothes to earn, gifts as she gets to know you, a daily job from each of her helpers, four new board jobs, and as many turkeys as you care to hunt.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.1 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer.

## New

- Added the Turkey Graveyard, a party fight while the feast runs. Every Extra Turkeys you hand Martha pays a Wishbone; give one to Bram the Shepherd and he takes you and your party into a graveyard where the hunted turkeys' souls won't rest. Put down the waves of Turkey Souls, beat the Great Turkey and set them free. It pays every time you win, sometimes with a Wishbone back, and more on your first win each day.
- Added a supper round after The Feast: Supper for Tomas, Supper for Elsie and Supper for Bram. Once you've sat down to The Feast, Martha sends a plate of the leftovers to each of her helpers around the Forgotten Temple. They come back every year with The Feast.
- Added the Feast Trophy, a turkey with the year on it to place in your home. Martha gives you one when you sit down to The Feast, in place of the plate.
- Added a celebration when you collect The Feast: a banner with a fanfare for everyone at the table, and a firework over your head.
- Added a daily job from each of Martha's helpers in the Forgotten Temple: Tomas the Miller wants flour, Elsie the Weaver wants spices and Bram the Shepherd wants eggs. Talk to them once you've done Martha's first quest. Each pays Harvest Coins and Martha's favor.
- Added Harvest Coins, the feast's own currency. Every one of Martha's quests and board jobs pays them, and her shop's own shelves take them. Unspent coins keep until next year's feast.
- Added the Harvest Set: the Harvest Gloves, Hat, Trousers and Tunic, sold at Martha's shop for Harvest Coins as her favor grows, from Neighbor up to Head of the Table. Each piece adds health and stamina and earns her favor 5% faster. Wear two pieces for more health, and all four for more again and another 10% favor. With MMO Skill Tree the set also adds Harvesting XP and Harvesting luck.
- Added three dishes of Martha's own, each better than any food in the game: Turkey Pie for more max health, Harvest Salad for more max stamina, and Martha's Roast for both, with Martha's favor coming faster while it lasts. Her shop sells their recipes for Harvest Coins, the Salad's once she counts you a Neighbor and the Roast's once you're Family. Cook them at a Chef's Stove.
- Added Extra Turkeys, a quest you can take from Martha's quest list as often as you like once Turkey Hunt is done. Every turkey you hunt while the feast runs earns her favor too, a Wild Turkey the most.
- Added gifts from Martha as she gets to know you. Martha's Neighbor, Martha's Friend and Martha's Family are new achievements, and Head of the Table now pays a gift beside its title. Collect them on the achievements page: Harvest Coins, hay, a Scarecrow, pies, and with MMO Skill Tree an XP boost.
- Added the Feast Basket. Martha's favor keeps counting past Head of the Table, and every 5,000 more packs you a basket of pies, Harvest Coins and Festival Tokens to collect on the Reputation page.
- Added four jobs to the Feast Board: the Pumpkin Job and the Carrot Job in the fields, the Vegetable Job and the Spice Job in the kitchen.
- Added a tip from each of Martha's helpers. Ask Tomas the Miller how to make dough, or Elsie the Weaver where spices come from. Bram the Shepherd knows turkeys.
- Added a Bundle of Hay and a Scarecrow to Martha's shop, and an achievement for wearing the whole Harvest Set.

## Changes

- Harvest Feast runs until December 6 now, five days longer.
- Martha's shop takes Harvest Coins for her recipes, popcorn, corn seeds and decorations now. Its Festival Shelf still takes Festival Tokens.
- Martha's shop sells the recipes for her own dishes in place of the game's Apple Pie, Meat Pie and Caesar Salad. A recipe you already learned stays learned.
- Martha, Tomas, Elsie and Bram look like proper villagers now, and Martha carries a lantern.
- The game's own turkeys count for Martha's turkey quests, the Turkey Job and Turkey Chaser now, as well as Wild Turkeys.
- Once you've done the supper round, Martha tells you her story is done till next year and what's left to do.
- Turkey Hunt now gives you a Dough and some Spices with the pumpkin pie recipe, so your first pie only needs a pumpkin.
- Martha says plainly what to do and where: wheat on the plains of the Emerald Wilds, carrots in its forests, pumpkins in its autumn woods, meat and vegetables on a Campfire, pies on a Chef's Stove.
- Her quests have plainer names. Supper in the Temple is now Meet Martha, her story runs from Wheat Harvest to The Feast, and the dailies are Daily Turkeys, Daily Wheat and Daily Roast. The board posts the Turkey Job, the Wheat Job, the Pie Job and the Cooking Job. Your progress carries over.
- Favor at the Feast is now Martha's Favor, and Known Face is now Friend.
- A Seat at the Table is now Feast Guest. It pays Harvest Coins, and so do Turkey Chaser, Full Baskets and The Full Table.

## Fixes

- Fixed the feast's quest steps reading in the singular where they asked for several, in every language.
- Fixed Martha's line for after the feast, which never showed.
- Fixed Martha's table standing in the path from the Forgotten Temple's portal. She sets it up at the side of the hall now.

We'll see you at the table!
