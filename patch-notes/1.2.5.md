Harvest Feast is on now, until December 1! Martha the Cook has a long table set up in the Forgotten Temple, with quests, a daily board, a shop and Wild Turkeys out in the autumn woods. Seasons of Orbis 1.2.5 is out now with a few fixes for it.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.1 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer.

## Harvest Feast

- A quest in your log, Supper in the Temple, sends you to Martha through any Forgotten Temple Gateway. Her welcome gives you an Objective Book, which opens your quests, achievements and the Almanac.
- Her four quests run from Wheat for the Ovens to The Long Table, the feast itself, which pays a Feast Plate with the year on it and comes back every Harvest Feast.
- She has three dailies, plus The Shared Table every day and The Guest List once a feast. Tomas the Miller, Elsie the Weaver and Bram the Shepherd stand around the temple, so there's always somebody to invite.
- The Feast Board posts a Field job and a Kitchen job every day, and a second of each once Martha gets to know you.
- Her shop takes Festival Tokens for pie and salad recipes, popcorn, corn seeds and autumn leaves. Its Festival Shelf has the winter decorations, a Snowman among them.
- Wild Turkeys roam the first zone's autumn woods, forests and plains by day, and pumpkins you pick by hand sometimes come with Spices for your pie.

## Fixes

- The feast's quest steps now read right in every language, and Martha's line for after the feast shows.

We'll see you at the table!
