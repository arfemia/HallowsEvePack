Seasons of Orbis 1.3.0 is out now! Christmas comes to the Forgotten Temple from December 2 to January 10. Santa needs a hand getting his presents back, his three helpers have work for you every day, and something big has moved into a cave up in the snow.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.5.3 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with it beside the pack Christmas trains your skills too. Everything new plays in all nine of the pack's languages.

## CHRISTMAS IS HERE!

**Santa** has set up by his tree on the north side of the Forgotten Temple's hall. Trorks in Santa hats robbed his sleigh on the road and ran off with the presents. A quest in your log, **Meet Santa**, sends you to him through any Forgotten Temple Gateway, and its pay includes an Objective Book.

Tomas the Miller, Elsie the Weaver and Bram the Shepherd stay on after the Harvest Feast to help him. Until the feast ends on December 6 they keep their feast spots and clothes, and their feast conversations gain "What's on your Christmas stall?" and "Any Christmas work for me?". From December 7, Tomas and Elsie set up beside Santa in winter clothes, and Bram changes into his on the meadow.

## Catch the Present Thieves

**Present Thieves** are Trorks in Santa hats. They come out at night, away from torches, in the woods and fields of the Emerald Wilds, and any time in the snow of the Whisperfrost Frontiers. About one in three still carries a **Christmas Present**: right-click it for a few Christmas Coins and a little surprise. Any other Trork you bring down while Christmas runs counts for Santa too.

## Santa's story

Catch the thieves, then feed Bram's reindeer, bake **Gingerbread Cookies** with Tomas and wrap presents with Elsie. The last step, **Christmas Eve**, fills Santa's sleigh, celebrates with a banner and a firework, and pays a **Christmas Ornament** with the year on it to hang in your home. Christmas Eve comes back every year.

From December 25 it's **Christmas Morning**, and a present waits for everyone under Santa's tree, with a Santa Suit inside.

## Every day at the temple

Santa has Daily Thieves and Daily Presents, and each helper has a daily of their own: dough for Tomas, Plant Fiber for Elsie, carrots for Bram. Each helper also posts one job a day. **The Christmas Board** at Santa posts an outdoor job and a workshop job, with more as you climb his Nice List. Extra Thieves is there as often as you like, and **Santa's Stocking** is free at his stall once a day.

Pick "Sing a carol" when you talk to anyone but Santa, once a day each, for a candy cane.

## Give a present

Hold a Christmas Present, look at a friend and left-click. They get **A Present from a Friend**, a better gift than the one inside, and **Christmas Cheer**, which climbs them up Santa's Nice List faster for half an hour, and you climb it a little too. It works with PvP off and inside the Forgotten Temple. Santa and his helpers take a present the same way when a quest asks for one, and a present pointed at anyone else stays in your hand.

## Santa's Nice List

**Santa's Nice List** runs from Newcomer through On the List, Nice and Very Nice to Santa's Favorite. His quests and jobs raise it, and so do thieves, carols, presents and the Yeti. Each step up has an achievement with a gift to collect, and Santa's Favorite comes with the title "Santa's Favorite". Past the top it keeps counting, and every 5,000 more packs you a **Santa's Sack**.

## Christmas Coins and four shops

**Christmas Coins** are Christmas's own currency. Every Christmas quest and job pays them, and unspent coins keep until next Christmas. Spend them at Santa's Stall on Christmas Packets and candy canes, at Tomas's Bakery on the recipes for Gingerbread Cookies, **Hot Cocoa** and **Christmas Cake**, at Elsie's Stall on presents, the **Christmas Wand**, the **Santa Suit** and the **Christmas Set**, and at Bram's Snowballs, where a bundle of snowballs is free every day. Each shop has the Festival Shelf too.

Cook the treats at a Chef's Stove. Cookies make you faster, cocoa keeps the cold out and your health coming back, and the cake does both and climbs you up the Nice List faster. The Christmas Set is a hat, coat, gloves and trousers that open as Santa gets to know you: each piece adds health and stamina, keeps out a little cold and earns his favor faster, and the whole set does more again.

## INTO THE YETI CAVE

Bring Bram's reindeer their carrots and he sells you **Yeti Bait**. Hand him one and he leads you, alone or with up to four friends, into **the Yeti Cave**. The more of you there are, the tougher the Yeti gets. Back away when a ring lights up around him, because every third attack he slams the ground. Below 60% of his health he throws snowballs that land on rings under your feet and slow you down, and near the end two White Wolves come to help him.

Every win leaves a chest with Christmas Coins, Festival Tokens, Essence of Ice, two Christmas Presents and Nice List favor, half as much after your first clear each day. Your first win each day adds his daily Claim and his Boss Kill Claim, and only the Boss Kill Claim holds the **Yeti Fur Hood** and the **Yeti Fur Coat**, which keep out the cold. Die in the cave and you keep your items.

## Snowball fight!

Throw a **Snowball** at a friend and they slow down for a moment. It never hurts, and it works anywhere, the Forgotten Temple included.

## Wait... There's More!?

Chop trees while Christmas runs and now and then a Winter Bauble falls out of the branches. The Christmas Wand turns a friend into one of Santa's elves or a reindeer for three minutes, and Elsie gives you an **Elf Suit** for helping her wrap presents.

Tomas, Elsie and Bram each keep a secret. Become their apprentice to earn it: Tomas's own **Christmas Loaf**, Elsie's **Reindeer Suit**, and a little extra from Bram in every Christmas Present you open.

Fourteen Christmas achievements, with the titles "the Merrymaker" and "Santa's Favorite", and a Christmas page in the Almanac with your tallies and every item to find. The Christmas Ornament counts toward the achievements for keepsakes from different seasons.

With MMO Skill Tree, Christmas pays skill XP for the work it asks, the helpers' jobs become Crafting, Building and Woodcutting jobs, Woodcutting and Crafting XP run a quarter faster all Christmas, and every skill does on Christmas Day and New Year's Eve.

Merry Christmas, and we'll see you by the tree!

## The full changelog

### Christmas

- Added Christmas, from December 2 to January 10 every year, with a banner as it starts and as it ends. Christmas Morning runs from December 25 to January 10 inside it, with a banner of its own.
- Added Santa, by his tree on the north side of the Forgotten Temple's hall, with a snowman and a pile of packets beside it. Press F to talk to him. Ask him where the thieves are, where his helpers are and who he is, see your standing on the Nice List, open his stall, his quests and the Almanac, and stop by once a day for a word and a little favor.
- Tomas the Miller, Elsie the Weaver and Bram the Shepherd help Santa from December 2. While Harvest Feast runs they keep their feast spots, clothes and conversations, and two new lines open their Christmas stall and their Christmas quests. Once the feast is over, Tomas and Elsie stand beside Santa with a table each, Bram stays on his meadow, and all three wear winter clothes and talk about Christmas. Your quests with them carry on through the change.

### Present Thieves

- Added the Present Thief, a Trork in a Santa hat who fights with his fists. One or two come out at night away from torches in the Emerald Wilds' woods, plains and autumn forests, and any time in the Whisperfrost Frontiers' tundra and forests, only while Christmas runs.
- About one in three drops a Christmas Present.
- Every Christmas quest, job and achievement that asks for Present Thieves also counts the game's grown Trorks.

### Santa's story

- Added Meet Santa, which starts on its own once Christmas does. It pays an Objective Book, 10 Christmas Coins and 2 Festival Tokens.
- Added Present Thieves: catch 5 Present Thieves for Santa. It also pays a Christmas Present.
- Added Reindeer Food: bring Bram 10 carrots, picked in the Emerald Wilds' forests. It pays 16 snowballs, and the Gingerbread Cookie recipe with a Dough and some Spices from Tomas.
- Added Christmas Cookies: bake a Gingerbread Cookie at a Chef's Stove and bring it to Tomas.
- Added Wrapping Presents: bring Elsie 10 Plant Fiber and give Bram a Christmas Present. It pays 3 Christmas Presents and an Elf Suit, and turns you into one of Santa's elves for three minutes.
- Added Christmas Eve: bring Santa 3 Gingerbread Cookies and 10 carrots, and give him a Christmas Present. It pays the Christmas Ornament with the year on it, 40 Christmas Coins, 5 Festival Tokens, 2 Gingerbread Cookies and a Hot Cocoa, with a 25% chance at a Winter Bauble, and plays a banner and a firework for everyone near you. It comes back every Christmas.
- Added Christmas Morning, from December 25: open your present under Santa's tree for 50 Christmas Coins, 3 Festival Tokens, a Small Red Dotted Christmas Packet and a Santa Suit. It comes back every Christmas Morning.
- Added Christmas Carols, once a Christmas: sing a carol to 3 people, then tell Santa. It pays 25 Christmas Coins and 4 Festival Tokens.
- Present Thieves, Reindeer Food, Christmas Cookies and Wrapping Presents each pay 15 Christmas Coins, 2 Festival Tokens and 2 candy canes, with a 25% chance at a Gingerbread Cookie.

### Every day

- Added Santa's Daily Thieves (catch 4 Present Thieves) and Daily Presents (give Tomas, Elsie and Bram a Christmas Present each).
- Added a daily from each helper: Daily Baking (2 Dough for Tomas), Daily Wrapping (10 Plant Fiber for Elsie, who wraps you 2 presents for it) and Daily Carrots (10 carrots for Bram, who adds 16 snowballs).
- Every daily pays 6 Christmas Coins and a Festival Token, with a 20% chance at a Gingerbread Cookie.
- Added Extra Thieves: catch 10 Present Thieves for 6 Christmas Coins, as often as you like.
- Added The Christmas Board at Santa, fresh every day. It posts one Outdoor Work job (the Thief Job, Carrot Job, Log Job or Egg Job) and one Workshop Work job (the Cookie Job, Dough Job, Present Job or Spice Job). A second outdoor job opens at On the List and a second workshop job at Nice. Each job pays 8 Christmas Coins and a Festival Token, with a 25% chance at 2 candy canes and a 10% chance at a Christmas Present.
- Added a job board for each helper, one job a day: Tomas's Jobs, Elsie's Jobs and Bram's Jobs. Without MMO Skill Tree each posts an errand, Wheat for the Mill or Bread for the Table for Tomas, a Decorating Errand or Wool for Stockings for Elsie, and a Log Errand or a Wolf Errand for Bram. Each pays 4 Christmas Coins.
- Added "Sing a carol" to every character's conversation but Santa's. Each character listens once a day, for a candy cane and a little Nice List favor.
- Added Santa's Stocking, free once a day at Santa's Stall: 3 Christmas Coins, candy canes or a Gingerbread Cookie, and a 10% chance at a Christmas Present.

### Presents and snowballs

- Added the Christmas Present. Right-click it to open it for 3 Christmas Coins and one of: 2 candy canes, a Gingerbread Cookie, a Hot Cocoa, 8 snowballs, a Winter Bauble, or a small green or white Christmas Packet.
- Left-click a friend with a present to give it to them. They get A Present from a Friend (6 Christmas Coins and one of: 4 candy canes, 2 Gingerbread Cookies, a Hot Cocoa, 16 snowballs, a Winter Bauble, or a small green or white Christmas Packet) and Christmas Cheer, 20% more Nice List favor for 30:00. You get 10 Nice List favor. Only a present that reaches a friend, Santa, Tomas, Elsie or Bram is spent.
- Added the Snowball. Throw it and whoever it hits is slowed for 2 seconds, with no damage, PvP or not.

### Santa's Nice List and Christmas Coins

- Added Santa's Nice List: Newcomer, On the List, Nice, Very Nice and Santa's Favorite. A Present Thief earns 3 favor and any other Trork 1 while Christmas runs.
- Added a gift at each step, collected on the achievements page: On the List (30 Christmas Coins, 2 Christmas Presents, 8 candy canes), Nice (60 Christmas Coins, a small green Christmas Packet, 16 snowballs), Very Nice (120 Christmas Coins, 4 Gingerbread Cookies, 4 Hot Cocoas) and Santa's Favorite (250 Christmas Coins and the title "Santa's Favorite").
- Added Santa's Sack, every 5,000 favor past Santa's Favorite: 40 Christmas Coins, 2 Festival Tokens, 2 Christmas Presents and 2 Gingerbread Cookies, with a 25% chance at a Small Red Dotted Christmas Packet.
- Added Christmas Coins, Christmas's own currency. Unspent coins keep until next Christmas.

### The Christmas shops

- Added Santa's Stall: the Small Christmas Packet, Small Green Christmas Packet and Small White Christmas Packet at 25 Christmas Coins each, and 4 candy canes for 6, twice a day.
- Added Tomas's Bakery: the Gingerbread Cookie Recipe (40 Christmas Coins), the Hot Cocoa Recipe (60, at Nice), the Christmas Cake Recipe (100, at Very Nice), and 2 Dough for 10 once a day.
- Added Elsie's Stall: the Christmas Present (15 Christmas Coins, five a day), the Christmas Wand (80, at On the List), the Santa Suit (150, at Very Nice), and the Christmas Set: the Christmas Gloves (120, at On the List), Christmas Hat (160, at Nice), Christmas Trousers (200, at Very Nice) and Christmas Coat (240, at Santa's Favorite).
- Added Bram's Snowballs: Free Snowballs, 16 once a day, 16 more for 2 Christmas Coins, and Yeti Bait for 25 once you've finished Reindeer Food.
- Every Christmas shop carries the Festival Shelf.

### Food and gear

- Added the Gingerbread Cookie: restores 5% health and makes you 15% faster for 10:00. Its recipe bakes 3 at a time from dough and spices.
- Added Hot Cocoa: restores 5% health and gives Health Regen III and 40% less ice damage for 10:00. Make it from a tankard of water, tree sap and spices, and you keep the tankard.
- Added the Christmas Cake: restores 5% health and gives all of the above, plus 10% more Nice List favor, for 10:00. Bake it from dough, eggs and tree sap.
- Added the Christmas Set: the Christmas Hat, Coat, Gloves and Trousers, cotton pieces that each add health and stamina, 5% Nice List favor and 5% less ice damage. Two pieces add 5 health, and all four add 10 health, another 10% Nice List favor and more cold protection.
- Added the Santa Suit and the Elf Suit: right-click one to look like Santa or one of his elves for 30:00. /zigcostume off takes it off sooner.
- Added the Christmas Wand: point it at a friend to turn them into one of Santa's elves or a reindeer for three minutes.
- Added the Christmas Ornament, a glass bauble for your tree with its year on it.
- A Winter Bauble falls out of a tree you chop 5% of the time while Christmas runs.

### The Yeti Cave

- Added the Yeti Cave, a party fight while Christmas runs. Hand Bram a Yeti Bait to go in, alone or with up to four friends. The Yeti has 1,000 health alone and 500 more for each friend with you.
- He swipes with his claws, and every third attack he slams the ground inside a ring that lights up around him first.
- Below 60% health, every 8 seconds a ring lights up under each of you and a big snowball lands on it, with ice damage and a 3-second slow. Below 30%, two White Wolves join him and the snowballs come every 6 seconds.
- Health packs drop now and then while you fight. If everyone falls or leaves, he's back at full health for the next try.
- A red Christmas Packet in the cave holds 8 to 16 snowballs, often a Christmas Present and sometimes Essence of Ice, full again every time you go in.
- Every clear pays 20 Christmas Coins, 2 Festival Tokens, 2 to 3 Essence of Ice, 2 Christmas Presents and 150 Nice List favor in a chest where he falls, half after your first clear each day.
- Your first win each day adds his daily Claim (15 Christmas Coins, 2 Festival Tokens and a Hot Cocoa, with a 50% chance at 2 Gingerbread Cookies, by your share of the fight) and his Boss Kill Claim (8 Christmas Coins and a Festival Token, with a 16.7% chance at the Yeti Fur Hood and a 10% chance at the Yeti Fur Coat).
- Added the Yeti Fur Hood (15% less ice damage, 5% physical and projectile protection, 15 health) and the Yeti Fur Coat (20% less ice damage, 9% physical and projectile protection, 30 health).
- Added Yeti Hunter, for beating him once each Christmas, which pays 25 Christmas Coins.
- Die in the cave and you keep your items.

### The helpers' secrets

- Added Tomas's Apprentice, once you've finished Christmas Cookies: bake 20 Christmas treats. It opens Tomas's Christmas Loaf Recipe at his bakery for 60 Christmas Coins. The loaf restores 5% health and makes you faster and warmer for 10:00, and bakes 2 at a time from dough and tree sap.
- Added Elsie's Apprentice, once you've finished Wrapping Presents: put up 30 Christmas decorations at your home. It opens the Reindeer Suit at her stall for 120 Christmas Coins, which makes you one of Santa's reindeer for 30:00.
- Added Bram's Apprentice, once you've finished Reindeer Food: chop 40 logs. From then on every Christmas Present you open also holds a Christmas decoration or the Snowflake Tree Topper.
- Each apprenticeship pays 20 Christmas Coins.

### Achievements, titles and the Almanac

- Added nine achievements that come round each Christmas: Christmas Ornament, Present Hunter (50 Present Thieves), Gift Giver (Daily Presents 5 times), Carol Singer, Christmas Decorator (10 decorations at home), Secret Santa (10 presents to other players), Snowball Fight (50 snowballs), Yeti Hunter, and The Merrymaker, with the title "the Merrymaker", for the first five in one Christmas.
- Added five you keep for good: On the List, Nice, Very Nice, Santa's Favorite and Christmas Set, for wearing all four pieces.
- The Christmas Ornament counts as Christmas's keepsake toward Season to Season, Round the Year and Seasons of Orbis.
- Added a Christmas page to the Almanac: your Christmas Ornaments, tallies of Present Thieves caught, presents opened and given, carols sung, snowballs thrown and Yetis beaten, a banner for the Yeti, and every item to find with where it comes from.

### With MMO Skill Tree

- Christmas's quests, jobs and achievements pay XP in the skill the work trains: Defense for thieves and the Yeti, Crafting for baking and presents, Harvesting for carrots and eggs, Woodcutting for logs, Building for decorating, Acrobatics for the walks and carols, and Artillery for snowballs.
- Each helper's board posts a skill job: a Crafting Job from Tomas, a Building Job from Elsie and a Woodcutting Job from Bram. Earn 1,250 XP in that skill for 4 Christmas Coins and 750 XP.
- Woodcutting and Crafting XP run a quarter faster while Christmas runs, and every skill does on Christmas Day and New Year's Eve.
- The Christmas Set adds Woodcutting XP at two pieces, and more Woodcutting XP and Woodcutting luck at four. Your luck raises the Winter Bauble's chance, up to 25%.
- Reach level 20 in Crafting, Building or Woodcutting to open Tomas's, Elsie's or Bram's secret without the apprenticeship.
- Present Thieves give 65 kill XP, the Yeti 400 and his wolves 65.
- The Nice List gifts and The Merrymaker add an XP boost for every skill, and Christmas Morning's present adds a 1.5x boost for two hours.

### Without MMO Skill Tree

- The Nice List gifts and The Merrymaker give a Hot Cocoa where the XP boost would be, and Christmas Morning adds A Bag of Treats: 4 candy canes, 3 Gingerbread Cookies, 2 Hot Cocoas or a whole Christmas Cake.

### Changes

- The festival shelf's winter decorations stay on sale until January 10, four days longer.

### For server owners

- Christmas, Christmas Morning, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve are calendar events (`Christmas`, `Christmas_Morning`, `Christmas_Day`, `Christmas_New_Years_Eve`) that `mods/ziggfreedcommon/calendar.json` can switch off or move.
- The Yeti Cave is a CommonLib dungeon, `Christmas_Yeti_Cave`: switch it off or change how many players share it in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json`. `/zigdungeon enter Christmas_Yeti_Cave` takes you in alone while Christmas runs, without spending a bait.
