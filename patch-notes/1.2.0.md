Seasons of Orbis 1.2.0 is out now! The Headless Horseman rides through Hallow's Eve in a graveyard of his own, with a hard ride for anyone who comes back, and Old Jack hands out all his work from one list.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.4.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.2]] beside it the Horseman pays Defense and Artillery XP. Everything new plays in all nine of the pack's languages.

## THE HEADLESS HORSEMAN RIDES

The **Headless Horseman** has come for Hallow's Eve. Buy a **Grave Candle** at Old Jack's shop in the Forgotten Temple and talk to the **Gravekeeper** beside him: he burns it to open the Horseman's Graveyard for you and up to four friends, and everyone who follows you in walks in free. The more of you there are, the tougher he gets.

He starts in the saddle, diving at you and circling the fountain while volleys of giant Lantern Bombs fall on the glowing rings under your feet. When his horse rears up screaming and glowing, get off the ring he's marked, because he's about to swoop through it, and when a whole row of rings lights up he's about to thunder across the graveyard dropping bombs. Knock him down to half his health and he's thrown from his horse to fight on foot with two Jack Skeletons. He slams the ground inside a glowing ring and charges after a glowing wind-up and a war cry. Then his head flies off and a shield wraps around him: he can't be hurt until you cut the head down, and if you leave it alone it flies back and heals him. Down to his last fifth, he burns with rage, hits harder, calls two more Jack Skeletons and his volleys leave the ground on fire.

Health potions drop into the graveyard now and then to keep you on your feet, and if his fire gets to you, Old Jack sells a **Fire Resistance Potion** too. Not sure what you're in for? Ask Old Jack about the Horseman.

## The hard ride

Finish **The Rider**, Old Jack's new quest, and the Gravekeeper will take you on the **hard ride** for a **Burning Grave Candle**, sold at Old Jack's shop once he counts you a Regular. The Horseman has about twice the health, hits harder, throws bombs at you from the saddle and sets the ground alight from his very first volley, and no potions drop to bail you out. He pays better for it, and keeps a few things he drops nowhere else.

## The Horseman's Hoard

Your first win each day pays his daily Claim and the **Horseman's Hoard**, both waiting in a chest where he falls. The Claim brings Hallow Sweets, Ectoplasm, Festival Tokens and a chance at his Breastplate, Gauntlets or Greaves. The Hoard always holds a Spectral Lantern Bomb and favor with Old Jack, and sometimes a Grave Candle back or a piece of the new **Flaming Hallowed set**. The hard ride keeps a Hoard of its own, with a handful of candles, his Flaming Breastplate and now and then an **Eternal Candle**, which opens the normal graveyard for your party and never burns down. Win on both rides and both pay the same day.

Bring him down 1, 10 and 50 times for The Horseman Falls, Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane, and the title that comes with the last. Cut his head down 10 times for Off With His Head, bring him down with nothing but Lantern Bombs for **By Lantern Light** (Lamplighter on the hard ride), and beat him this year for **First Ride**, a badge you can only earn in his first Hallow's Eve.

## Dress for the fire

Wear the Horseman's armour with a Jack Helm and a Jack Shield and the **Hallowed set** grows with it, for more health and harder fire hits. Trade up to the Burning Jack Helm and the Flaming Jack Shield, add his flaming pieces, and you're wearing the **Flaming Hallowed set**, stronger again. Both Jack Shields now ward off some of his fire too, and four new achievements are yours the moment you put a set on.

## Old Jack's story

Old Jack's story now reads as one tale, and the Horseman rides right through it. **The Rider** sends you into the graveyard every Hallow's Eve, then **Fire for the Lantern** sends you back on the hard ride for the Horseman's Ember, and that ember lights your Hollow Lantern. The lantern now comes as The Hollow Lantern quest's own pay, with the year still on it.

## Old Jack's one list

Ask Old Jack "What needs doing?" and **one list** shows his quests, today's board jobs and his errands, each tagged Bounty or Errand with its grade. Take them from Jack, hand them in to Jack, collect your pay from Jack.

## Wait... There's More!?

Hallow's Eve's page in the **Almanac** now shows the Headless Horseman: where to find him, what his Hoard pays, and how many times you and the whole server have brought him down. His wins also rank by party size on the Bosses tab in Records. Old Jack's rewards say what they are and list what they pay when you hover them, and Hallow Sweets tell you what they're for.

We'll see you in the graveyard!

## The full changelog

### The Headless Horseman

- Added the Headless Horseman, who rides in his own graveyard during Hallow's Eve. Talk to the Gravekeeper beside Old Jack in the Forgotten Temple to go in. Going in costs a Grave Candle from Old Jack's shop, one per party: whoever opens the gate pays on a confirm screen, and the rest of the party follows free. Up to five players share a graveyard, and his health grows with each player in the fight.
- Added the Gravekeeper beside Old Jack. Ask him to take you into the Horseman's Graveyard, ask him who he is, or ask him for a trick or treat. Once you've finished The Rider, he also takes you on the hard ride.
- In the saddle he dives at you, circles the fountain, swoops through a spot he rings first and drops a row of bombs across the graveyard after lighting every ring in it, and every 6 seconds a volley falls on rings marked under each player. At half his health he's thrown and fights on foot with two Jack Skeletons.
- On foot he slams the ground inside a ring that lights up around him, and charges after a glowing wind-up and a war cry. A wall stops his charge and stuns him.
- His head flies off soon after he's thrown, and again 40 seconds after each flight. While it flies he's wrapped in a shield and can't be hurt until it's killed. A head left alone for 25 seconds flies back to him and heals him.
- At a fifth of his health, once his head has flown at least once, he turns to Wrath: he moves faster and hits harder, two more Jack Skeletons join him, and a burning volley falls every 8 seconds, leaving fire on the ground.
- Now and then a health potion drops into the graveyard while you fight him, though never on the hard ride. Walk into it to heal.
- He takes half damage from fire, Lantern Bombs included.
- If everyone in the fight falls or leaves, he's back at full health for the next try.
- Added the Horseman's own lines to his fight: a banner as he rises that tells you to keep off the glowing rings and cut his head down, a notice with your share of the fight when he falls, and a notice after a wipe that explains his head. The banner only shows to your party and anyone in your graveyard.
- When you enter the graveyard, on either ride, the camera sweeps over the graves toward his fight, then hands control back to you.
- You arrive in the graveyard facing into it, and once the camera is yours again a notice says what the fight needs: beat the Horseman, and cut his head down when it flies off.
- Added a chest hidden somewhere in the graveyard, on either ride. It holds a Lantern Bomb or two and sometimes a Grave Candle, whoever in your party opens it first takes what's inside, and it's full again every time you go in.
- The graveyard opens only while Hallow's Eve runs. Outside it, the Gravekeeper is gone.

### The hard ride

- Added the Headless Horseman's hard ride. Once you've finished The Rider, ask the Gravekeeper for the hard ride. Going in costs a Burning Grave Candle, one per party.
- On the hard ride he has about twice the health and hits harder, throws bombs at you from the saddle, drops a volley every 4 seconds that leaves fire from the start, and his head flies off again 28 seconds after each flight. No health potions drop there.
- Added the hard ride's own daily Claim. Your first hard win each day pays 40 Hallow Sweets, 5 Ectoplasm and 4 Festival Tokens, with a 50% chance at two Spectral Lantern Bombs, a 20% chance at a Shattering Lantern Bomb and a 25% chance each at his Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves.
- Clearing the hard graveyard pays its own Claim of 20 Hallow Sweets and 2 Festival Tokens, in full the first time each day and half after that.
- A normal win and a hard win each pay on the same day.
- Wins and head kills on the hard ride count toward every Horseman achievement and Almanac tally.

### His rewards

- Added his daily Claim. Your first win each day pays 25 Hallow Sweets, 3 Ectoplasm and 2 Festival Tokens, with a 30% chance at two Spectral Lantern Bombs, a 10% chance at a Shattering Lantern Bomb and a 15% chance each at his Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves.
- Added a share of his daily Claim for everyone who fights him, by what you did: the damage you deal him, his Head and his Jack Skeletons, the damage you take and your time in the fight. Cutting his head down and killing his skeletons count the most.
- Added the Horseman's Hoard, paid on your first win each day beside his daily Claim. Each ride has its own. The normal ride's always holds a Spectral Lantern Bomb and 100 favor with Old Jack, with a 50% chance at a Grave Candle, a 20% chance each at a Tombstone and a Lost Civilization Lantern like the ones in his graveyard, and a 5% chance each at the Burning Jack Helm, the Flaming Jack Shield and his Flaming Gauntlets and Greaves.
- The hard ride's Hoard always holds two Spectral Lantern Bombs, 1 to 4 Grave Candles and 150 favor with the Hallowed, with a 10% chance each at the Burning Jack Helm, the Flaming Jack Shield, his three flaming pieces and an Eternal Candle.
- When he falls, your daily Claim and Hoard wait for you in a chest where he fell. Only your share shows, and anything you leave in it waits under Claims in your menu.
- Added the Horseman's Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves, from his daily Claim. Each adds a little health and makes your fire hits 3% stronger, Lantern Bombs included.
- Added the Flaming Horseman's Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves, found in the Horseman's Hoard, and the breastplate only on the hard ride. They ward off fire and make your fire hits 8% stronger each.
- Added the Eternal Candle, found now and then in the hard ride's Hoard. Carry it and the Gravekeeper opens the normal graveyard for your party without burning a Grave Candle, and you keep it. The hard ride still takes a Burning Grave Candle.
- Clearing the graveyard pays its own Claim of 10 Hallow Sweets and a Festival Token, in full the first time each day and half after that.

### Gear and sets

- Added the Flaming Hallowed set: the Burning Jack Helm, the Flaming Jack Shield and the Horseman's three flaming pieces. Wear the helm and his flaming armour for more health and harder fire hits, and carry the Flaming Jack Shield in your off hand for more again.
- The Hallowed set now takes in the Horseman's armour: with a helm, his breastplate, gauntlets and greaves give more health and harder fire hits, and a Jack Shield in your off hand adds more again. Every flaming piece counts toward it too.
- The Jack Shield now resists 5% of fire damage and the Flaming Jack Shield 10%, burns included, whether you hold it or carry it in your off hand.
- Hallow's Eve gear now raises the favor you earn by a percent, where it added favor of its own while you wore it. The Jack Helm raises Old Jack's Favor by 10% and the Burning Jack Helm by 20%. The Jack Shield raises the Hallowed's by 10% and the Flaming Jack Shield by 20%, and any piece of the Hallowed set worn with a shield in your off hand adds another 10%.
- Hover a piece to see its number, such as "Old Jack's Favor Gain: 10%". Your Reputation page tells you how much your gear raises each reputation, and taking the gear off never lowers favor you earned.

### Achievements and records

- Added The Horseman Falls, Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane, for 1, 10 and 50 wins in one Hallow's Eve. They pay 250, 500 and 1,000 favor with the Hallowed, and Horseman's Bane also gives the title "Horseman's Bane".
- The Hallowed counts your first win over the Headless Horseman. Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane are a ladder of their own.
- Added Off With His Head, under Hallow's Eve in your achievements: cut down the Horseman's head 10 times in one Hallow's Eve for 250 favor with the Hallowed.
- Added First Ride, a badge for bringing down the Headless Horseman during Hallow's Eve 2026, his first year. It can't be earned after this Hallow's Eve.
- Added By Lantern Light, a Hallow's Eve achievement: bring down the Headless Horseman with nothing but Lantern Bombs hitting him. His Head and the Jack Skeletons can be hit with anything, and everyone in the fight earns it.
- Added Lamplighter, the same feat on the hard ride, which gives the title Lamplighter.
- Added four achievements for wearing Hallow's Eve's sets: Hallowed Armour, Fully Hallowed, Flaming Armour and Fully Ablaze. Each is earned once and kept, the moment you put the pieces on.
- Added the Headless Horseman to the Bosses tab on the Records page, under Seasonal, with his wins ranked by party size. The hard ride has its own entry.

### Old Jack

- Old Jack's "What needs doing?" now opens one list with his quests, today's board jobs and his errands, each tagged Bounty or Errand with its grade. You take, hand in and collect them all at Jack, and a job you took at his board can be handed in to him too. His menu no longer has separate board and errands lines.
- Martha does the same with the Feast Board when Harvest Feast comes around.
- Added The Rider to Old Jack's story, a quest that comes back every Hallow's Eve: buy a Grave Candle at his shop and bring down the Headless Horseman, in either order, then report back to Jack. He asks you about it once Pumpkin Patch is done.
- Added Fire for the Lantern to Old Jack's story. After his five errands and The Rider, beat the Horseman on the hard ride and bring Jack the Horseman's Ember, which waits for you in the chest where he fell. The Hollow Lantern comes after it, and both come back each Hallow's Eve.
- Old Jack's story now reads as one tale: why he's in the Forgotten Temple, what his lanterns are for, and how the Horseman fits in. His quest cards no longer repeat what he just told you.
- Old Jack's and Martha's story quests show as main quests, first in their lists.
- The Hollow Lantern now comes with The Hollow Lantern quest's own pay, collected when you hand it in, with the year still on it. Harvest Feast's plate works the same way.
- Added "Tell me about the Horseman." to Old Jack's menu. He tells you where to find the Horseman and what to do about his head, and once you've beaten him, he has more to say.
- Old Jack's quest rewards now show what they are (Old Jack's Welcome, Old Jack's Pay, Old Jack's Daily Pay and Old Jack's Thanks), where they read Loot Bundle, and hovering one lists what it pays and the chance of each extra.
- Old Jack's board pay now shows as Old Jack's Extras in your rewards, where it read Loot Bundle, and hovering it lists what it pays. The Hallowed's reward past Exalted, the trick-or-treat knock and a cracked Cursed Geode show their own names too, and the rarest finds stay a surprise.
- Added "What are the sweets for?" to Old Jack's menu. Hallow Sweets also say what they're for when you hover them, and your first Sweets of the season come with a notice.

### Old Jack's shop

- Every line that called Old Jack's wares a stall now says shop, and the Festival Shelf open all year is the Festival Shop.
- Added the Grave Candle for 5 Hallow Sweets, and the Burning Grave Candle for 45 once he counts you a Regular. He also trades a Burning Grave Candle for 10 Grave Candles.
- With the MMO Skill Bounty Pack, you can also buy a Grave Candle for 250 Life Essence and a Burning Grave Candle for 1,000.
- Added the Fire Resistance Potion for 15 Hallow Sweets. Drink it and fire does you half the harm for three minutes.
- Old Jack buys a spare Eternal Candle for 50 Hallow Sweets.
- Old Jack's shop now takes a Jack Helm from your bag toward the Burning Jack Helm (480 Hallow Sweets and the helm), and a Jack Shield toward the Flaming Jack Shield (650 and the shield). The ranks they need are the same.

### Almanac

- Added the Headless Horseman to Hallow's Eve's page in the Almanac: a banner showing him and where to find him, what his Hoard pays (each piece shows as a '?' until you own it) with a button to your boss fight records, and how many times you and the whole server have brought him down and cut his head off.
- The Spectral and Shattering Lantern Bombs in the Almanac now say the Headless Horseman pays them.

### Lantern Bombs and finds

- Lantern Bombs, and the Shattering bomb's shards, now hurt other players where your world has PvP on. They never hurt you, and nobody takes damage in the Forgotten Temple.
- A Hallowed Pumpkin, or a pinch of spice in Harvest Feast, found while harvesting now shows the game's own pickup notice in place of its own line.

### With MMO Skill Tree

- The Horseman's daily Claim adds 1,500 Defense XP, or 2,500 on the hard ride, and your first win each day pays 4,000 Defense XP with the normal ride's Hoard and 8,000 with the hard ride's.
- Clearing the graveyard pays 500 Defense XP, or 850 on the hard ride, half on later clears that day.
- The Horseman Falls, Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane pay 1,000, 3,000 and 8,000 Defense XP, and Off With His Head pays 2,000.
- The Rider pays 2,500 Defense XP and Fire for the Lantern 4,000.
- By Lantern Light and Lamplighter pay 3,000 and 5,000 Artillery XP.
- Season to Season, Round the Year and Seasons of Orbis now also give an XP boost token for every skill: 1.25x, 1.5x and 2x for an hour.

### For server owners

- The Horseman's Graveyard and its hard ride are CommonLib dungeons, `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard` and `Hallows_Eve_Graveyard_Hard`, so `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json` can switch either off or change how many players share it. `/zigdungeon enter Hallows_Eve_Graveyard` takes you in alone while Hallow's Eve runs, without spending a candle.
