Seasons of Orbis 1.2.0 is out now! The Headless Horseman rides through Hallow's Eve in a graveyard of his own, and Old Jack hands out all his work from one list.

Built for Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.4.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.1 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.2]] beside it the Horseman pays Defense XP.

## THE HEADLESS HORSEMAN RIDES

The **Headless Horseman** has come for Hallow's Eve. Find the **Gravekeeper** beside Old Jack in the Forgotten Temple, press F, and step into the Horseman's Graveyard, alone or with up to four friends. The more of you there are, the tougher he gets.

He starts in the saddle, circling low and raining Lantern Bombs on you, and every so often a volley lands on the glowing rings under your feet. Knock him down to two thirds and he's thrown from his horse to fight on foot with two Jack Skeletons. Then his head flies off: he can't be hurt until you cut it down, and if you leave it alone it flies back and heals him. At one third he burns with rage, hits harder, calls two more Jack Skeletons and his volleys leave the ground on fire.

Not sure what you're in for? Ask Old Jack about the rider he keeps hearing about.

## The Horseman's Hoard

Your first win each day pays his daily Claim, with Hallow Sweets, Ectoplasm, Festival Tokens and a shot at Lantern Bombs, and the **Horseman's Hoard**, which always holds a Spectral Lantern Bomb and sometimes a piece of his armour: the Horseman's Breastplate, Gauntlets or Greaves. Each piece makes your Fire damage 5% stronger, Lantern Bombs included.

Bring him down 1, 10 and 50 times for The Horseman Falls, Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane, and the title that comes with the last.

## Old Jack's one list

Ask Old Jack "What needs doing?" and **one list** shows his quests, today's board jobs and his errands, each tagged Bounty or Errand with its grade. Take them from Jack, hand them in to Jack, collect your pay from Jack.

We'll see you in the graveyard!

## The full changelog

### The Headless Horseman

- Added the Headless Horseman, who rides in his own graveyard during Hallow's Eve. Press F on the Gravekeeper beside Old Jack in the Forgotten Temple to go in. Up to five players share a graveyard, and his health grows with each player in the fight.
- In the saddle he lobs Lantern Bombs, and every 12 seconds a volley falls on rings marked under each player. At two thirds of his health he's thrown and fights on foot with two Jack Skeletons.
- On foot, every 40 seconds his head flies off and he can't be hurt until it's killed. A head left alone for 25 seconds flies back to him and heals him.
- At one third of his health he turns to Wrath: he moves faster and hits harder, two more Jack Skeletons join him, and a burning volley falls every 8 seconds, leaving fire on the ground.
- If everyone in the fight falls or leaves, he's back at full health for the next try.
- Added his daily Claim. Your first win each day pays 25 Hallow Sweets, 3 Ectoplasm and 2 Festival Tokens, with a 30% chance at two Spectral Lantern Bombs and a 10% chance at a Shattering Lantern Bomb. How much you get depends on your part in the fight.
- Added the Horseman's Hoard, paid on your first win each day beside his daily Claim: always a Spectral Lantern Bomb, and a 5% chance each at the Horseman's Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves.
- Added the Horseman's Breastplate, Gauntlets and Greaves, ancient steel that lasts twice as long as the game's. Each raises the Fire damage you deal by 5%.
- Clearing the graveyard pays its own Claim of 10 Hallow Sweets and a Festival Token, in full the first time each day and half after that.
- Added The Horseman Falls, Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane, for 1, 10 and 50 wins in one Hallow's Eve. They pay 250, 500 and 1,000 favor with the Hallowed, and Horseman's Bane also gives the title "Horseman's Bane".
- The Hallowed counts your first win over the Headless Horseman. Horseman Hunter and Horseman's Bane are a ladder of their own.
- Added "Who's the rider I keep hearing about?" to Old Jack's menu. He tells you where to find the Horseman and what to do about his head.
- The graveyard opens only while Hallow's Eve runs. Outside it, the Gravekeeper is gone.
- With MMO Skill Tree, his daily Claim adds 1,500 Defense XP, and his three achievements pay 1,000, 3,000 and 8,000 Defense XP.

### Old Jack and the boards

- Old Jack's "What needs doing?" now opens one list with his quests, today's board jobs and his errands, each tagged Bounty or Errand with its grade. You take, hand in and collect them all at Jack, and a job you took at his board can be handed in to him too. His menu no longer has separate board and errands lines.
- Martha does the same with the Feast Board when Harvest Feast comes around.
- Old Jack's board pay now shows as Old Jack's Extras in your rewards, where it read Loot Bundle.

### Gear and favor

- Hallow's Eve gear now raises the favor you earn by a percent, where it added favor of its own while you wore it. The Jack Helm raises Old Jack's Favor by 10% and the Burning Jack Helm by 20%. The Jack Shield raises the Hallowed's by 10% and the Flaming Jack Shield by 20%, and the full Hallowed set adds another 10%.
- Hover a piece to see its number, such as "Old Jack's Favor Gain: 10%". Your Reputation page tells you how much your gear raises each reputation, and taking the gear off never lowers favor you earned.

### Lantern Bombs

- Lantern Bombs, and the Shattering bomb's shards, now hurt other players where your world has PvP on. They never hurt you, and nobody takes damage in the Forgotten Temple.

### For server owners

- The Horseman's Graveyard is a CommonLib dungeon, so `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json` can switch it off or change how many players share it. `/zigdungeon enter Hallows_Eve_Graveyard` takes you in alone while Hallow's Eve runs.
