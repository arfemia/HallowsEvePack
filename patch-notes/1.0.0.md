First release. Seasons of Orbis brings yearly events to your server, and its first is Hallow's Eve, an autumn event that runs from October 1 to November 3. Old Jack keeps the Forgotten Temple with work for you and a stall that takes Hallow Sweets, and after dark the dead walk. Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.2.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.0 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.0]] beside it the season trains your skills as well.

### The season and its nights

- **Hallow's Eve comes round every year.** It runs from October 1 to November 3, with Harvest Moon on the last three days of October. A banner marks each start and each end, and a server owner can switch either event off in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/calendar.json`.
- **Things that walk at night.** After dark, away from torches, Jack Skeletons, Hollow Ghouls and Lost Souls come out, and Geode Wraiths walk the mineshafts of the first three zones. They drop Lantern Bombs, Ectoplasm, Hallowed Pumpkins and Cursed Geodes, and they are gone by dawn. Each one is as tough as the game's own creature it is built on, so a Hollow Ghoul bites like a Ghoul, even in the first zone. None of them are Memories.
- **Cursed Geodes and the pumpkin patch.** Mining stone and ore turns up a Cursed Geode now and then. Crack it open for crystals plus a bomb, some Ectoplasm or fire essence. A pumpkin you harvest by hand sometimes comes up Hallowed.
- **Harvest Moon.** About twice as many creatures walk at night, and every Cursed Geode and Hallowed Pumpkin you find comes in pairs.
- **Off means gone.** When the event ends or a server owner switches it off, Old Jack leaves and takes his stall, his board, his quests and the trick-or-treat line with him. No new creatures come, and the ones still out are gone by dawn. Nothing you earned goes with him.

### Old Jack's season

- **A questline at the Forgotten Temple.** Old Jack, a pumpkin-headed host, takes his spot in the temple when the event starts and leaves when it ends. He has six quests, from Hallowed Pumpkins to lighting the Hollow Lantern, and the lantern quest comes back every year.
- **Dailies and a nightly board.** Three dailies from Old Jack, and his board posts one contract a night that pays the moment it is done. On Harvest Moon nights the board leans toward a skeleton hunt.
- **Hallow Sweets and Old Jack's stall.** The event pays in Hallow Sweets, spent at Old Jack's stall on Lantern Bombs, a Jack Lantern, an Ecto Lantern (sweets and Ectoplasm), the Jack Helm and the Costume Wand, with a bundle of bombs on Harvest Moon nights. Unspent sweets keep until next year. You find the stall and the board only in the Forgotten Temple.
- **Left for next year.** A quest or a board job you have not finished when the event ends waits for Old Jack to come back.
- **Trick or treat.** Once a day, ask any character who has a conversation for you, Old Jack included: a few sweets every time, then more sweets, candy canes or a pumpkin pie, or now and then a costume. Already in a costume, a trick turns into a treat. The game's own merchants have no conversation, so they hand out nothing.
- **Achievements and a title.** Lighting the Hollow Lantern earns a keepsake with the year in its name, and five more achievements cover the season's work. Earn all six in one season and The Hallowed gives you the title "the Hallowed". Each year's achievements stay with you as feats.
- **An Almanac page.** Hallow's Eve has its own Almanac page with your keepsakes and lifetime tallies of bombs thrown, ghouls felled, souls freed and geodes cracked.

### Items

- **Lantern Bombs.** Four carved-pumpkin bombs, each a step up from the last: the Lantern Bomb, the Burning Lantern Bomb (it sets what it hits alight), the Spectral Lantern Bomb (it also slows them) and the Shattering Lantern Bomb (it also throws flaming shards). A blast never hurts a player. Old Jack sells them and the season's creatures drop them.
- **The Hallowed set.** The Jack Helm and the Jack Shield. Wear the helm with the shield in your off hand for more health and less damage from fire. You glow like a lantern while you do.
- **The Costume Wand.** Turn another player into a Jack Skeleton or a Lost Soul for a few minutes. They can take the costume off any time with `/zigcostume off`.
- **Lanterns.** The Jack Lantern and the green Ecto Lantern, to place and light. The Hollow Lantern is the keepsake Old Jack gives once each year.
- **Ectoplasm and the Hallowed Pumpkin.** What the Lost Souls leave behind, and the season's own pumpkin.
- **The Carving Bench.** Craft it at a Workbench from pumpkins and wood. It carves lanterns, the Jack Helm and Lantern Bombs all year, and the Jack Shield during Hallow's Eve.
- **The items keep working off season.** Bombs throw, the wand dresses, the set applies, and a Cursed Geode kept past the event still cracks.

### With other mods

- **MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.0]] or newer.** The season trains skills too. Lantern Bombs train Artillery, Old Jack's rewards and the cracked geodes pay skill XP, and his board posts skill dailies on some nights. The Hallowed set adds luck and Artillery damage, the Carving Bench trains Crafting, and Harvest Moon lifts Harvesting and Artillery XP. Its own notes have the detail.
- **The MMO Skill Bounty Pack 1.3.0 or newer**, which runs on MMO Skill Tree. Its Daily bounty board posts a haunt contract each day of the event.

Everything in the pack ships in nine languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
