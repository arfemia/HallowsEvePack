First release. Seasons of Orbis brings yearly events to your server, and its first is Hallow's Eve, an autumn event that runs from October 1 to November 3. Old Jack sets up in the Forgotten Temple with work for you and a stall that takes Hallow Sweets, and after dark the dead walk. Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.2.0 or newer and RPG Stations 1.1.0 or newer. It never needs MMO Skill Tree, though with [[1.7.0]] beside it the season trains your skills as well.

### The season and its nights

- **Hallow's Eve comes round every year.** It runs from October 1 to November 3, with Harvest Moon on the last three days of October. A banner marks each start and each end, and a server owner can switch either event off in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/calendar.json`.
- **Things that walk at night.** After dark, away from torches, Jack Skeletons, Hollow Ghouls and Lost Souls come out, and Geode Wraiths walk the mineshafts of the first three zones. They drop Lantern Bombs, Ectoplasm, Hallowed Pumpkins and Cursed Geodes, and they are gone by dawn. Each one is as tough as the game's own creature it is built on, so a Hollow Ghoul bites like a Ghoul, even in the first zone. None of them are Memories.
- **Cursed Geodes and the pumpkin patch.** Mining stone and ore turns up a Cursed Geode now and then. Crack it open for crystals plus a bomb, some Ectoplasm or Essence of Fire. A pumpkin you harvest by hand sometimes comes up Hallowed.
- **Harvest Moon.** About twice as many creatures walk at night, and every Cursed Geode and Hallowed Pumpkin you find comes in pairs.
- **Off means gone.** When the event ends or a server owner switches it off, Old Jack leaves and takes his stall, his board, his quests and the trick-or-treat line with him. No new creatures come, and the ones still out are gone by dawn. Nothing you earned goes with him.

### Old Jack's season

- **Lanterns in the Temple.** While the event runs, every player finds this quest in their quest log, tracked, sending them through any Forgotten Temple Gateway to find Old Jack inside. Talk to him and he opens his quest list on it: hand it in, collect its few sweets and an Objective Book right there, and he offers his first quest. The book opens your quests, achievements and the Almanac, so a server without MMO Skill Tree has a way to hand one out. It pays once, and never to a player who has already brought him Hallowed Pumpkins. Abandon it and you can take it straight back from the Quests tab.
- **A questline at the Forgotten Temple.** Old Jack, a pumpkin-headed host, takes his spot in the temple when the event starts and leaves when it ends. He has six quests, from Hallowed Pumpkins to lighting the Hollow Lantern, and the lantern quest comes back every year.
- **Dailies, and a board with work for the dark and the daylight.** Three dailies from Old Jack. His board posts a Night job and a Day job every day: the Night work hunts creatures that only come out after dark, and the Day work wants Lantern Bombs thrown or Cursed Geodes cracked. A second Night job opens once you are a Regular with Old Jack, and a second Day job once you are Trusted; until then you see them on the board, locked. A finished job's pay waits at the board until you collect it, so collect it before Old Jack packs up for the year. On Harvest Moon nights the Night work leans toward a skeleton hunt.
- **Hallow Sweets and Old Jack's stall.** The event pays in Hallow Sweets, spent at Old Jack's stall on Lantern Bombs, a Jack Lantern, an Ecto Lantern (sweets and Ectoplasm), the Jack Helm and the Jack Shield, the Costume Wand and the game's own Halloween Broomstick, which no recipe or drop hands out, with a bundle of Shattering and Spectral Lantern Bombs on Harvest Moon nights. Unspent sweets keep until next year. You find the stall and the board only in the Forgotten Temple.
- **Decorations at the stall.** A Decorations shelf sells six of the game's own Halloween pieces for sweets: Halloween lights, a scary and a cute carved pumpkin, a straw basket, a scarecrow and a wagon.
- **Left for next year.** A quest or a board job you have not finished when the event ends waits for Old Jack to come back, and so does a finished job you never collected.
- **Trick or treat.** Once a day, ask any character who has a conversation for you, Old Jack included: a few sweets every time, then more sweets, candy canes or a pumpkin pie, or now and then a costume. Already in a costume, a trick turns into a treat. The game's own merchants have no conversation, so they hand out nothing.
- **Achievements and a title.** Lighting the Hollow Lantern earns a keepsake with the year in its name, waiting on the achievements page for you to collect, and five more achievements cover the season's work. Earn all six in one season and the achievement The Hallowed gives you the title "the Hallowed". Each year's achievements stay with you as feats.
- **Keepsakes across the years.** Three achievements stay with you from one season to the next. Old Friend is for being here for three Hallow's Eves. Lantern Keeper is for lighting the Hollow Lantern in three seasons, and gives the title "Keeper of Lanterns". First Lanterns is for lighting it in 2026, the first season, so nobody can earn it after this year. None of them counts toward the achievement The Hallowed.
- **An Almanac page.** Hallow's Eve has its own Almanac page. It opens on a banner built from the season's own items, with a warm glow behind the lantern and an orange strip, and keeps your keepsakes and lifetime tallies of bombs thrown, ghouls felled, Jack Skeletons knocked down, souls freed, geodes cracked and Geode Wraiths put down. A server owner can rearrange the banner, or swap in a picture of their own, in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/almanac.json`.

### Favor with Old Jack and the Hallowed

- **Two reputations.** Old Jack's Favor runs from Stranger through Regular, Trusted and Confidant to Jack's Own, and stops there. The Hallowed, the season's second reputation, uses the plain ranks up to Exalted and keeps going past it: each time your favor climbs another step beyond Exalted, the Hallowed leave you a cache. Favor stays with you from one Hallow's Eve to the next, and through death.
- **Earning it.** Every quest Old Jack gives and every job on his board pays favor with both, and the quest's rewards say how much. Putting down the season's night creatures raises your favor with the Hallowed, and so does each trick-or-treat knock. A bar on screen moves with each gain, and a notice marks each new rank. The menu's Reputation tab shows where you stand.
- **Favor from gear.** The Jack Helm adds favor with Old Jack while you wear it. The Jack Shield and the full Hallowed set add favor with the Hallowed reputation, and the shield counts in either hand, so two shields stack. The Burning Jack Helm and the Flaming Jack Shield add twice what the plain pair does. What your gear adds counts toward every rank and lock, and the Reputation tab shows it apart.
- **A word with Old Jack.** Once a day he points you at his board and pays a little favor for stopping by when you answer him or say goodbye. Once he knows you, "How do we stand?" opens the Reputation page.
- **What his favor opens.** As a Regular you can take the board's second Night job. Trusted opens its second Day job. Reach Confidant for the achievement Jack's Confidant and its title; like the keepsakes, it is yours to keep, and the Jack Helm's favor counts toward it. At Jack's Own he sells you the Burning Jack Helm.
- **What the Hallowed opens.** Revered opens the Burning Jack Helm at Old Jack's stall, and Exalted opens the Flaming Jack Shield. Both are charred black and sturdier than the plain pair: the helm turns more of a hit and some fire, and the shield holds a guard for less stamina. Until you reach the rank, you see them on the shelf, locked.

### Items

- **Lantern Bombs.** Four carved-pumpkin bombs, each a step up from the last: the Lantern Bomb, the Burning Lantern Bomb (it sets what it hits alight), the Spectral Lantern Bomb (it also slows them) and the Shattering Lantern Bomb (it also throws flaming shards). A blast never hurts a player. Old Jack sells them and the season's creatures drop them.
- **The Hallowed set.** The Jack Helm and the Jack Shield. Wear the helm with the shield in your off hand for more health and less damage from fire. You glow like a lantern while you do. Lighting the Hollow Lantern pays a Jack Shield every year, so everyone who lights it can finish the set. The Burning Jack Helm and the Flaming Jack Shield count toward the set too, with either plain piece.
- **The Costume Wand.** Turn another player into a Jack Skeleton or a Lost Soul for a few minutes. They can take the costume off any time with `/zigcostume off`.
- **Lanterns.** The Jack Lantern and the green Ecto Lantern, to place and light. The Hollow Lantern is the keepsake Old Jack gives once each year.
- **Ectoplasm and the Hallowed Pumpkin.** What the Lost Souls leave behind, and the season's own pumpkin.
- **The Carving Bench.** Craft it at a Workbench from pumpkins and wood. It carves lanterns, the Jack Helm and Lantern Bombs all year, and the Jack Shield during Hallow's Eve.
- **The items keep working off season.** Bombs throw, the wand dresses, the set applies, and a Cursed Geode kept past the event still cracks.

### With other mods

- **MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.0]] or newer.** The season trains skills too. Lantern Bombs train Artillery. Old Jack's quests, his board jobs and the season's achievements pay XP in the skill the work trains: Defense for the fights, Harvesting for the pumpkins, Mining for the geodes and Artillery for the bombs. An achievement's XP waits on the achievements page until you collect it. The achievements The Hallowed and Jack's Guest each add an XP boost token, which arrives the moment you earn it, cracked geodes pay Mining XP, and his board posts a Skill job every day, which pays favor like his own jobs. The Hallowed set adds luck and Artillery damage, the Carving Bench trains Crafting, and Harvest Moon lifts Harvesting and Artillery XP. On a server without MMO Skill Tree those rewards don't show and nothing else changes. Its own notes have the detail.
- **The MMO Skill Bounty Pack 1.3.0 or newer**, which runs on MMO Skill Tree. Its Daily bounty board posts a haunt contract each day of the event.

Everything in the pack ships in nine languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
